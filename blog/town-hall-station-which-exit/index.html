Town Hall, the easy way to the QVB and Darling Harbour

Pop out the right Town Hall exit and the QVB, Darling Harbour or your ride home is right there. Here is which exit to take.

Karan Sharma 24 November 2026 5 min read

Hello, and welcome to Town Hall! The QVB, Darling Harbour and a great coffee are all a happy stroll away, and the right exit puts you right on the doorstep. Let me show you which one is yours.

Here's the quick version.

  • Fancy the light rail? Come up at exit 2 and it's right there.
  • Heading to Sydney Town Hall or Darling Harbour? Exit 5 brings you out by Town Hall Square.
  • Hopping on a bus? Exit 1 is York St and exit 5 is Druitt St.
  • Out late and having a ball? Come up at exit 2, because all the night buses wait on Park St.

Which exit at Town Hall?

Every exit has a number and a name on the signs, and the names make them easy to remember.

  • Exit 1, QVB is your way to the QVB and The Galeries. York St buses leave from stands A to F.
  • Exit 2, George St is your way to the light rail stop, and to the Park St buses at stands G to K. The lift to the street is here too.
  • Exit 3 is Pavilion Plaza, a lovely spot to meet a friend.
  • Exit 4, Cathedral is your way to the light rail stop, St Andrew's Cathedral and Darling Harbour.
  • Exit 5, Town Hall is your way to Sydney Town Hall, Town Hall Square and Darling Harbour. Druitt St buses leave from stands L and N.

Two exits reach the light rail. Want a bus as well? Pick exit 2 and you'll find both without a single extra step. Exit 4 works too, and you get a bonus lap of the block.

Which platform for your train?

Here's where your train waits, so you can stroll straight to it.

  • Platform 1 is the T2, and some weekday T3 services to Liverpool.
  • Platform 2 is the T1 west and the T9, heading south through Central.
  • Platform 3 is the T1 and the T9 heading north towards Gordon. A few intercity services to the Central Coast leave from here too.
  • Platform 4 is the T4 towards Cronulla and the Illawarra. A few intercity services to the South Coast leave from here too.
  • Platform 5 is the T4 through the eastern suburbs to Bondi Junction.
  • Platform 6 is the T3 to Liverpool and Lidcombe, and the T8 to the airport.

Late at night the T2 to Central sometimes slips over to platform 6 after midnight, so give the board a quick peek before you sit down.

Two trains, both saying Hornsby

Here's a fun one! Two trains leave Town Hall just minutes apart, and both say Hornsby on the front. On a Wednesday night, the 11.05pm from platform 2 is a T9 to Hornsby via Central. Eight minutes later, the 11.13pm from platform 3 is a T1 to Hornsby via Gordon.

They leave in opposite directions. One heads south through Central and reaches Hornsby the long way round. The other goes north through North Sydney. Both are right, and only one is yours.

So have a look at the word after "via" and you'll get home happy.

Where does your bus go from?

There are three streets, and each one has its own exit.

  • York St is at exit 1, stands A to F. Come here for the BN1 to Mona Vale, the 100 to Taronga Zoo and the 120 to Chatswood.
  • Park St is at exit 2, stands G to K. Come here for the 324 and 325 to Watsons Bay, the 389 to Bondi Junction, and every night bus.
  • Druitt St is at exit 5, stands L and N. Come here for the 505 to Woolwich, the 506 to Macquarie University and the 507 to Meadowbank.

If all you have is the route number, you can find your stop by that number.

Where do the night buses go from?

Out late and having the best time? Every night bus at Town Hall leaves from Park St, so it's an easy wander home.

Most trains stop from around midnight, and the night buses take over. The last of them leave between 4.00am and 4.30am. Come up at exit 2 on George St and you're right by the stands.

Stand H has twelve routes.

  • N10 Sutherland
  • N11 Cronulla
  • N20 Riverwood
  • N30 Macarthur
  • N40 East Hills
  • N50 Liverpool
  • N60 Fairfield
  • N61 Carlingford
  • N70 Penrith
  • N71 Richmond
  • N80 Hornsby via Strathfield
  • N81 Parramatta

Stand K has three routes.

  • N90 Hornsby via Chatswood
  • N91 Macquarie Centre
  • N92 Tallawong

On a Wednesday night, the first bus out of stand H is the N50 to Liverpool at 12.04am. After that each route runs roughly once an hour. So finish your drink, check the time, then stroll over to Park St and hop on whichever bus is yours.

Stand K starts a little later. The first N91 is at 1.10am, and the first N90 and N92 are at 1.40am.

The N61 and the N81 run overnight on Thursday, Friday and Saturday. The N92 finishes at Tallawong, where the Park & Ride is.

Is there a metro at Town Hall?

Fancy the metro? Gadigal is the nearest metro station, and it has two entrances, one on Park St and one on Bathurst St.

Come up at exit 2 and walk along Park St towards Pitt St. Metro services leave Gadigal every 4 to 10 minutes through the day, so you can just turn up and go.

Handy things to know

  • Toilets are on the concourse, towards the Bathurst St end.
  • The lift to the street is at exit 2, on George St.
  • Prams and big bags are happiest in the lifts, which make your day much smoother than the escalators.
  • Light rail is right outside, the L2 and the L3, from exit 2 or exit 4.
  • Tickets come from the single trip ticket machines at the station.

So pick your exit before you head down and you'll come up exactly where you want to be. Pop up at Park St for a late-night ride home, York St for the zoo, and Druitt St for Macquarie University. Have a great day out!

And have a look at the live times before you head down.
