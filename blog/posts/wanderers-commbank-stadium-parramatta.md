Hello, football fans, and welcome to Parramatta! You're off to see the Wanderers take on Perth Glory at CommBank Stadium on Saturday 5 December, and it's going to be a brilliant night.

Gates open at 6.45pm and kick-off is at 7.40pm, so come early, grab a good spot and soak up the buzz.

Heads up, the trains are doing something different that Saturday, so here's the easy way in. From the city, ride to Granville. A replacement bus takes you into Parramatta, and the stadium is a short walk or ride from there.

::: facts Your night, in short
When: **Saturday 5 December**, gates open **6.45pm**
Kick-off: **7.40pm**, full time **9.35pm**
Where: CommBank Stadium, Parramatta
Getting in: a train to **Granville**, then a bus to Parramatta
Light rail in: two stops on the **L4** to Prince Alfred Square
On foot: about **15 minutes** from Parramatta station
Light rail home: the L4 runs until **1am**, every day
:::

::: callout Give yourself a little extra time
A bus fills in for the train on the last stretch into Parramatta that day, so with a little extra time you can stroll in and enjoy the buzz when you arrive.
:::

## How do you get to CommBank Stadium?

Aim for Parramatta, and everything else is close by from there.

Coming from the city? Catch a train heading west and get off at Granville. Change there for a replacement bus into Parramatta, and you're nearly at the football!

Coming from Penrith or Richmond? A bus takes the place of the T1 and T5 trains as far as Granville that day, so give yourself a little extra time and settle in for the ride.

Coming from the south west? The T2 keeps running between Leppington, Granville and the city, so change at Granville like everyone else.

## Which way from Parramatta station?

You've got two ways to go, and both are short.

The first is on foot. Walk up Church Street, turn into Victoria Road and follow it all the way to the ground. It takes about 15 minutes, which is just enough time to warm up your voice.

The second is the light rail. Walk to the Parramatta Square stop and catch an L4 towards Westmead. It's only two stops, Church Street and then Prince Alfred Square, so ride past the first and get off at the second. Then stroll in along Victoria Road.

## Is there a bus that goes to the gate?

Yes, and it's a great one! Buses leave Parramatta Interchange from Stand A4 and stop on O'Connell Street, right outside the stadium. Those are the 546, 600, 601, 603, 604, 606 and 706. Every one of them is accessible.

Not sure which stop is yours? You can [find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Coming in by ferry?

Ooh, the ferry! That's the scenic way in. Ride the F3 up the river to Parramatta Wharf, then wander along the riverside and through Argus Lane to the Robin Thomas light rail stop. Catch an L4 towards Westmead and get off at Prince Alfred Square.

The F3 is popular and can reach capacity, so give yourself a little extra time. At low tide, buses can stand in for the ferry between Parramatta and Rydalmere.

## What about getting home?

Full time is 9.35pm, and getting back into Parramatta is the easy part. The L4 runs from 5am until 1am, seven days a week, so celebrate (or commiserate!) at your own pace.

Going to the city? Take a replacement bus to Granville, then change there for a train into town.

Heading home up past Hornsby? Buses fill in for trains that night, between Hornsby and Gosford from 8.20pm and between Hornsby and Berowra from 8.40pm, so settle in and replay the highlights.

Trackwork plans can move, so peek at the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you set off and again before you leave the ground. You'll see exactly what's rolling in next.

## What else will make the night easy?

- Prince Alfred Square has wheelchair access, so it's an easy way in if you use a wheelchair.
- Paying is easy. Just tap on and tap off with a contactless card or a linked device.
- Planning to drive? You can, and the car parks around the ground are expected to be popular. The roads get busy after the match as every car park empties at once, so bring some patience and a good playlist.
- Grabbing a rideshare home? Meet your driver a little way from the stadium and you'll glide past the crawl.

Come for the 6.45pm gates, take the stroll in down Church Street and enjoy every minute. Have a brilliant night!
