Eighty thousand people a night, two nights in a row. Another twenty thousand are in the precinct for
other shows across the same weekend. That is nearly a hundred thousand of us walking down Olympic
Boulevarde.

Now the bit that catches people out. Gates open at 5pm. The show starts at 6pm.

So this is not a night you drift towards after dinner. You want to be off the train by about half
past five, and that part is on us.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Saturday 12 and Sunday 13 December**, Accor Stadium
Clock: gates at **5pm**, show at **6pm**
Station: **Olympic Park**, then a 10 minute walk
Trains in: express from Central every **7 to 8 minutes** from 4pm
Trains home: to Central every **5 minutes** after the show
Buses home: last one out of the precinct by **midnight**
:::

::: callout Do this first
Keep your concert ticket once you are through the gate. It is your fare home as well as your fare
there. Show it to staff when you board.
:::

## What time do you really need to leave?

Work back from six o'clock and the whole evening changes shape.

It is a 10 minute walk from Olympic Park station to the stadium, down Olympic Boulevarde. The
express trains from Central start running at 4pm. Be on one of those.

The event buses go earlier still. The first one leaves Narrabeen Lakes at 2.35pm.

## Which train gets you to Accor Stadium?

From Central, take an express to Olympic Park. They run every 7 to 8 minutes from 4pm and stop at
Redfern and Strathfield on the way.

From Penrith or Blacktown, extra services run every 15 minutes from 4pm. They call at Seven Hills,
Westmead and Parramatta. Then Granville, Lidcombe and Olympic Park.

From Campbelltown, three extra trains leave at 5.39pm, 5.54pm and 6.09pm. Even the first of those
leaves after the gates have opened, so catch a regular service earlier if you want the start.

From the Central Coast or the Hunter, one extra train leaves Newcastle Interchange at 4.18pm. Ride
it to Strathfield and change there for the express.

From anywhere else, aim for Lidcombe. Regular services run there, and trains from Lidcombe go on to
Olympic Park.

On the metro, the M1 runs between Tallawong and Bankstown. Change at Central for the express. A gate
is kept open at metro stations for people holding a concert ticket.

## What if you are nowhere near a train?

Nine event bus routes run for these two shows. They come in from the northern beaches and the inner
west. Also the Sutherland Shire, the Hills and the southern suburbs.

Buses go every 5 to 20 minutes between 2.35pm and 7.05pm, depending on the route. They arrive at the
precinct from 4pm until 8pm. Your concert ticket covers them.

Each route picks up along the way, so the first time below is from the far end.

- **Route 1A** from Narrabeen Lakes: first bus 2.35pm, last bus 6.35pm.
- **Route 1B** from Warriewood: first bus 2.42pm, last bus 6.42pm.
- **Route 2** from Glebe Point: first bus 3.03pm, last bus 7.03pm.
- **Route 4** from Maroubra Beach: first bus 2.40pm, last bus 6.40pm.
- **Route 5A** from Hills Showground station: first bus 3.14pm, last bus 7.14pm.
- **Route 5B** from Tallawong station: first bus 2.58pm, last bus 6.58pm.
- **Route 6** from Sutherland station: first bus 2.54pm, last bus 6.54pm.
- **Route 7** from Shark Park at Cronulla: first bus 2.35pm, last bus 6.35pm.
- **Route 8** from Dural: first bus 3.05pm, last bus 7.05pm.

Read the last times twice. On every one of the nine routes, the last bus leaves after the show has
already started.

On the metro, route 1A also picks up at Chatswood and North Ryde, and route 1B at Macquarie Park.
Route 5A comes from Hills Showground and Castle Hill. Route 5B starts at Tallawong and calls at
Rouse Hill. Then Kellyville, Bella Vista and Norwest.

## Where does your bus home leave from?

There are two bus terminals at Olympic Park, and they are not next to each other. That is the kind
of thing you want to know before you walk out of the stadium.

- **Routes 1A, 1B, 2 and 4** use the Aquatic Terminal on Olympic Boulevarde near Herb Elliott Avenue.
- **Routes 5A, 5B, 6, 7 and 8** use the Plaza Terminal near Kevin Coombs Avenue.

Buses leave about every five minutes once the show finishes. The last one on each route is out of the
precinct by midnight.

## How do you get home by train?

Express trains leave Olympic Park for Central every 5 minutes after the show. They stop at
Strathfield and Redfern.

Heading west, trains go every 7 to 8 minutes. They call at Lidcombe, Granville and Parramatta. Then
Westmead and Seven Hills, and all stations to Blacktown, Schofields or Penrith.

For Campbelltown, extra trains leave Lidcombe at 10.22pm and 10.53pm. For the Hunter, an extra train
leaves Central at 11.18pm. For the South Coast, an extra service leaves Central at 11.43pm.

Your concert ticket is valid on public transport until 4am the next morning.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk back to the
platform. A published frequency is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## Is there trackwork that weekend?

Yes, on both days. It can still change, so check your own line before you leave the house.

- **The T4**: buses replace trains between Cronulla and Sutherland.
- **The T8**: on Sunday daytime, buses replace the all stations trains between Wolli Creek and
  Revesby. The express trains keep running.
- **Towards the South Coast**: buses replace trains between Bomaderry and Wollongong.
- **The Southern Highlands**: on Saturday daytime, buses replace trains between Goulburn, Moss Vale
  and Campbelltown.

## If you were going to drive

Do not drive into the precinct without pre-booked parking. Spaces are expected to sell out in
advance, and there is nothing to sort out on the night.

Parking is a flat $40. Even with a space, allow up to an hour to get out of the car park afterwards.

Driving to a station is the version that works. Park & Ride gives you up to 18 hours of free parking
a day when you complete a public transport trip. Press the intercom at the exit gate and show your
concert ticket.

The car parks at the metro stations are big. Tallawong has 1,000 spaces and Bella Vista has 800.
Kellyville has 1,389, with 150 more at the Decora Drive overflow. Hills Showground has 600. You can
[check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).

## The practical stuff

- **Ferry**: Sydney Olympic Park Wharf is 3.6km from the stadium, about a 40 minute walk. The 526
  bus connects.
- **F3 ferries** are popular and can reach capacity.
- **Light rail**: the L1, L2 and L3 all run to Central, where you change for the express.
- **Regular buses**: the 526 runs through the precinct between Strathfield and Rhodes, and the 533
  goes to Chatswood.
- **Taxi ranks** on Herb Elliott Avenue at the Pullman Hotel, and on Edwin Flack Avenue opposite the
  stadium.
- **Rideshare** is geo-fenced around the precinct, and prices surge after the show.
- **Accessible pick up and drop off** in Dawn Fraser Avenue, off Edwin Flack Avenue.
- **Roads** in the precinct close before, during and after the show.
- **Airport stations**: the station access fee is not covered by your concert ticket.

Get there well before the gates open and enjoy the precinct while it fills up. With a 6pm start,
that is the easy part.
