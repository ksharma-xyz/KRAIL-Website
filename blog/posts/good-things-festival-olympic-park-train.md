Nearly ten hours of bands at the Showground, from midday right through until 9.45pm. One Saturday,
one site, and no good reason at all to leave before the last song.

Thousands of people, and another show on at the arena next door. Getting out of Olympic Park is the
only hard part of the day. That part is on us.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Saturday 5 December**, midday to 9.45pm
Where: Sydney Showground, a short walk from Olympic Park station
Getting there: a train to Lidcombe, then **Platform Zero**
Your fare: **included in the festival ticket** on trains, the metro and light rail
Trains home: express to Central every **15 minutes** from 10pm
:::

::: callout Do this first
Keep your festival ticket once you are inside. It is your fare home as well as your fare there.
Show it to staff when you board.
:::

## How do you get there?

Take a train to Lidcombe. Any one of them will do.

At Lidcombe, walk to Platform Zero. The direct train to Olympic Park goes from there.

It runs every 10 minutes until late, and extra services run for the festival. From Central the
whole trip is about 30 minutes. From Parramatta it is about 18.

Olympic Park station is a short walk from the Showground.

## Coming from the west? Read this part twice.

There is weekend trackwork on Saturday 5 December, and it lands squarely on you.

On the T1, buses replace trains between Penrith, Richmond and Granville. On the T5, buses replace
trains between Richmond and Granville.

So catch the replacement bus to Granville first. Then take a train to Lidcombe. At Lidcombe you
change for Olympic Park like everybody else.

Buses also run between some western stations and Tallawong. If the metro is the easier half of your
trip, that is the way in.

## What else is shut that day?

More than you would expect, and all of it on the same Saturday.

The L2 and the L3 do not run at all on 5 and 6 December. Buses replace them between Randwick,
Kingsford and Central.

The metro stops between Tallawong and Bankstown from 4am to 9am. After that it is running normally.

On the T1, buses replace trains between Berowra and Hornsby from 8.40pm that night.

Check your own line before you leave the house.

## How do you get home after the last song?

The festival finishes at 9.45pm. The first extra express leaves Olympic Park at 10pm.

They go every 15 minutes until midnight, straight to Central. They stop at Strathfield and Redfern
on the way.

Your festival ticket still works, right through until 4am on the Sunday.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk back to the
platform. A published frequency is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## If you were going to drive

Book a space before you go, or leave the car at home. You cannot buy parking on the day of an event.

Booking closes at 11.59pm on the Friday night. Even with a space, allow up to an hour to get out of
the car park afterwards.

Driving to a station is the version that works. Park & Ride gives you up to 18 hours of free
parking a day when you complete a public transport trip.

Your festival ticket covers that parking. Press the intercom at the exit gate and show it. You can
[check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).

## The practical stuff

- **Ferry**: the F3 runs to Sydney Olympic Park Wharf, about 33 minutes from Circular Quay.
- **From the wharf**: catch the connecting 526 bus. Walking it is about 40 minutes, or 3.6km.
- **Capacity**: the F3 is very popular and can fill up, so allow extra time.
- **Low tide**: between Parramatta and Rydalmere the F3 is sometimes a bus instead.
- **Buses in**: the 525 runs Parramatta to Lidcombe. The 533 runs Olympic Park to Chatswood.
- **Buses and ferries**: your festival ticket does not cover them. Tap a card or your phone.
- **Airport stations**: the station access fee is not covered either.
- **Light rail**: the L1 from Central and the L4 at Parramatta both run from 5am to 1am.
- **Taxis**: the ranks are on Herb Elliott Avenue.
- **Bring a hat**: the music starts at midday and the day is a long one.

Get there for the first band. Nearly ten hours of it, and the trip home is already sorted.
