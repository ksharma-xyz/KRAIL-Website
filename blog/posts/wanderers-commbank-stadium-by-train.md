Two games, one ground, one Friday night in Parramatta. The women play Wellington at 4.50pm, and
the men take on Adelaide at 7.40pm.

The tram that would normally drop you near the ground is shut that week. That part is on us. Here is
the train to Parramatta, the bus from Stand A4, and the walk.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Friday 20 November**, CommBank Stadium in Parramatta
Kick off: women **4.50pm**, men **7.40pm**, full time **8.35pm**
Station: **Parramatta**, then a 15 minute walk
The tram: **not running** past the ground that night
Last train to the city: **12.53am**
:::

::: callout Do this first
Do not plan around the light rail. The stretch you would use is closed for maintenance that week.
Walk the 15 minutes from Parramatta Square instead, or take a bus from Stand A4.
:::

## How do you get to CommBank Stadium?

Take a train to Parramatta. It is the closest station to the ground.

The 6.04pm from Central leaves platform 18 and is at Parramatta at 6.30pm, on platform 2. That lands
you there with time to spare before the men kick off.

Coming from the north west, ride the M1 metro to Chatswood or Central and change for a train to
Parramatta. You can also change at Epping, or at Bankstown for a train to Lidcombe.

From Parramatta you either walk or catch a bus. Both are below.

## Why can't you take the tram to the ground?

Because the stretch you need is the stretch that is closed.

The L4 does not run between Parramatta Square and Westmead from Wednesday 18 to Friday 20 November.
That is the part that passes the ground.

Buses replace it about every 10 to 15 minutes, from 5am to 1am.

Heading towards Westmead, the replacement stop for Parramatta Square is on Smith Street after Darcy
Street. It is about a minute from the tram stop.

Get off at the Prince Alfred Square replacement stop. It is on Victoria Road, opposite All Saints
Anglican Church. From there, follow Victoria Road to the ground.

The light rail fare applies on these buses. If the bus has a card reader, tap off as well as on.

## Is it quicker to walk?

Often, yes. It is a 15 minute walk to the ground from Parramatta Square.

Head for Prince Alfred Square, then follow Victoria Road the rest of the way.

Wear shoes you can walk in. You are doing it twice.

## Which bus drops you at the gate?

Buses from Stand A4 at Parramatta Interchange stop on O'Connell Street, right outside the ground.

Look for the 600, the 601 or the 604. The 606 and the 706 pull in there too.

On the evening of the match the 606 goes at 6.10pm and the 706 at 6.30pm.

Stand A4 is the accessible option as well. Every one of those buses is accessible.

## What time is the last train home?

Full time is 8.35pm. Add the walk back, and you are on a Parramatta platform around 9pm.

- **9.13pm** to the City Circle, from platform 4.
- **9.19pm** towards North Sydney via Central, from platform 1.
- **10.16pm** is the last direct run to the City Circle, from platform 4.
- **12.53am** is the last city bound train of all, from platform 3, into Central at 1.25am.
- **1.48am** is the last one west to Penrith, from platform 4.

Platform numbers shift around late at night. Read the board when you get there, or check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you start walking back.

## Heading south after the game?

Check this one before you leave home.

From 9.30pm that night, buses replace trains between Waterfall, Cronulla and Hurstville. Services
running further south are on buses between Waterfall and Hurstville as well.

Trains still run between Hurstville and Bondi Junction, some of them to a changed timetable.

## The practical stuff

- **Gates** open at 4pm, fifty minutes before the women kick off.
- **Ferry**: the closest wharf is Parramatta. F3 services are popular and can reach capacity.
- **Low tide** can put the Parramatta to Rydalmere leg of the ferry trip on a bus instead.
- **Taxi rank** on the eastern side of Villiers Street, between Ross Street and Victoria Road.
- **O'Connell Street** is a no stopping zone. Nobody gets dropped off there, at any hour.
- **Parking** at the ground: there is none for the public on game days.
- **Car parks** in walking distance are the Justice Precinct off Hunter Street and the station car
  park off Wentworth Street.
- **Traffic** builds on Church Street, Victoria Road, James Ruse Drive and Parramatta Road.

Get there for the women's game at 4.50pm and make a night of it. Two matches, one trip across town.
