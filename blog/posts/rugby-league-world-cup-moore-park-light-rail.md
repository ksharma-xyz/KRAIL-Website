The Rugby League World Cup runs at Allianz Stadium from 15 October to 8 November. The Moore Park
light rail stop sits right across from the ground. Everyone uses it at the same moment, so there will
be a queue.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Thursday 15 October**, gates open 6.30pm, kick off **8.05pm**
Stop: **Moore Park** light rail, on Anzac Parade just south of the ground
Your fare: **included in the match ticket**, both ways, until 4am
Walk instead: Central to the ground in about **25 minutes**
Last one home: **1.22am** to Circular Quay
:::

Keep your match ticket in your pocket once you are through the gates. It is your fare on the train
and the metro. It works on the bus, the ferry and the light rail too. Both directions, right through
until 4am the next morning. Show it to staff when you board.

## How do you get there?

Take the light rail from Central. Services leave from Chalmers Street. Catch an L2 towards Randwick
or an L3 towards Juniors Kingsford. Moore Park is the second stop, after Surry Hills.

The 6.35pm from Chalmers Street is at Moore Park at 6.40pm. Before 7.15pm they go every four
minutes. After that it is every five.

Coming from the east instead? From Bondi Junction Interchange, catch the 333, 352 or 440 to Oxford
Street. Route 355 goes to Lang Road. Either way you finish on foot. Regular buses along Anzac Parade
near the ground include the 339, 373, 374 and 396.

## Or walk it from Central

This is the quiet option, and it works in both directions. Leave Central by Devonshire Street. Then
take the Albert 'Tibby' Cotter Walkway across to Moore Park. It is about 25 minutes, with no queue at
either end.

Get there early and the Entertainment Quarter is right beside the ground. Eat before the gates open
rather than after full time.

## What about getting home?

This is the part that goes wrong if nobody thinks about it first.

Extra services run after the match. They show as **LX** and they go to Central Chalmers Street. On
15 October the first one leaves Moore Park at 9.53pm and the last at 11.03pm. Some of them go two
minutes apart.

The regular L2 and L3 to Circular Quay run right through the same hour. They leave every five to six
minutes.

Heading south? The L2 to Randwick and the L3 to Juniors Kingsford also go every five minutes once
the ground empties.

Express shuttle buses run from Moore Park to Central after the 15 October game as well.

The last city bound service from Moore Park that night is the 1.22am to Circular Quay. After that
there is nothing from the stop until 4.51am.

## Is your train home a bus that night?

Check this before you plan the trip back on 15 October. Full time is around 10pm, and the track is
being worked on from about then.

- On the **T2** and the **T5**, buses replace trains between Leppington and Fairfield from 9.40pm until 1.30am.
- On the **T3**, buses replace trains between Liverpool and Lidcombe over the same hours.
- On the **T6**, buses replace trains between Bankstown and Lidcombe from 9.10pm until 1.30am.
- On the **T1**, trains run to a changed timetable from 9.20pm and make extra stops between Seven Hills and Lidcombe.

If you are heading west or south west, allow a lot more than you would on a normal Thursday.

## If you were going to drive

Driver Avenue closes outside the stadium from 4.30pm on 15 October. It reopens once the crowd has
cleared.

Clearways run from 3pm to midnight on parts of Albion Street, Fitzroy Street, Lang Road and Moore
Park Road. On Cleveland Street they run from 6am to midnight. A car left in a clearway gets towed and
a fee applies.

The car parks fill fast. The Entertainment Quarter Wilson car park is open 24 hours, entered from
Lang Road via Errol Flynn Boulevard. The grass car parks on Driver Avenue open at 5.05pm on 15
October. So does the car park at Sydney Boys and Girls High School, entered from Cleveland Street.

Driving to a station is the version that works. Park & Ride gives you up to 18 hours of free parking
when you complete a public transport trip. Your match ticket covers that parking too. Press the
intercom at the exit gate on the way out and show the ticket. You can
[check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).

## The practical stuff

- **Taxi rank** on Errol Flynn Boulevard, near the Hordern Pavilion.
- **Rideshare pick up** on Errol Flynn Boulevard, or on Moore Park Road at Oatley Road on 15 October.
- **Airport stations** charge an access fee, and the match ticket does not cover it.
- **Keep the ticket** for the trip home. Without it you need a card or a contactless payment.
- **Accessible trips** show with the wheelchair icon in the trip planner.
- **8 November** is the other big day at the ground. Gates open 4pm, then kick offs at 4.15pm, 5.45pm and 8.05pm.
- **Car park times** and road closures for 8 November are yet to be confirmed.
