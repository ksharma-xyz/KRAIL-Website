Up to 180,000 people are expected on Norton Street in Leichhardt on Sunday 25 October. The closest
station is no use to you that day, because buses are replacing the trains into Petersham. The light
rail is the way in.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Sunday 25 October**, 10am to 5pm
Light rail: **L1** to Leichhardt North or Marion
Then: a **750m** walk, about 10 minutes
How often: every **6 minutes** from 10am to 7pm
Roads: Norton Street shut from **3am to 9pm**
:::

## How do you get there without a car?

Take the L1. It runs from Central out to Dulwich Hill through Darling Harbour and the inner west,
and both Leichhardt North and Marion are stops on it.

Get off at either one. Both are about 750 metres from Norton Street, which is a 10 minute walk.

On a Sunday the L1 goes every 6 minutes between 10am and 7pm. That covers the festival from open to
close.

At Central, catch it from the Grand Concourse. The L2 and L3 use Chalmers Street instead, so coming
in on one of those means walking across to the Grand Concourse first.

On the metro, stay on to Central and change there.

## What about the train?

Petersham is the closest station, and it is not close. Norton Street is 1.5km away, about a 20
minute walk via Railway Street.

On the day, buses replace trains between Strathfield and Central. Getting to Petersham by train
means changing to a replacement bus on the way.

Trains are not running between Central and Wynyard either. If you are starting in the city, the
metro, the light rail and local buses are what still run across it.

## Catching a bus instead?

Routes 437, 440 and 445 divert from 3am to 9pm. They run via Marion Street, Flood Street and
William Street while the road is shut.

The stops on Norton Street and Allen Street are closed. Two temporary ones replace them. One is on
William Street at Norton Street, stop ID 2040117. The other is on William Street at James Street,
stop ID 2040118.

If you are not sure which stop is yours, you can
[find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## The practical stuff

- **Crowds**: up to 180,000 people are expected across the seven hours.
- **Walking**: 750 metres from either light rail stop, 1.5km from Petersham.
- **Prams and wheelchairs**: accessible trips carry a wheelchair icon in the trip planner.
- **Roads**: Norton Street is closed between Marion Street and William Street from 3am to 9pm.
- **Parking**: street parking nearby is limited. Clearways apply on Norton Street from 3am.
- **Towing**: a clearway is a strict no-parking zone, and a car left in one is towed for a fee.
- **Taxi and rideshare**: pick a spot well clear of the closures and walk in from there.
- **Timing**: the arrival times shown for the L1 are approximate, so allow a little extra.

## Getting home

The L1 runs until 1am in both directions, every night of the week. There is no last service to
sprint for after a 5pm finish.

After 9pm on a Sunday it drops to every 15 minutes. Nothing runs at all between 1am and 5am.

The L1 does not carry real time yet. The minutes on the display are an estimate rather than a
countdown. For the trains and buses either side of it, check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave.
