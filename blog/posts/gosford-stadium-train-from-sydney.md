Mariners against Perth Glory, five o'clock kick off, on a Saturday afternoon. Gates open at four,
so there is a whole afternoon in it if you want one.

Here is the thing that keeps people in the car. Gosford is a long way from a Sydney front door, and
the trains home thin right out after the whistle. That part is on us.

::: facts The match, in short
When: **Saturday 21 November**, gates 4pm, kick off 5pm, full time 7pm
Station: **Gosford**, then a short walk via Showground Road and Dane Drive
Getting there: the **2.18pm** from Central is at Gosford at **3.36pm**
Coming home: trains at **7.21pm**, **7.31pm** and 8.09pm
Last train back: **11.28pm**, from platform 1
:::

::: callout Do this first
Pick your train home before kick off. Full time is 7pm, and the two straight after it leave Gosford
at 7.21pm and 7.31pm.
:::

## How do you get there from Sydney?

Take an intercity train from Central towards Wyong or Newcastle Interchange. Get off at Gosford.

They run about every half hour through the Saturday afternoon.

The 2.18pm from Central is at Gosford at 3.36pm. Gates open at four, so that is the one to be on.

Leaving it later? The 2.48pm gets in at 4.15pm and the 3.18pm at 4.36pm. The 3.48pm arrives at
5.15pm, which is after kick off.

## What is the walk from the station?

From Gosford station the stadium is a short walk via Showground Road and Dane Drive.

Dane Drive closes to cars at 3pm, between the Central Coast Highway and Donnison Street. It opens
again once the crowd has cleared.

## Coming off the metro or the light rail?

On the metro, change at Epping or Central for a train to Gosford.

On the L1, L2 or L3, change at Central.

On the L4, get off at Parramatta Square. Take a train towards Epping or Central from there.

The L4 is closed between Westmead and Carlingford that weekend. Buses are doing its job, so allow
extra time.

## Check your line before you leave home

There is trackwork across the city on Saturday 21 November.

- **T4**: buses replace trains between Cronulla, Waterfall and Central.
- **T8**: buses replace trains between Sydenham and Central.
- **T3**: a changed timetable between Liverpool and Lidcombe, 6.30am to 10.30am.
- **T6**: every 30 minutes, 6.30am to 10.30am.

Going up in the morning instead? From 5.30am to 11.10am, all trains leave from platform 2 at
Hawkesbury River and Cowan. Trains towards Gosford keep stopping at Cowan.

## What time is your train home?

Full time is 7pm. Every train back to Sydney leaves Gosford from platform 1.

- **7.21pm**, into Central at 8.36pm.
- **7.31pm**, into Central at 9pm.
- **8.09pm**, into Central at 9.30pm.
- **9pm**, into Central at 10.29pm.
- **10pm**, into Central at 11.30pm.

The gaps get long after the 8.09pm. Want dinner in Gosford first? Aim at the 9pm.

## When is the last train back?

The last one leaves Gosford at 11.28pm and reaches Central at 1.02am. After that there is nothing
until 3.16am.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk back up Dane Drive.
A timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually turning up.

## The practical stuff

- **Buses** 19, 21, 22, 23, 64 and 67 run towards the stadium and stop near it.
- **Tapping on**: a contactless card or a linked device works on every leg.
- **Taxi ranks** at the Central Coast Leagues Club, and at Gosford station on William Street.
- **Accessible trips** are marked in the trip planner, and you can filter to only those.
- **Traffic** on the roads in is heavier than usual before and after the match.

## If you were going to drive

Three car parks sit within walking distance of the ground.

Baker Street Car Park (P1) is about 100m away, on a flat rate. The station car park (P2) is about
300m away, on Park & Ride conditions. The Central Coast Leagues Club car park charges a fee.

Get there when the gates open and take the afternoon slowly. The way home is already sorted.
