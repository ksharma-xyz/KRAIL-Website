Kick-off at Campbelltown Sports Stadium is 7.40pm on Saturday 31 October. Leumeah station is a
short walk from the ground. The last train to the city leaves at 11.34pm, which is a bit over two
hours after full time.

The thing that puts people in a car for a night game out here is the trip home. You picture yourself
at Leumeah at eleven at night with nothing left to catch. On a Saturday that is not what the
timetable does.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Saturday 31 October**, kick-off **7.40pm**, done by about **9.30pm**
Station: **Leumeah** on the T8, a short walk to the ground
Getting there: the **6.21pm** from Central is at Leumeah at **7.14pm**
Trains home: about every **15 minutes**, last one **11.34pm**
Buses to the ground: routes **870**, **871**, **872**, **879** and **881**
:::

## How do you get there?

Take a T8 train towards Campbelltown or Macarthur. Get off at Leumeah. It is a short walk from
there to the ground.

From Central, the 6.21pm is at Leumeah at 7.14pm. That train leaves Town Hall at 6.06pm. The next
one goes from Central at 6.36pm and gets in at 7.29pm.

Trains arrive about every 15 minutes through the evening, so a missed one is not a missed kick-off.

Coming in on the metro or the light rail, change at Central for a T8 train. From Parramatta, take a
train to Glenfield and change there for Leumeah.

Driving to the station instead of the ground works too. The commuter car park at Leumeah is off
Airds Road and Plough Inn Road. A pedestrian link runs from there to the stadium.

## What does the trackwork change?

There is trackwork across several lines that Saturday. Buses replace trains between Hornsby and
Strathfield. The inner west services do not run, and T1 and T2 trains make extra stops instead.

The T8 to Leumeah keeps running. It is the leg that gets you to the T8 that is worth a look before
you go.

## What time is the train home?

Full time is around 9.30pm. Trains towards the city leave Leumeah at 9.49pm and 10.04pm, then carry
on about every 15 minutes.

The last one goes at 11.34pm and reaches Central at 12.40am. Nobody has to leave on 85 minutes to
beat the timetable.


## The practical stuff

- **Buses**: routes 870, 871, 872, 879 and 881 stop within walking distance of the ground.
- **Taxis**: the nearest rank is at Leumeah station.
- **Parking** off Old Leumeah Road is limited, so the station car park is the other option.
- **Driving** in means heavier traffic than usual on the roads near the ground.
- **Accessible travel**: you can plan the trip with accessible services only.
- **One stop too far**: from Campbelltown, a city-bound train is back at Leumeah in two minutes.
