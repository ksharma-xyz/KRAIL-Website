Two platforms at Town Hall both say T4. One of them has you at Bondi Junction ten minutes later.

The other one is heading south, and you find that out somewhere past Central. Six platforms, five
exits, and the letters on the front of the train are not enough. That part is on us.

::: facts Town Hall, platform by platform
Platform 1: the T2 west, and some T3 services
Platform 2: the T1 and the T9 west, through Strathfield
Platform 3: the T1 and the T9 north, over the bridge
Platform 4: the T4 south to Hurstville and Cronulla
Platform 5: the T4 east to Bondi Junction
Platform 6: the T8 to the airport, and the T3
:::

## Which platform at Town Hall?

The platforms sit on two levels, and the station splits into two sides.

One side serves platforms 1, 2 and 4. The other serves platforms 3, 5 and 6. The lifts and the
stairs are signed with those numbers.

Read the platform number first. Then pick your stairs. Going down the wrong side means coming back
up.

- **Platform 1**: the T2 out through the Inner West. Some T3 services on weekdays.
- **Platform 2**: the T1 west to Parramatta and Penrith. The T9 north through Strathfield.
- **Platform 3**: the T1 and the T9 over the Harbour Bridge to the North Shore.
- **Platform 4**: the T4 south to Hurstville, Cronulla and Waterfall.
- **Platform 5**: the T4 east to Bondi Junction.
- **Platform 6**: the T8 to the airport, and the T3 to Liverpool and Lidcombe.

## Why do two platforms both say T4?

Because two different T4 trips call here, and they go opposite ways.

Platform 5 goes east. The 11.02am on a weekday calls at Martin Place, Kings Cross and Edgecliff. It
is at Bondi Junction at 11.12am.

Platform 4 goes south. The 11.06am is at Central at 11.08am, then Sydenham and Wolli Creek.
Hurstville comes up at 11.34am.

Four minutes apart on the indicator. Nothing alike after that.

Read the destination, not the line code.

## Which train gets you to Central first?

Three of the six platforms do it in two or three minutes.

The 11.01am from platform 1 is at Central at 11.04am. The 11.05am from platform 2 gets there at
11.08am. Platform 4 is the quickest, at two minutes.

Platform 6 goes the long way round. The 11.03am calls at Wynyard, Circular Quay, St James and
Museum first. It reaches Central at 11.16am.

Thirteen minutes instead of three. The train is not running late. It is just going round the other
way.

Platforms 3 and 5 do not reach Central at all. One goes over the bridge, the other goes east.

## Which exit do you want?

The exits are numbered 1 to 5, and the number is on the sign above the stairs.

- **Exit 1, QVB**: the York St bus stands A to F. The QVB and The Galeries.
- **Exit 2, George St**: the light rail stop. Also the Park St bus stands G to K.
- **Exit 3, Pavilion Plaza**: Pavilion Plaza.
- **Exit 4, Cathedral**: the light rail stop again, and St Andrew's Cathedral.
- **Exit 5, Town Hall**: the Druitt St bus stands L and N. Sydney Town Hall and Town Hall Square.

Darling Harbour is signed from Exit 4 and from Exit 5. Either one works.

## Where do the buses go from?

Three different streets, which is the part that catches people out.

York St has stands A to F, through Exit 1. Park St has stands G to K, through Exit 2. Druitt St has
stands L and N, through Exit 5.

Stand E and Stand F take arrivals only. Nothing leaves from either.

Park St is where it gets confusing. Stand G and Stand J sit on the same street and carry the same
route numbers.

Stand G sends the 324 and the 325 to Watsons Bay. The 311 from it goes to Central.

Stand J sends the same 324 and 325 to Walsh Bay. The 311 from it goes to Millers Point, and the
389 to Pyrmont.

Same numbers, different stand, different end of town.

Over on York St, Stand D has the 100 to Taronga Zoo and the 120 to Chatswood. Stand B has the BN1
to Mona Vale.

Check the stand letter before the route number. If you only know the number, you can
[find the stop by it](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## How do you get home after the trains stop?

From Park St, Stand H. One stand carries almost all of them.

On a Wednesday night the N50 to Liverpool goes at 12.04am. The N71 to Richmond goes at 12.29am. The
N70 to Penrith goes at 12.59am, and the N10 to Sutherland at 1.00am.

Each one then repeats every hour until about 4am. Miss one and you wait a full hour, so this is the
one to check before you walk up.

Three routes leave from Stand K instead. The N90 to Hornsby, the N91 to Macquarie Centre and the
N92 to Tallawong all go from there.

The trains themselves stop at different times by platform. On a Wednesday night the last one to the
airport leaves platform 6 at 11.51pm. Platform 4 keeps running south until 1.06am.

So the airport trains stop more than an hour before the T4 south does. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you head down.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** are at the southern end of the station, near Exit 4. There is an accessible toilet and
  a baby change room.
- **Lifts** run to the platforms and are signed by number. One side covers 1, 2 and 4. The other
  covers 3, 5 and 6.
- **Prams and suitcases** should take the lift rather than the stairs.
- **Tickets**: there are top up and single trip ticket machines at the station.
- **Light rail**: the L2 and the L3 stop here. Use Exit 2 or Exit 4.
- **Staff**: the station office is beside Exit 2, on the George St side.

Two platforms say T4 and two stands on Park St say 324. Pick by where you are getting off, and
check the exit number before you walk up.
