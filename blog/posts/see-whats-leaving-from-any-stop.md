You are at Seven Hills with nowhere particular to be, and you just want to know what is leaving next.
KRAIL has a live departure board for the stop, and it fits right in your pocket.

::: shot
![The stop sheet for Seven Hills Station with its stop ID and a Departure Board row](/images/krail/see-whats-leaving-from-any-stop/01-stop-sheet-departure-board-button.png "Every stop carries a board. It is one row down.")

## Two ways to open it

- On your trip, tap the little clock icon next to the stop name, then tap **Departure Board**.
- Or tap any stop on the map, then tap **Departure Board** on the card that slides up.
:::

::: shot flip
![A live departure board mixing modes, with a bus in 1 min from Stand A, a train showing Now on Platform 1, and delays with the old time struck through](/images/krail/see-whats-leaving-from-any-stop/02-live-board-delays.png "Trains and buses in one list, in the order they actually leave.")

## What you see

Every row gives you:

- a countdown to when it leaves
- the line, with its own coloured badge
- the platform or stand
- **Delayed** or **Early** if it is running off time
:::

The board updates by itself every 30 seconds, so you can keep it open while you wait.

## Only care about one line?

Tap the filter icon, then tap your line's badge. Everything else disappears, and you are left with just the services
you might actually catch.

Just missed one? Tap **Show previous departures** to see what left in the last 30 minutes, which is
handy for working out whether it was yours.

## Good to know

- It works for train stations, bus stops, ferry wharves and light rail stops.
- The board needs a data connection, because it is live.
