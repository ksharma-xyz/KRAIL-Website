At Wynyard the platform number tells you the direction. Platform 4 goes north over the bridge.
Platforms 3 and 5 go to Town Hall. Platform 6 goes to Circular Quay first, then the long way round
to Central.

The platforms here are numbered 3 to 6. On the concourse, one set of stairs is signed for platforms
3-4 and another for platforms 5-6. Pick the right set and you have already done the hard part.

::: facts Wynyard, in short
Going north: **Platform 4**, across the Harbour Bridge
Town Hall and Central: **Platform 3** or **platform 5**
Circular Quay and the airport: **Platform 6**
Buses: stands on **five streets** around the station
Staffed: **24 hours**
:::

## Which platform at Wynyard?

- **Platform 3**: T1 and T9 trains to Town Hall, Central and out west.
- **Platform 4**: T1 and T9 trains north to Milsons Point and North Sydney.
- **Platform 5**: T2 and T3 trains to Town Hall, Central and the south west.
- **Platform 6**: T8 and some T2 trains, to Circular Quay first.

Platform 4 is the one that crosses the Harbour Bridge. Nothing else at Wynyard does.

The 11.02am from platform 4 is at Milsons Point at 11.05am and North Sydney at 11.07am. If you are
heading for Chatswood, Gordon or Hornsby, that is your platform.

Watch the word after "via" on anything to Hornsby. A train to Hornsby leaves platform 3 as well. It
goes through the city and out via Strathfield, which is the long way round.

## Why is platform 6 slower to Central?

Because it takes the city loop the other way.

A train from platform 6 calls at Circular Quay first. Then St James and Museum. Then Central.

A train from platform 5 calls at Town Hall. Then Central.

On a weekday the 11.06am from platform 6 reaches Central at 11.16am. The 11.07am from platform 5
reaches Central at 11.13am.

One leaves a minute later and gets there three minutes earlier.

So take platform 3 or 5 for Town Hall, Central, or anywhere west and south west. Take platform 6 for
Circular Quay.

Platform 6 is also the one for the airport. That train runs through Central and on to Green Square,
Mascot and both terminals.

## Where are the buses at Wynyard?

They are spread over five streets. The stand letter is what tells you which one is yours.

- **Carrington Street**, stands A to D. The Northern Beaches. The B1 to Mona Vale leaves from stand B.
- **Clarence Street**, stands Q to T. The lower north shore and the Hills. Lane Cove, Macquarie
  Park and St Ives too. The Hills motorway buses go from stand Q.
- **York Street**, stands G to L. Mostly the same routes, caught earlier in their run.
- **Jamison Street** has stand U. **Kent Street** has stand V.

## Why is the York Street bus slower?

Because it has the QVB to do before it leaves town.

Take the 100 towards Taronga Zoo. A bus from stand H on York Street runs down to the QVB and turns
round. It comes back up Clarence Street and passes Wynyard a second time.

The 5.02pm from stand H is back at Wynyard at 5.13pm. It reaches Neutral Bay Junction at 5.24pm.
The 5.05pm from stand T on Clarence Street is there at 5.16pm.

Three minutes later off the kerb, eight minutes earlier at the other end.

Read the street on the departure, not just the route number.

## How do you get to Barangaroo from Wynyard?

Follow the signs for Barangaroo rather than any street name.

A walkway called Wynyard Walk runs from the Clarence Street end of the station down to the water. It
comes out at the metro station and the ferry wharves.

The F3, F4 and F10 ferries leave from there.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are at the station 24 hours.
- **Toilets** are on the concourse, inside the paid area. They open during service hours.
- **Light rail**: the L2 and L3 stop on George St. Leave by the George St exit.
- **Taxis** rank on Carrington St and Pitt St.
- **Paying**: tap on and off with a card or a phone at the readers.

## Getting home late

The four platforms stop at four different times. Find your platform and read across.

- **Platform 3**: 12.10am Monday to Thursday. 1.10am Friday. 1.03am Saturday. 12.18am Sunday.
- **Platform 4**: 12.31am Monday to Thursday. 1.31am Friday. 1.21am Saturday. 12.45am Sunday.
- **Platform 5**: 12.34am Monday to Thursday. 1.17am Friday. 1.06am Saturday. 12.06am Sunday.
- **Platform 6**: 12.43am Monday to Thursday. 1.43am Friday. 1.28am Saturday. 12.58am Sunday.

Sunday has a catch going north. The 12.45am from platform 4 only runs as far as North Sydney. If you
are going further than that, the 12.36am is your last one.

Trackwork moves these times on some nights. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

Four platforms, and the number is the instruction. Platform 4 crosses the bridge. Platform 6 goes to
Circular Quay. The other two go to Town Hall.
