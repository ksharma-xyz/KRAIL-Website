Blacktown is where the line splits. One half carries on west to Penrith. The other turns north for
Quakers Hill, Schofields and Richmond.

Both say T1 on the front. Get the wrong one and you work it out a few stops later, in the wrong
half of western Sydney.

The Richmond trains leave from platform 2, and platform 2 is not where you would guess. That part
is on us.

::: facts Blacktown, in short
North to Richmond: **platform 2**
West to Penrith: **platforms 6 and 7**
Back towards Parramatta: **platforms 1, 3, 4 and 5**
Own island: **platforms 1 and 2**, up at the north end
Bus stands: **A to N**, with no I
:::

## Which platform at Blacktown goes to Richmond?

Platform 2. Nothing else.

Marayong, Quakers Hill, Schofields, Riverstone and Richmond are all up that branch. Every train
that turns north leaves from platform 2.

The catch is where platform 2 is. Platforms 1 and 2 share an island of their own at the north end
of the station. Platforms 3 to 7 are down the other way.

So you cannot drift across from platform 7 when you hear the announcement. You have to go back up
to the concourse and walk the length of it.

Give yourself the extra minute.

## Which platform goes west, and which goes back to the city?

The rest of Blacktown sorts into two groups.

- **Platforms 6 and 7**: west. Doonside, Rooty Hill, Mount Druitt, Penrith and Emu Plains. The
  intercity trains up to Katoomba and Lithgow go from platform 6.
- **Platforms 1, 3, 4 and 5**: back towards Parramatta. That is the way to the city, and the T5 to
  Leppington goes this way too.

On a Tuesday morning the 8.01am from platform 7 is headed for Penrith. The 8.01am from platform 6
is the intercity train to Lithgow. They leave in the same minute from two different islands.

Read the destination, then the platform, then walk.

## Which stand is your bus on at Blacktown?

The stands sit over on the Richmond Road side, across the concourse from the platforms. They are
lettered A to N, and they skip I. Thirteen of them.

Pick the place you are going and the letter follows.

- **Parramatta**: Stand H for the 700. Stand K for the 705 and the 711. Stand M for the 661 and
  the 706.
- **Rouse Hill and Castle Hill**: Stand M for the 730, 731, 732 and 735. Stand A for the 752.
- **Mount Druitt**: Stands B, C, E and F all have one. Go by the route number here.
- **Macquarie Park and Epping**: Stand L, for the 611 and the 630.
- **Fairfield**: Stand J, for the 800 and the 812.
- **Riverstone**: Stand M, for the 734.
- **Blacktown Hospital**: Stand G, for the 721.
- **Night buses**: Stand D.

The 711 is the one that catches people. From Stand K it runs to Parramatta. From Stand D it runs
to Westpoint, the other way entirely.

Check the letter before the number. If the number is all you have, you can
[find the stop by it](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## How do you get home from Blacktown late?

The two branches keep running past midnight. The city direction does not.

The last train north is the 1.01am to Richmond from platform 2. The last train west is the 1.04am
to Penrith from platform 7.

The city direction stops much earlier. The last one goes at 12.03am from platform 1. The next is
the 3.36am from platform 4.

The night buses cover it, and they split the same way the trains do. All of them go from Stand D.

- The **N71** runs to Town Hall at 12.23am, then 1.23am, 2.23am, 3.23am and 4.23am.
- The **N71** runs to Richmond at 1.32am, 2.32am, 3.32am and 4.32am.
- The **N70** runs to Town Hall at 12.53am, then 1.53am, 2.53am and 3.53am.
- The **N70** runs to Penrith at 2.02am, 3.02am and 4.02am.

Hourly, all of them. Trackwork moves these on some nights, so check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you head down.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are on duty 24 hours, on weekdays and at weekends.
- **Lifts, escalators and stairs** run between the concourse and every platform.
- **Toilets** are by the concourse, and there is an accessible one.
- **Parking** is in the commuter car park, and there is an accessible car space.
- **Taxis** rank at the Main Street end, beside one of the station entries.
- **Kiss and Ride** is at the north end, by the platform 1 and 2 entry.
- **Bikes** go in the racks, the lockers or the bike shed.
- **Phone charging** is free at the station.
- **Peak times** here run 6.30am to 10am and 3pm to 7pm, Monday to Thursday.
- **Paying**: tap on and off with a card or a phone. There are top up and single trip ticket
  machines.

One station, two branches and thirteen bus stands. Platform 2 for Richmond, platforms 6 and 7 for
Penrith, and everything else points back at Parramatta.
