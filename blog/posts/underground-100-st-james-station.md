Hidden platforms. Historic trains rolling through the city. Vintage buses pulling up outside on
Elizabeth Street.

Underground 100 is at St James station on Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December, 10am to 4pm. The venue
is the station itself. Getting there is the whole trip, and that part is on us.

::: facts The weekend, in short
When: **Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 December**, 10am to 4pm
Where: **St James station**, Elizabeth St, Sydney
Trains: the **T2**, the **T3** and the **T8** stop here
Book ahead: the **train journeys** and the **hidden platforms**
Prices: **free** to $10, depending on the ticket
:::

::: callout Do this first
Two things need booking before you turn up. The centenary train journeys, and the hidden platform
experience. Places on both are limited.
:::

## What is on down there?

A hundred years of trains under the city, marked at St James for two days.

- **Immersive displays** about a century beneath the street.
- **Live entertainment** inspired by the 1920s.
- **Historic trains** running centenary journeys through the city.
- **Guided experiences** into spaces that are rarely seen.
- **Vintage buses** from Elizabeth Street outside, running from 9am to 5pm.
- **Food stalls** and heritage merchandise.

The vintage buses are run by the Sydney Bus Museum. They are free, or a gold coin donation.

## How do you get to St James?

Take a train. The T2, the T3 and the T8 all stop at St James.

From Central it is four minutes. Museum is the only stop in between.

On the metro, get off at Gadigal or Martin Place. Both are a short walk away.

On the light rail, ride the L2 or the L3 to
[QVB or Town Hall](/blog/town-hall-station-which-exit/).

Coming by ferry, get off at Circular Quay. Take a train or a bus from there.

Buses stop at the door. The stands are on Elizabeth Street and Castlereagh Street.

## Which way out, and which platform home?

There are two ways out, one at each end of the concourse.

Elizabeth Street is the easy one. The lift and the marked step-free path are at that end.

Macquarie Street is the other, and it is stairs. That exit shuts at 9.45pm.

Going home, platform 1 heads for Circular Quay. Three minutes.

Platform 2 goes the other way. Museum in one minute, then Central.

## What changes on the trains that weekend?

There is trackwork on both days, and some of it starts early.

- On the **T2**, buses replace trains between Leppington and Glenfield.
- On the **T8**, buses replace trains between Glenfield and Sydenham.
- On the **T3**, Saturday morning runs to a changed timetable until 10.30am.
- On the **T6**, Saturday morning trains drop to every 30 minutes.

All of it is listed as subject to change. Check your line the night before.

Then check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) in the morning. A timetable is
the plan. The countdown is what is actually running.

## The practical stuff

- **Hours**: 10am to 4pm, both days.
- **Toilets** are on the platforms, open while trains are running.
- **Staff** are on duty at this station 24 hours.
- **Step-free access** is from Elizabeth Street, by lift, along a marked path.
- **Taxi ranks** on Pitt, Elizabeth, Castlereagh and Phillip Streets.
- **Driving** is not recommended. Parking nearby is limited and fills early.
- **Hyde Park** is right outside the door.
- **Close by**: the State Library, the Theatre Royal, Hyde Park Barracks and St Mary's Cathedral.

Lock in the booked bits, then let the rest of the day go slowly. The Domain is about 400 metres
from the station, and the Art Gallery of NSW is just past it.
