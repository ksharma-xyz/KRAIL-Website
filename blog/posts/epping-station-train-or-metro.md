Epping has two ways into the city. The metro from platform 5, and the train from platform 1. They
do not stop in the same places. So the one you want depends on where you are getting off.

The metro runs under the harbour. In the city it calls at Barangaroo, Martin Place, Gadigal and
Central.

The train runs through Strathfield. In the city it calls at Central, Town Hall and Wynyard.

The board shows where a train ends up, not the city. Platform 5 says Sydenham. The T9 on platform 1
says Gordon, or Hornsby on a weekend. Both of those run through the city on the way.

::: facts Epping, in short
Metro to the city: **platform 5**, every 4 to 10 minutes
Metro the other way: **platform 6**, out to Tallawong
Trains to the city: **platform 1**, and platform 2 in the peaks
Trains going north: **platform 3**, and platform 2 the rest of the day
Buses: on Beecroft Rd and Cambridge St, in lettered stands
:::

## Which platform at Epping?

- **Platform 1**: trains towards the city, through Strathfield.
- **Platform 2**: trains to Hornsby for most of the day. In the peaks it goes to the city instead.
- **Platform 3**: trains north, up to Hornsby and on to the Central Coast.
- **Platform 5**: the metro to the city and on to Sydenham.
- **Platform 6**: the metro out to Macquarie Park and Tallawong.

There is no platform 4 at Epping. The map jumps straight from 3 to 5.

Three of those never swap. Platform 3 goes north. Platform 5 goes to the city. Platform 6 goes to
Tallawong. Platform 2 is the one that moves, so read the board there.

## Metro or train into the city?

The metro leaves platform 5 every 4 to 10 minutes all day. You never need to look it up.

On a weekday the 11.02am metro is at [Chatswood](/blog/chatswood-station-metro-platform/) at
11.15am. It reaches Martin Place at 11.26am and Central at 11.30am.

Two very different trains go to the city from platform 1.

One is the intercity service running through to the Central Coast. It comes twice an hour and stops
almost nowhere. The 11am is at Strathfield at 11.11am and Central at 11.25am.

The other is the T9, which stops at everything. The 11.12am is at Central at 11.48am. Town Hall is
11.51am and Wynyard is 11.54am.

Take the metro for Barangaroo, Martin Place or Gadigal.
Take the train for Town Hall or Wynyard.
For Central, take whichever one leaves first.

## Two trains say Hornsby

This is the one that catches people, and it catches them on a weekend.

On a Saturday morning the board on platform 2 says Hornsby. The board on platform 1 says Hornsby via
Gordon. Same name on both.

The 11.08am from platform 2 is at Hornsby at 11.23am. Fifteen minutes.

The 11.25am from platform 1 is at Hornsby at 12.51pm. It goes through the city first, over the
Harbour Bridge, then all the way up the North Shore.

Same destination. An hour and a half between them.

Platform 1 is the one heading for the city. If you want to go straight up the line, you want
platform 2.

## How far down is the metro?

The metro is not across the platform from the train. It is two levels below it.

The station map draws four levels. Concourse at the top. Then the train platforms. Then a transfer
concourse. The metro platforms are at the bottom.

So choose before you go down. Changing your mind costs you two levels back up.

There are lifts the whole way. From the concourse one lift goes to platforms 1 and 2, another to
platform 3, and another to platforms 5 and 6. The train platforms have their own lift down to the
metro.

## Which way out?

The concourse runs between two streets, with a third at the end.

Beecroft Rd is one side. That is where the bus stands are.

Langston Pl is the other side. The toilets are on the concourse at that end.

Cambridge St is at the end of the concourse. Walk out that way for the commuter car park.

## Where are the buses?

The bus stands are out on the street, not inside the station. Most of them are on Beecroft Rd, each
with a letter.

The 550 is the one to get right. It leaves from stand D for Parramatta and from stand C for
Macquarie Park. Same route number, two stands, pointing opposite ways.

The 630 to Blacktown goes from stand F. The 651 to Rouse Hill goes from stand G.

The rest are around on Cambridge St. The 291 to McMahons Point goes from stand A.

Know the number but not the stand? You can
[find the stop by its route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Getting home late

The metro runs later than the train on most nights. Sunday is the exception, and the gap is a big
one.

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: the last metro to the city is 12.14am. The last train is 12.00am.
- **Friday nights**: the last metro is 1.14am and the last train 12.27am.
- **Saturday nights**: the last metro is 1.12am and the last train 12.40am.
- **Sunday nights**: the last metro to the city is 10.42pm. The last train is 12.33am.

On a Sunday the metro into the city gives up before eleven. The 10.52pm from platform 5 only runs as
far as Chatswood.

Miss the 10.42pm and you have not missed the night. Go up to platform 1 instead. The 11.04pm gets
into Central at 11.30pm.

Heading the other way, the last metro to Tallawong leaves platform 6 at 12.58am Monday to Thursday.
On Friday and Saturday nights it is 1.58am. On a Sunday it is 11.28pm. The
[Park & Ride](/blog/tallawong-parking-lottery/) is at the end of that one.

Trackwork changes these times on some weekends.
Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are at the station whenever services are running.
- **Toilets** are on the concourse, open while the station is staffed.
- **Lifts** reach every platform from the concourse.
- **Bikes** can be parked at the station, and the lockers can be booked online.
- **Car park**: the way in on foot is from Cambridge St.
- **Paying**: tap on and off with a card or a linked device. There are top up and single trip ticket
  machines as well.

Platform 1 for the city on the train. Platform 5 for the city on the metro. Everywhere else at
Epping, read the board before you step on.
