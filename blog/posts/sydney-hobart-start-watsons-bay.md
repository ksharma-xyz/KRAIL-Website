One o'clock on Boxing Day, the fleet comes down the harbour and turns out through the Heads for
Tasmania. Watsons Bay is the corner they do it on.

Get there early. Big crowds are expected at the Heads, and you want to be sitting down before noon
with something cold.

Here is the catch. The ferry you would take to Watsons Bay is not running when
you need it. The last one leaves Circular Quay at 11.15am. That part is on us.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Saturday 26 December**, the race starts at **1pm**
Getting there: Extra route **329X** buses from Circular Quay, **9am to 12.30pm**
Last ferry out: **11.15am** from Circular Quay, then nothing until 1.15pm
Coming home: Route **329X** from **1.30pm**, or the ferry from **1.45pm**
Out of the harbour: the fleet is gone by **3pm**
:::

## How do you get to Watsons Bay?

1. Get to Circular Quay. It is the closest station to the harbour.
2. Take an extra route 329X bus from there to Watsons Bay. They run between 9am and 12.30pm.
3. Want the ferry instead? Catch the F9 before 11.15am. That is the last one out.
4. Coming from the east, take an extra route 380 from Bondi Junction between 10am and 12pm.
5. Be in your spot before noon. The exclusion zone on the water starts at 12pm.

If you are not sure which stop is yours, you can
[find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Why does the ferry stop running?

A Sydney Harbour exclusion zone sits across the start area from 12pm until 2.20pm. The ferries that
would cross it pause while it is in place.

For the F9 that means the last one out of Circular Quay is at 11.15am. The last one back from
Watsons Bay is at 11.45am. Then it pauses, and the next one out of Circular Quay is at 1.15pm.

The F7 Double Bay is the same story. Its last ferry out of Circular Quay goes at 10.57am and
services resume at 1.57pm.

Every other ferry runs as normal, on a public holiday timetable.

## What if you would rather watch from Manly?

Manly is on the list of good vantage points too. The ferry there has the same problem.

F1 services stop through the middle of the day. Extra route 170X buses run every 15 minutes between
Manly and Wynyard from 12pm until 2.45pm. The ferry picks up again from Manly at 2.40pm.

## How do you get home?

The worry that puts people in a car is being stuck out at the Heads when it is over. There are four
ways back, and each one has a time on it.

- **Route 329X** runs Watsons Bay to Circular Quay from 1.30pm until 4.30pm.
- **The F9 ferry** starts again from Watsons Bay at 1.45pm. Extra sailings go at 2pm, 2.30pm and 3pm.
- **Route 380** runs Watsons Bay to Bondi Junction from 1.15pm until 5.30pm.
- **Route 324** runs Watsons Bay to Edgecliff from 11.50am until 2.30pm. Change there for a train.

Pick the one you are leaving on before you set off. Then check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk up to the road.

## Should you drive?

No. Traffic is heavier than normal on the roads around the foreshore. Street parking near the
vantage points is limited and a fee may apply.

Police may also close roads near the vantage points as the crowds build. Buses then use different
stops or finish short of where you wanted them.

Route 325 towards the city is already diverted. It runs via Wentworth Road instead of Vaucluse Road
between 9am and 6pm, so the Vaucluse Road stops are missed.

## The practical stuff

- **Boxing Day** is a public holiday, so the ferries that do run use a holiday timetable.
- **Trains**: Circular Quay is the closest station to the harbour. Wynyard and Martin Place are a
  short walk from the start area.
- **Metro**: get off at Martin Place and connect from there to Circular Quay.
- **Light rail**: the L2 and the L3 both run to Circular Quay.
- **Trackwork** can change how the trains run on the day. Check your trip before you leave.
- **Kayaks and paddleboards** cannot anchor around the exclusion zone between 12pm and 2.20pm.
- **South Head** is north of the wharf, inside Sydney Harbour National Park. The park is open
  sunrise to sunset.
- **Tap on and off** the way you would on any other trip.
- **A hat and sunscreen**. It is late December and the start is in the middle of the day.

Pick the bus out and the ride home before you go. Then you get to spend the afternoon watching the
boats instead of your phone.

If you want this stretch of coast on a quieter day, the
[bus to Nielsen Park](/blog/milk-beach-nielsen-park-by-bus/) drops you a few bays short of here.
