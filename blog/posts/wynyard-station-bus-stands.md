The 324 from York Street goes to Watsons Bay. The 324 from Kent Street goes to Walsh Bay. Same
number, same station, opposite ends of the harbour.

Wynyard has bus stands lettered A to V, spread across five streets. Take the wrong stairs up and
you watch yours pull out one street over. That part is on us.

::: facts Wynyard, in short
Platforms: **3 and 4** over the bridge, **5 and 6** round the city
Bus stands: **A to V**, across five streets
Ways out: George St, Wynyard Park, York St, Clarence St
Down the tunnel: **Barangaroo**, for the metro and the ferry wharves
Last train out: **12.43am**, and the next one is at 4.31am
:::

## Which exit do you want at Wynyard?

Four ways out of the concourse. Each one is named after where it puts you.

- **George St** for the light rail. The L2 and the L3 both stop on George Street.
- **Wynyard Park** for the park itself. The Carrington Street stands run along the far side.
- **York St** for the York Street stands, G through M.
- **Clarence St** for the Clarence Street stands, Q through T.

There is a fifth way out and it goes down rather than up. The Wynyard Walk is a tunnel from the
Clarence Street end, and it comes out at Barangaroo.

## Which stand is your bus on?

Pick the street first. The letters follow it.

- **Carrington Street**, along Wynyard Park. Stand A for Manly, Balgowlah and Warringah Mall. Stand
  B for the B1 to Mona Vale. Stand C for Collaroy Plateau, Narrabeen and Avalon. Stand D for Dee
  Why and Narraweena.
- **York Street**, straight up from the concourse. Stand G for Watsons Bay and Central. Stand H for
  Taronga Zoo and Chatswood. Stands J and L for buses carrying on to the QVB. Stand K for the night
  routes heading into the city. Stand M for the BN1.
- **Clarence Street**, one block west. Stand Q for Bella Vista, Castle Hill and Kellyville. Stand R
  for Lane Cove, Macquarie University and Epping. Stand S for St Ives Chase, Terrey Hills and
  Belrose. Stand T for Taronga Zoo, Spit Junction and Balmoral.
- **Kent Street**, down at the Barangaroo end. Stand V for Walsh Bay and Millers Point. Also
  Cherrybrook, Dural and West Pennant Hills.
- **Jamison Street** has one stand and one route. Stand U, and the 165X to South Curl Curl.

## Why does the same bus number leave from two streets?

Because the two are pointed opposite ways.

The 324 and the 325 from Stand G on York Street run east to Watsons Bay. The same two numbers from
Stand V on Kent Street run west to Walsh Bay.

The 311 does it as well. From Stand G it heads for Central. From Stand V it heads for Millers
Point.

The Hills buses split the same way. The 607X from Stand Q on Clarence Street runs out to Bella
Vista. The 607X from Stand J on York Street is going no further than the QVB.

Read the stand letter before the route number. If the number is all you have, you can
[find the stop by it](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Which platform for your train?

The stairs are signed in pairs. Platforms 3 and 4 on one side, platforms 5 and 6 on the other.

- **Platform 4** is the only one going north over the bridge. Milsons Point and North Sydney
  first. Then Gordon, Hornsby and Berowra.
- **Platform 3** goes the other way. Central first, then out west to Parramatta, Blacktown and
  Penrith.
- **Platform 5** is the T2 and the T3. Town Hall and Central first. Then Strathfield, Granville,
  Leppington or Liverpool.
- **Platform 6** is the T8, and it turns towards Circular Quay first.

That last one is the trap. At 8.01 on a weekday morning two trains leave Wynyard in the same
minute.

The one from platform 5 is at Town Hall at 8.04am and Central at 8.07am.

The one from platform 6 calls at Circular Quay at 8.03am, St James at 8.06am and Museum at 8.08am.
It reaches Central at 8.11am.

Both end up at Central. One of them sees half the city first.

## How do you get home after the trains stop?

The last train out is the 12.43am from platform 6. The next one is the 4.31am from platform 4.
Those four hours belong to the buses.

- The **BN1** to Mona Vale goes from Stand B on Carrington Street at 1.15am, then every half hour.
- The **N91** to Macquarie Centre goes from Stand R on Clarence Street at 1.15am, 2.15am, 3.15am
  and 4.15am.
- The **N92** to Tallawong goes from the same stand at 1.44am, 2.44am and 3.44am.
- The **N90** to Hornsby follows it a minute later, at 1.45am, 2.45am and 3.45am.
- The **324** to Watsons Bay goes from Stand G on York Street at 1.11am, 2.11am, 3.11am and 4.11am.

Hourly, most of them. Miss one and the wait is a long one, so check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk up.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** are on the concourse, inside the paid area, open during service hours.
- **Lifts** run from the concourse to platforms 3 and 4, and to platforms 5 and 6.
- **Taxi ranks** are on Carrington Street and on Pitt Street.
- **Ferries** leave from the Barangaroo wharves at the bottom of the Wynyard Walk. The F3, the F4
  and the F10.
- **The metro** at Barangaroo runs to Tallawong one way and Bankstown the other.
- **Light rail** is the L2 and the L3, out the George Street exit.
- **Phone charging** is free at the station.
- **Peak times** here run 6.30am to 10am and 3pm to 7pm, Monday to Thursday.

Five streets of bus stands and two sides of the concourse. Check the letter, then the number, then
go up the right stairs.
