Seven Hills to Central, the same two stops, typed out again every morning. Save the trip once and
tomorrow you open KRAIL, tap it, and the next services are already on your screen.

::: shot
![A KRAIL timetable from Seven Hills Station to Central Station with live departures, and an unfilled star in the top right](/images/krail/save-your-trip-once-open-it-in-one-tap/01-timetable-star-unsaved.png "The star sits at the top of every trip.")

## Tap the star

Search your trip the normal way. When the times come up, tap the **star** at the top. That is it, the
trip is saved.
:::

Until you have saved your first trip, KRAIL even offers: a little tile pops up asking **Save this trip?** with the
line "Your trip will be one tap away on the home screen." Tap **Save** and you are done.

::: shot flip
![The KRAIL home screen showing two saved trips above a Park and Ride section](/images/krail/save-your-trip-once-open-it-in-one-tap/03-home-saved-trips-card.png "Your trips, first thing on the home screen.")

## Your trips, right on the home screen

Every trip you save shows up under **Saved Trips** when you open the app. Tap one and you jump straight
to the live times for that trip. No searching, no typing, no scrolling.
:::

Want your morning trip at the top? Long press any saved trip and the cards start to wiggle. Drag them
into the order you like, tap the cross on any you no longer need, then tap **Done**. The order stays put.

## Call your stops Home and Work

Stop names can be a mouthful. With labels, "Parramatta Station" can simply be **Work**.

KRAIL comes with **Home** and **Work** ready to go. Label both stops of a trip and your saved trip card reads "Home to Work" instead of two long station names. Make your own labels too, like Gym or
Mum's place, and rename or remove your own labels whenever you like.

## Good to know

- Save as many trips as you want. There is no limit.
- Everything is saved on your phone. There is no account and nothing to sign in to.
- Tap into a saved trip to see live times. The card on the home screen is your shortcut, the live
  times are one tap behind it.
- Save a trip from a station with a Park & Ride and that station's live parking turns up on your home
  screen too, without you adding it.
