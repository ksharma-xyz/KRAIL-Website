You are on Darling Street in Balmain, you can see a stop across the road, and you have no idea what
it is called. In KRAIL you do not need the name. Open the map, tap the stop, and use it.

::: shot
![The KRAIL map over the Sydney central business district with stop pins coloured by transport mode](/images/krail/pick-your-stop-on-the-map/02-map-sydney-cbd-stops.jpg "Every nearby stop, coloured by how it gets you there.")

## Tap Select on map

Tap **Starting from** or **Destination** like you normally would, then tap **Select on map**. A map
opens with stops dotted all over it.
:::

The first time, KRAIL shows you **Map Options**, which is where you set up what the map shows. You
can choose:

- how far to look, 1 km, 3 km or 5 km
- which stops to show: trains, metro, buses, light rail, ferries and coaches

Only want ferry wharves? Switch the rest off, tap **Save**, and the map gets a lot less
busy.

::: shot flip
![A tapped pin showing Martin Place Station, Elizabeth Street, Stand C, with its stop ID and a Select Stop button](/images/krail/pick-your-stop-on-the-map/03-stop-sheet-select-stop.jpg "Tap a pin and it tells you the name you could not read from the street.")

## Tap a stop and see what is leaving

Tap any stop and a card slides up with its name. Tap **Departure Board** on it and you can see what is
coming before you even pick it. Happy with it? Tap **Select Stop** and it goes straight into your
search.
:::

## Show where you are

Tap to share your location and the map jumps to you. KRAIL only asks for your location when you tap
to share it, never on its own. Say no and the map still works, it just starts in the city.

## Good to know

- The stops are stored on your phone, so the dots show up fast. The map itself needs a data connection
  to draw the streets.
- This is handy for places you have never been: a mate's new suburb, a stadium, a beach you are
  catching a bus to for the first time.
