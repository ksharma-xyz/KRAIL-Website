A full afternoon of racing, and the whole thing is done by six. Rosehill Gardens opens its gates at
11.30am on Saturday 28 November. First race 12.35pm, last race 5.55pm.

Everyone else will be sitting on James Ruse Drive. That part is on us. A tram stops across the road
from the track, and a bus pulls up on James Ruse Drive itself.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Saturday 28 November**, gates **11.30am**
Racing: first race **12.35pm**, last race **5.55pm**
Getting there: the **L4** tram to Rosehill Gardens, or route **920** from Parramatta
Coming home: the L4 runs about **5am to 1am**, daily
Parking: free in P4, P5 and the P6 infield
:::

## How do you get to Rosehill Gardens without a car?

1. Take a train to Parramatta. The T1, T2 and T5 all stop there.
2. Leave by the Parramatta Square exit. The light rail stop is there.
3. Take the L4 towards Carlingford.
4. Get off at Rosehill Gardens, two stops along.
5. Walk down Grand Avenue and through the underpass to the gates.

The 11.23am tram from Parramatta Square is at Rosehill Gardens at 11.29am. From the tram it is about
a 12 minute walk to the gates.

## Is the bus quicker than the tram?

Route 920 gets you closer, which is the part that matters when you are already late for the first
race.

It leaves Parramatta Interchange from Stand A2. It stops on James Ruse Drive, opposite Prospect
Street, and the walk from there is about 10 minutes.

The 11.14am bus is at the Rosehill Gardens stop at 11.19am. Weekend buses run about every 20
minutes.

So take whichever one is leaving when you get to Parramatta. If you are not sure which stand is
yours, you can [find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Can you come up the river instead?

You can, and if you have the morning spare, take the slow way in.

The F3 ferry runs to Parramatta Wharf. From the wharf, walk the riverside and Argus Lane to the
Robin Thomas light rail stop. Take the L4 from there.

Two things to know before you plan around it. The F3 is a popular run and can reach capacity. And at
low tide, buses replace the ferry between Parramatta and Rydalmere.

## Are the trains running normally that Saturday?

Not everywhere. Weekend trackwork lands on the same day.

The T1 and T4 are affected. So are the T5, T8 and T9. The advice is still to take a train to
Parramatta or Westmead and change there.

So check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) the night before, and again
before you leave. A timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually happening.

## The practical stuff

- **Gates** open at 11.30am. First race 12.35pm. Last race 5.55pm.
- **Tram stop**: Rosehill Gardens, on the L4, across from the racecourse.
- **Bus stop**: James Ruse Drive, opposite Prospect Street.
- **Walking in** from the tram, it is Grand Avenue and then the underpass.
- **Parking** is free and unreserved in P4 and P5. They are off James Ruse Drive and Prospect
  Street. The P6 infield is through Gate 2 on Unwin Street.
- **Taxis** drop off at the Grand Avenue gates. They pick up on Oak Street, over the Equine Bridge.
- **Trams share the road** around the racecourse, so cross at the crossings.
- **Accessible trips** show with a wheelchair icon when you plan one.
- **Tap on and off** the way you would on any other trip.

## How do you get home?

The worry that puts people in a car is being stuck at a tram stop in the dark after everyone leaves
at once.

This one finishes in daylight. The last race goes at 5.55pm.

The 6.13pm tram from Rosehill Gardens is at Parramatta Square at 6.19pm, and at Parramatta Station
four minutes later.

Stay on for a drink if you like. The L4 runs from about 5am to 1am, every day. The 11.43pm tram from
Rosehill Gardens is at Parramatta Square at 11.49pm.

Either way, you get to spend the afternoon watching the races instead of watching the traffic.
