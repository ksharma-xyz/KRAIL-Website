Five and a half thousand students on one stage. A massed choir, a full orchestra and a stage band.
Another 500 are performing outside before the doors open.

Four shows across two days, and the Friday one starts at 11 in the morning. Getting everyone to
Olympic Park by then is the hard part. That part is on us.

Your last leg is a shuttle train from Lidcombe, and it goes from Platform Zero. Look for the zero.

::: facts The shows, in short
When: **Friday 27 and Saturday 28 November**, Afterpay Arena
Friday: shows at **11am** and **7pm**
Saturday: shows at **1pm** and **7pm**
Doors: **60 minutes** before each show, which runs about two and a half hours
Station: **Olympic Park**, a short walk from the arena
The change: Lidcombe **Platform Zero**, every **10 minutes** until late
:::

::: callout Do this first
Check which of the four shows your ticket is for. Friday at 11am and Friday at 7pm are two
completely different trips.
:::

## Which show are you in?

There are four of them. Friday has 11am and 7pm. Saturday has 1pm and 7pm.

Doors open 60 minutes before each one. So the Friday morning doors go at 10am, which puts your
family on a train in the middle of the peak.

Each show runs about two and a half hours. The venue is Afterpay Arena, which you may still know as
Qudos Bank Arena.

## How do you get to the arena by train?

Take any train to Lidcombe. At Lidcombe, head for Platform Zero.

The direct train to Olympic Park goes from there every 10 minutes until late. It brings you in on
platform 2 or platform 3.

The arena is a short walk from the station. Give yourself a few minutes at Lidcombe to find the
right platform.

## Is there a direct train for the evening shows?

Two extra trains run in every night of the show, and both go straight through.

- **Blacktown at 5.34pm.** It calls at Parramatta and Lidcombe, then Olympic Park.
- **Liverpool at 5.45pm.** It calls at Cabramatta and Fairfield. Then Granville, Lidcombe and Olympic Park.

Neither one asks you to change at Lidcombe. Stay on the train.

## Coming from the north shore on Saturday?

Check this before you leave the house. On Saturday, buses replace trains between Hornsby and Milsons
Point.

Those replacement buses also call at Crows Nest and Victoria Cross.

Take the metro between Chatswood and the city instead. Or take the ferry from Milsons Point to
Circular Quay. Then change at Central for a train to Lidcombe.

## What is the plan for getting home?

The shuttle back to Lidcombe runs every 10 minutes until late. Light rail runs until 1am.

Friday night is the one to check. From 11pm, trains run one direction only out of Central, and the
first stop is Museum. Light rail still runs both ways between Circular Quay and Central.

Also from 11pm on the Friday, buses replace trains between North Sydney and Milsons Point.

Heading up the mountains after the Friday evening show? It does not work. The last train to Katoomba
leaves Central at 8.09pm and gets in at 10.08pm. Your show is still running at that point. From
9.40pm it is buses between Blacktown and Lithgow.

On the Saturday night the Hunter is the one to watch. From 10pm, buses replace trains from Newcastle
Interchange to Telarah.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk back to the
platform. A published timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## The practical stuff

- **Bus 526** runs between Strathfield and Rhodes Shopping Centre, through the precinct and the wharf.
- **Bus 525** runs between Parramatta and Lidcombe. Change there for the Olympic Park train.
- **Bus 533** runs between Olympic Park and Chatswood via Rhodes and North Ryde.
- **Metro**: the M1 runs between Tallawong and Bankstown. Change at Epping, Central or Bankstown for a train towards Lidcombe.
- **Light rail** runs from 5am to 1am daily. Change at Central for the train to Lidcombe.
- **On the L4**, get off at Parramatta Square and walk to Parramatta station.
- **Ferry**: the closest wharf is Sydney Olympic Park Wharf. It is 3.6km from the venue, about a 40 minute walk, or take the 526.
- **F3 services** are popular and can reach capacity. At low tide they can be buses between Parramatta and Rydalmere.
- **Wheelchair access** at Lidcombe Platform Zero and at both Olympic Park platforms.
- **Taxis** rank on Herb Elliott Avenue near the Pullman Hotel.
- **Roads** inside the precinct can be closed, so pick a rideshare point away from the arena.
- **Parking** is pre-booked online only, at a flat $40. Booking closes at 11.59pm the night before.

Get there early and stay after. SpecFest runs outside before and after each show, with another 500
students performing out there and food to go round.

Plan on more than the two and a half hours inside.
