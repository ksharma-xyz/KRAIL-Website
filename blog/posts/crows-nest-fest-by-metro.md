Crows Nest Fest is on Sunday 18 October, 10am to 5pm, on Willoughby Road. The festival sits
directly at the Clarke St exit of Crows Nest metro station. Ride there and walk straight out.

The thing that puts people in a car is parking. Willoughby Road closes at 12.01am that day and
stays shut until 11pm. So does the piece of Clarke Street outside the station. Over 40,000 people
are expected, and the car parks that stay open fill early.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Sunday 18 October**, 10am to 5pm
Where: **Willoughby Road**, Crows Nest
Station: **Crows Nest**, out the Clarke St exit
From Central: **10 minutes**, from platform 26
Last one home: **10.59pm** to the city, 11.11pm to Chatswood
:::

## Which station, and which exit?

Crows Nest, and the Clarke St exit. The event is at that exit, so there is nothing to work out.

Trains do not stop at Crows Nest. The closest train station is St Leonards, and that is a short
walk to Willoughby Road as well.

Crows Nest has two metro platforms, and they matter more on the way home than on the way in.

- **Platform 1**: towards the city and Sydenham.
- **Platform 2**: towards Chatswood and Tallawong.

So you arrive on platform 2 if you came up from the city. You arrive on platform 1 if you came
down from the north.

## How do you get there?

The metro runs every 10 minutes all day on the Sunday. There is no timetable to learn.

- **From Central**, go to platform 26. The 10am is at Crows Nest at 10.10am.
- **From Town Hall**, there is no metro. Walk to Gadigal, platform 2. The 9.52am is at Crows Nest at 10am.
- **From Chatswood**, platform 2. The 11.05am is at Crows Nest at 11.09am, four minutes later.
- **From Tallawong**, platform 2. The 9.08am is at Crows Nest at 9.49am.
- **From Epping**, platform 5. The 9.42am is at Crows Nest at 9.59am.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk out the door. A
timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually happening.

## Coming from the west or the south west?

Trackwork is on that Sunday, and it lands on the lines through Parramatta.

- **T1**: buses replace trains between Parramatta and Lidcombe.
- **T2**: buses replace trains between Cabramatta and Parramatta.
- **T5**: buses replace trains between Parramatta and Cabramatta.
- **T3 and T6**: changed timetables and stopping patterns.

Give yourself an extra half hour if you are starting west of Lidcombe. The metro itself runs
normally all day.

## Getting home

The festival finishes at 5pm, and everybody leaves at once. Platform 1 is the one for the city.
The 5.09pm is at Central at 5.20pm.

For Chatswood, Macquarie Park and Tallawong, go to platform 2 instead.

The last metro to the city leaves Crows Nest at 10.59pm and is at Central at 11.10pm. The last one
north leaves at 11.11pm. That one is at Chatswood at 11.14pm and Tallawong at 11.52pm.

Miss both and you are still fine. Route 144 leaves the Pacific Highway opposite the station at
11.25pm and gets to Chatswood at 11.38pm. St Leonards is a short walk, and the 11.41pm train from
there is at Central at 12.02am.

## The practical stuff

- **Roads close** from 12.01am to 11pm, Willoughby Road between Burlington and Albany streets among them.
- **Clarke Street** is shut between Willoughby Road and Oxley Street, which is the stretch outside the metro exit.
- **Buses 115 and 263** divert around Willoughby Road and use temporary stops for the same hours.
- **Car parks** on Holtermann, Hume, Nicholson and Alexander streets open 7am to 11pm. A fee may apply.
- **Paying for the trip**: tap on and tap off with a card or your phone.
- **Accessible trips** carry a wheelchair icon in the trip planner.
- **Arriving by ferry**: get off at Milsons Point or McMahons Point and take a bus from there.

Over 200 stalls and four stages, spread down a street that is closed to cars all day. The exit
you want puts you at it. Take the metro.
