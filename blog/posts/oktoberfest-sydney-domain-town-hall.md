Oktoberfest in the Garden is at the Domain on Saturday 31 October. It runs from 1pm to 9pm.
Coming from the west, no train takes you straight to St James that day. Get off at Town Hall and
walk.

Eight hours is a long day out. You do not have to stay for all of it.

::: facts The trip, in short
When: **Saturday 31 October**, 1pm to 9pm
Where: The Domain, **Art Gallery Road**
Closest stations: **Martin Place, St James, Town Hall**
From the west: stay on to **Town Hall**
On the metro: get off at **Gadigal or Martin Place**
:::

::: callout Do this first
If you are coming from the west, stop planning around St James. Plan around Town Hall.
:::

## Why not just go to St James?

St James is one of the three closest stations, so it is the obvious one to aim for. On this
Saturday it is also the one that catches people out.

The inner west trains are not running that day. Coming from the west you have to change trains to
reach Museum, St James or Circular Quay.

There are two ways around that. Stay on to Town Hall and walk. Or change at Central.

## How do you get there from the west?

Take the train towards the city and stay on to Town Hall. The 12.06pm from Parramatta platform 3 is
at Town Hall at 12.55pm, on platform 3. Walk from there.

Want St James anyway? Change at Central. Look for the loop service that runs via Museum. The
12.56pm from Central platform 21 is at St James at 1pm.

That is four minutes once you are on it. The change at Central is the slow part.

## Coming in on the metro

Get off at Gadigal or Martin Place. Both are a short walk from the Domain.

On a Saturday the metro comes every 10 minutes. The noon one from Central platform 26 is at Martin
Place at 12.03pm.

## Light rail, bus and ferry

- **L2 and L3**: the closest stops are QVB and Town Hall.
- **L1**: change at Central for the metro, or for an L2 or L3.
- **L4**: get off at Parramatta Square, then take a train to Town Hall or St James.
- **Light rail hours**: 5am to 1am, every day.
- **Buses**: the closest stands are Town Hall QVB and Park Street, Martin Place, St James and Hyde
  Park.
- **Ferries**: Circular Quay is within walking distance. Weekend ferries fill up.

## Getting home

The event finishes at 9pm. Nothing is closing for hours after that.

These are the last ones out of Town Hall on the night.

- **Platform 2**, west: 1.07am to Penrith via Parramatta, then 1.14am to Leppington.
- **Platform 3**, north: 1.18am to Hornsby via Gordon.
- **Platform 4**, south: 1.29am to Cronulla via Banksia.
- **Platform 5**, east: 1.18am to Bondi Junction.
- **Platform 6**, south through the airport: 12.55am to Macarthur.

From Martin Place the last metro to Tallawong leaves platform 4 at 1.34am. The last one to Sydenham
leaves platform 3 at 1.37am.

Still out after the trains stop? Night buses leave Town Hall from Park Street, Stand H.

Trackwork moves times around. Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/)
before you walk to the platform.

## Driving

Parking near the Domain is limited and fills early. Fees usually apply.

Rideshare and taxis drop off on Hospital Road at Art Gallery Road. For a pick-up afterwards, head
towards Elizabeth Street or Sir John Young Crescent.

Some roads around the Domain may close for crowd safety.
