You grab your keys, drive to the station, and before you even turn in you already know there is a
space waiting for you. That is what the Park & Ride card in KRAIL is for, and it is a quick check.

Open KRAIL and the Park & Ride section is sitting right there on your home screen. Tap your station
and you see how many spots are free, how full it is, and when those numbers were last updated.

::: shot
![The Tallawong Station card opened to show three car parks, P1 with 16 spots available and 86 percent full, P2 with 279 spots and 38 percent full, P3 with 348 spots and 12 percent full, each with the time it was last updated](/images/krail/check-park-and-ride-spaces-before-you-leave/03-card-expanded-live-spaces.png "Three car parks, each with its own count and its own update time.")

## Open the card and see the spaces

Each station card opens up to show every car park it has. Tallawong, for example, has more than one,
so you see them one under the other and can pick the one with room. Each car park tells you three things:

- how many spots are available right now
- how full it is, as a percentage
- the time the count was last updated
:::

::: highlight
Open the card to get the latest count. The **Last updated at** time on it tells you exactly how fresh
the numbers are.
:::

::: shot flip
![The Add Park and Ride screen with a search box and an alphabetical list of stations, each with a plus button](/images/krail/check-park-and-ride-spaces-before-you-leave/01-picker-list.png "The picker, A to Z. Campbelltown carries two car parks of its own.")

## Add your station in a few taps

Tap **Add Park & Ride** (it says **Add another** once you have one) and you get a list of stations from A to Z. Type in the search box if you
already know the name, then tap a station to add it. You will feel a little buzz and see a tick.
:::

::: callout Pro tip
Save a trip that starts or ends at a station with a Park & Ride and KRAIL adds that station's card
for you. You never have to go looking for it.
:::

## Good to know

- The live counts cover the commuter car parks that report their numbers to Transport for NSW, like
  Tallawong, Kellyville, Bella Vista, Schofields and Hornsby. Street parking and private car parks
  are not in there.
- Check it before you leave home, not when you are already in the queue at the boom gate.
- Your cards live on your phone. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.
