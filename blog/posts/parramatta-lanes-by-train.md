Parramatta Lanes runs for four nights, Wednesday 21 to Saturday 24 October. The closest station is
Parramatta. Walk out the Parramatta Square exit and you are at it.

Up to 200,000 people are expected across the four nights. The roads through the middle of the
festival close from 1pm each day, so the car is the hard way to do this.

There is a second thing, and it is the one that catches people. **The tram you would normally
catch in from Westmead does not run while the festival is on.**

::: facts The nights, in short
When: **Wednesday 21 to Saturday 24 October**, 5pm to 10pm, and to 10.30pm on Friday and Saturday
Station: **Parramatta**, out the Parramatta Square exit
Light rail: **no trams** between Westmead and Parramatta Square during festival hours
Last ferry back to the city: **4.38pm** on the weeknights, 6.38pm on the Saturday
Entry: free, across more than 20 sites in the Parramatta CBD
:::

## How do you get there?

Take a train to Parramatta. Then take the Parramatta Square exit.

- **From Central on the Wednesday**, the 5.16pm from platform 18 is at Parramatta at 5.42pm.
- **From Central on the Saturday**, the 5.11pm from platform 6 is at Parramatta at 5.44pm.

Parramatta has four platforms, and they do not hold the same direction all evening. Read the
indicator board rather than going from memory. You can also
[check the live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave the house.

Buses come into Parramatta Interchange, which is a short walk from the lanes.

## Coming from Westmead or the hospital?

Read this bit twice, because it is the one nobody checks.

From 5pm each night the trams stop running between Westmead and Parramatta Square. Buses replace
them. Those buses do not stop at Ngara or Westmead Hospital. Use Benaud Oval or Westmead Station
instead.

There is a simpler answer. Westmead is a train station too, one stop from Parramatta. The 6.09pm
from Westmead is at Parramatta at 6.13pm. Four minutes, and no replacement bus to find.

Coming from the other direction, nothing changes. Trams run from Carlingford to Parramatta Square
as normal. Get off at Parramatta Square. They go every seven and a half minutes until 7pm, then
every ten.

## What about the ferry?

Ride it in if you like. Do not count on it to get you home.

The last ferry from Parramatta Wharf to Circular Quay leaves at **4.38pm** on the Wednesday,
Thursday and Friday. The festival opens at 5pm. The 5.55pm and the 6.55pm only go as far as
Rydalmere.

On the Saturday the last one to Circular Quay goes at 6.38pm. After that the river is done for the
night and the train is your way back.

If you do arrive by water, walk the riverside and through Argus Lane to the Robin Thomas stop.
Trams from there to Parramatta Square keep running right through the festival.

## What is the last train home?

It depends which night you go, and the difference is large.

**Wednesday and Thursday.** The last train through the city leaves Parramatta platform 1 at
11.53pm, running to Berowra. The 11.56pm from the same platform gets you as far as Central. After
those two, the 12.19am goes no further than Lidcombe.

**Friday.** Trains to Central leave platform 1 at 11.55pm, 12.10am, 12.23am and 12.53am. The catch
is at the other end. From 10.40pm there are no trains between Central and Wynyard. Use the T4, the
metro, the light rail or a local bus to finish the trip.

**Saturday.** Leave a little earlier than you want to. The last train that runs the whole way to
Central goes from platform 1 at 11.21pm. The 11.38pm ends at Olympic Park, and you finish on a
replacement bus. The city loop is shut all night.

Heading west is the easier direction, but the platform moves. On the Saturday night the Penrith
trains go from platform 2 and run past 2am. On the Wednesday the last one leaves at 12.48am.

## The practical stuff

- **Festival sites**: more than 20 of them, in gardens, rooftop car parks and lanes.
- **Road closures** start at 1pm daily, George Street and Phillip Street among them.
- **Accessible travel**: routes 600, 601, 603, 604, 606 and 706 leave from stand A4 at Parramatta Interchange.
- **Paying for the trip**: tap on and tap off with a card or your phone.
- **Taxis**: the rank is on the eastern side of Villiers Street, between Ross Street and Victoria Road.
- **Low tide** can turn the ferry between Parramatta and Rydalmere into a bus.
- **If you must drive**: the Eat Street car park is shut for parking from 20 to 26 October. The Parramatta Station car park on Wentworth Street and the Justice Precinct car park on Hunter Street are the closest.

Four nights, twenty-odd sites, one street festival. Come hungry and give yourself a bit of room on
the way home.
