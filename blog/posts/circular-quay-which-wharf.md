The Manly ferry leaves Circular Quay from wharf 3. From the 8.20pm it leaves from wharf 4 instead.
Upstairs, platform 1 goes to Wynyard and platform 2 goes to St James. The buses are on Alfred St,
and only two of the four stands are for leaving.

The worry here is never the timetable. It is that you have four minutes and five wharves to choose
from. Here is which is which.

::: facts Circular Quay, in short
Manly ferry: **wharf 3** until the 7.50pm, then **wharf 4**
Going west: **wharf 5** for Pyrmont Bay, Cockatoo Island and the river
Going north and east: **wharf 4** for the zoo, Mosman Bay and Watsons Bay
Trains: **platform 1** for Wynyard and Town Hall, **platform 2** for St James and Museum
Buses: stands **A** and **B** on Alfred St. C and D are arrivals only
:::

## Which wharf for the Manly ferry?

Wharf 3, for most of the day. Through the middle of the day it goes at quarter past, half past and
quarter to the hour.

Then it moves. The 7.50pm is the last Manly ferry from wharf 3. From the 8.20pm it leaves from
wharf 4, and that holds every night of the week.

So the wharf you learned on a Saturday afternoon is the wrong one on a Saturday night. This is the
single thing at Circular Quay that catches people who use it often.

The last one of the night goes at 11.50pm from Monday to Thursday. On Friday and Saturday nights it
is 12.20am. On a Sunday it is 11.20pm. All three leave from wharf 4.

## Which wharf for everything else?

Wharf 5 is the western half of the harbour. That is Pyrmont Bay, Cockatoo Island and the river
up to Sydney Olympic Park and Parramatta.

Wharf 4 is the north and the east. Taronga Zoo, Neutral Bay and Mosman Bay go from there. So do
Double Bay and Watsons Bay.

Wharf 2 and wharf 6 are for private ferries and cruises. A fast ferry to Manly runs from wharf 2,
and it is not the F1.

Every wharf has a side A and a side B. The side changes from one service to the next, so take it off
the screen rather than off memory.

## Platform 1 or platform 2?

Both platforms get you to Central. They go the opposite way round to reach it.

Platform 1 calls at Wynyard first, then Town Hall. Platform 2 calls at St James first, then Museum.

On a weekday the 8.02am from platform 1 is at Town Hall at 8.08am and Central at 8.12am. The 8.03am
from platform 2 is at Museum at 8.08am and Central at 8.12am. One minute apart, opposite directions,
the same minute at Central.

So do not choose by the name on the front of the train. Choose by the next station you want.

The T2 and the T3 use platform 1. Trains to the airport leave from platform 2, so take platform 2
for the airport.

## Which bus stand on Alfred St?

There are four stands and only two of them are for leaving.

- **Stand A**: the 304 to Green Square and the 343 to Kingsford.
- **Stand B**: the 333 to North Bondi, the 396 to Maroubra Beach, and the 392N to Matraville
  overnight.
- **Stands C and D**: arrivals only. Nothing leaves from either of them.

If you know the route number and not the stand, you can
[find the stop by its number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) before you walk down.

The 333 from stand B is the one to Bondi, and it
[runs all night](/blog/bondi-to-coogee-without-a-car/).

## Getting home late

The last train out of Circular Quay leaves from platform 2. That is true on every night of the week.

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: platform 1 finishes at 12.22am, platform 2 at 12.45am.
- **Friday nights**: 1.15am and 1.46am.
- **Saturday nights**: 1.03am and 1.31am.
- **Sunday nights**: 12.03am and 1.01am.

If platform 1 has finished for the night, go up to platform 2. It still reaches Central, by way of
St James and Museum.

Trackwork closes the loop through the city on some nights, and then neither platform runs. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are at the station 24 hours.
- **Toilets** are outside the station rather than on the concourse.
- **Lifts** go to both platforms, and each platform has stairs and an escalator too.
- **Light rail** leaves from the Circular Quay light rail stop. The L2 and the L3 both call there.
- **Taxis** rank on Argyle St in The Rocks and on Pitt St.
- **Bikes** can be parked at the quay.
- **Hearing loops** are fitted at the station.
- **On foot** from the quay: the Opera House, The Rocks and the Royal Botanic Garden.

Wharf 3 in the afternoon is wharf 4 by eight. If you carry one thing off this page, carry that.
