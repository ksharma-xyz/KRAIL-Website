Liverpool is a station you can fly out of. A bus from the stands downstairs puts you at the
Western Sydney airport about 45 minutes later, suitcase and all.

There are two of those buses. They leave from the same stand at the same minute. One of them lands
you at the terminal 18 minutes sooner. That part is on us.

::: facts Liverpool station, in short
Airport buses: the **825** and the **860**, both from Stand **C**
Trains to the city: the **T2** and the **T3**, two different ways round
Platforms: **four**, each with its own stairs and lift
Never wait here: Stand **A** and Stand **E** are arrivals only
Night buses: Stand **G**, every night of the week
:::

## Which bus goes to the airport?

Both of them go from Stand C, and both run about every half hour from 5am to 10pm, every day.

The 825 runs via Bonnyrigg. The 860 runs via Leppington. Same terminal, very different trip.

Take a weekday morning. Both leave Liverpool at 10.06am. The 825 is at the airport at 10.52am. The
860 gets there at 11.10am.

So look for the 825 first. Take the 860 if the 825 has just gone and you have time in hand.

First out of Liverpool is the 825 at 5.10am. Last out is the 825 at 10.10pm. The 860 runs 5.09am to
10.09pm.

There is no train to this airport yet, so the bus is the way in.

::: callout Give yourself the slack
Even the quick bus is 46 minutes on paper, and that is before traffic. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave for the station.
:::

## Where is your bus stand?

Seven stands sit beside the station, and two of them will never take you anywhere.

**Stand A and Stand E are arrivals only.** That is where a bus drops you off. Nothing departs from
either one. Walk past them.

Here is the rest of it:

- **Stand B**: Parramatta on route 80, around the clock.
- **Stand C**: the airport, Parramatta, Miller, Carnes Hill.
- **Stand D**: Campbelltown, Casula, Narellan, Ingleburn, Carnes Hill.
- **Stand F**: Fairfield, Cabramatta, Parramatta, Prairiewood, Warwick Farm, Badgerys Creek,
  Bringelly, Rutleigh Park.
- **Stand G**: Holsworthy, Chipping Norton, Fairfield, Burwood, and every night bus.

Three different stands have a bus to Parramatta. Get your route number before you pick a stand, or
[find the stop by the number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) instead.

Off to TAFE? TAFE NSW Liverpool sits right up by Stand F.

## Which train goes to the city?

Two of them do, and they take different ways round.

The T2 comes through from Leppington. It runs via Fairfield and Granville.

The T3 starts here at Liverpool. It goes the long way, through Chester Hill and Regents Park.

On a weekday the 10.18am T3 is at Central at 11.17am. The 10.23am T2 is at Central at 11.20am. Five
minutes later off the platform, three minutes later into town.

**The T5 is not a city train.** It calls at Fairfield and Parramatta like the T2 does, then turns
north for Blacktown and Richmond. The 10.26am is at Parramatta at 10.54am and Blacktown at 11.12am.

Check where the train finishes, not which stops it makes.

## The practical stuff

- **Platforms** each have their own way up. One lot of stairs and a lift for platform 1, another for
  platforms 2 and 3, a third for platform 4. Read the sign before you climb.
- **Street entry** is on Bigge St.
- **Down to the buses**: a lift and an escalator from the concourse reach stands A, B, C, F and G.
  Stand D is further along the same row, past Stand C.
- **Bikes**: lockers and racks below the concourse.
- **Toilets** on the concourse, including an accessible one.
- **Taxis and pick-ups** are on the far side of the station from the stands.

## Getting home late

Here is the last train out of Central back to Liverpool.

- **Monday to Thursday**: 12.24am, at Liverpool at 1.24am.
- **Friday**: 1.24am, at Liverpool at 2.24am.
- **Saturday night**: 1.13am, at Liverpool at 2.07am.
- **Sunday night**: 12.13am, at Liverpool at 1.07am.

Miss it and the night buses pick you up. The N50 leaves Town Hall at 12.04am and then every hour
until 4.04am. It reaches Liverpool at 1.20am, and hourly after that to 5.20am.

On weeknights the N30 fills the gaps, leaving Town Hall every half hour from 12.30am. It takes about
48 minutes.

Going the other way, the N50 leaves Liverpool from Stand G at 11.38pm, then hourly until 3.38am.

Coming home from Parramatta instead? Route 80 runs all night, every night, once an hour.

Get your stand letter before you head down the escalator, and Liverpool is an easy station. Get it
wrong and you are at Stand A, watching buses arrive.
