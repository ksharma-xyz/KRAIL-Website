Welcome to Liverpool! This is where your trip to the new Western Sydney Airport begins. The airport
opened on Sunday 25 October, and your bus leaves from a stand just downstairs. Bring the suitcase!

Two buses make the trip, and on a weekday morning they leave from the same stand at the same minute.
One of them gets you to the terminal 18 minutes sooner, and I'll show you which.

::: facts Liverpool station, in short
Off to the airport? Hop on the **825** or the **860**, both from Stand **C**.
Heading to the city? The **T2** and the **T3** both go, each their own way round.
Every platform has a lift as well as stairs, so the suitcase is no trouble.
Out late? The **N50** leaves from Stand **G**, every night.
:::

## Which bus goes to the airport?

Head to Stand C, because both airport buses leave from there, every 30 minutes, every day. The first
one goes just after 5am and the last just after 10pm, so you can fly early or late.

The 825 goes via Bonnyrigg and the 860 goes via Leppington. It's the same airport with two different
ways to get there!

Picture a weekday morning, with both buses leaving Liverpool at 10.06am. The 825 has you at the
airport by 10.52am, and the 860 gets you there at 11.10am.

Hop on the 825 and you'll be there first. The 860 gets you there too, just a little later.

For now there's no direct train, so the bus is your way in, and it's a lovely easy one.

::: callout Leave yourself some time
The quicker bus is scheduled for 46 minutes, so set off with a little time to spare. Pop open the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave for the station and you'll
know exactly when yours is due.
:::

## Where is your bus stand?

Seven stands sit beside the station, labelled A to G, and they're easy once you know your letter.

**Stand A** and **Stand E** are where buses drop you off, so your bus leaves from one of the others.
Give them a friendly wave as you walk past!

Here's where everything else goes:

- **Stand B** is where you catch route 80 to Parramatta, and it runs around the clock.
- **Stand C** is the airport stand! It also has buses to Parramatta, Miller and Carnes Hill.
- **Stand D** is yours if you're off to Campbelltown, Casula, Narellan, Ingleburn or Carnes Hill.
- **Stand F** is the one for Fairfield, Cabramatta, Parramatta and Prairiewood. Warwick Farm,
  Badgerys Creek, Bringelly and Rutleigh Park buses leave from here too.
- **Stand G** is the one for Holsworthy, Chipping Norton, Fairfield and Burwood. The night buses
  leave from here too.

Off to Parramatta? Buses go from three different stands, so have your route number handy. Or
[find the stop by the number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) and let that do the work.

Off to TAFE? TAFE NSW Liverpool is right beside Stand F, so you're nearly there.

## Which train goes to the city?

Two trains head for the city, and each takes its own way round.

The T2 comes in from Leppington and runs via Fairfield and Granville.

The T3 starts right here at Liverpool and goes through Chester Hill and Regents Park.

Try a weekday morning. The 10.18am T3 gets you to Central at 11.17am, and the 10.23am T2 gets you
there at 11.20am. They land you in town just three minutes apart, so hop on whichever one is waiting!

The T5 is your train if you're off to Parramatta, Blacktown or Richmond, because it stays out of the
city. It calls at Fairfield and Parramatta like the T2 does, then carries on to Blacktown and
Richmond. The 10.26am gets you to Parramatta at 10.54am and Blacktown at 11.12am.

Glance at where your train finishes before you hop on, and you're all set.

## What's handy to know at the station?

- **Coming in from the street?** The entry is on Bigge St.
- **Which way to your platform?** The four platforms all have stairs and a lift, and the signs will
  point you to yours.
- **Heading down to the buses?** Take the lift or the escalator from the concourse to Stands A, B, C,
  F and G.
- **Got a bike with you?** There are lockers and racks below the concourse.
- **Need a loo stop?** The toilets are on the concourse, and that includes an accessible one.
- **Need a taxi?** The rank and the pick-up spot are on the far side of the station from the stands.

## How do you get home late?

Out for a big night in the city? Lovely! Here's the last train from Central back to Liverpool.

- **Monday to Thursday**, it leaves at 12.24am and gets to Liverpool at 1.24am.
- **Friday**, it leaves at 1.24am and gets to Liverpool at 2.24am.
- **Saturday night**, it leaves at 1.13am and gets to Liverpool at 2.07am.
- **Sunday night**, it leaves at 12.13am and gets to Liverpool at 1.07am.

Friday gets the latest one of the week, so that's the night to go big!

Missed it? The night buses have you covered. The N50 leaves Town Hall at 12.04am and then every hour
until 4.04am, and the first one gets you to Liverpool at 1.20am. The last one gets in at 5.20am.

From Monday to Friday, the N30 chips in as well. It leaves Town Hall every 30 minutes from 12.30am,
and the ride takes 48 minutes.

Going the other way, the N50 leaves Liverpool from Stand G at 11.38pm, then every hour until 3.38am.

Coming home from Parramatta instead? Route 80 runs all night, every night, once an hour.

Know your stand letter before you ride the escalator down, and Liverpool is a breeze. Have a
wonderful trip!
