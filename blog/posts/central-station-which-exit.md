Central is three stations sharing one roof. The trains, the metro and the light rail all leave from
different parts of it. The exit number on the sign is how you tell them apart.

::: facts Central, in short
Suburban trains: platforms **16 to 25**
Intercity and regional: platforms **1 to 14**, off the Grand Concourse
Metro: platforms **26 and 27**
Light rail: **L1** at the Grand Concourse, **L2 and L3** at Chalmers Street
Staffed: **24 hours**, every day
:::

## Which platform do you want?

Platforms 16 to 25 are the suburban ones. The T1, T2 and T3 go from there. So do the T4, T8
and T9.

Your platform is not fixed. On a Thursday morning the T8 used platform 17, platform 22 and platform
23 inside ten minutes. Read the screens every time.

Platforms 1 to 14 sit at the other end, off the Grand Concourse. Intercity and regional trains leave
from there.

The metro is down at the Chalmers Street end, on its own two platforms.

- **Platform 26**: the metro towards Tallawong.
- **Platform 27**: the metro towards Sydenham.

A metro comes every 4 to 10 minutes. There is nothing to look up.

## Which exit?

The exits are numbered and the numbers are on the signs. What each number is for is not.

- **Chinatown, Haymarket or the Capitol Theatre**: Exit 1 or Exit 2.
- **Surry Hills**: Exit 3 or Exit 6.
- **Railway Square, UTS or Paddy's Markets**: Exit 1 or Exit 7.
- **Moore Park**: Exit 5.
- **Prince Alfred Park**: Exit 6.
- **A coach, a taxi or a lift from someone**: Exit 1, out to the Western Forecourt.

Coach bays 1 to 9 are on the Western Forecourt. Bays 15 to 18 are around on Pitt Street. Both are
through Exit 1.

## The light rail leaves from two different places

This is the one that catches people out.

The L1 to Dulwich Hill goes from the Grand Concourse stop. Take Exit 1.

The L2 to Randwick and the L3 to Kingsford go from Chalmers Street. Take Exit 5.

Those two stops sit at opposite ends of the station. Pick the wrong one and you walk the length of
Central with your bag.

## Where does your bus go from?

The stands are lettered, and they are spread across four streets.

- **Stands A to D** are on Eddy Avenue. Take Exit 2.
- **Stands E and F** are on Elizabeth Street. Take Exit 3.
- **Stand G** is on Devonshire Street. Take Exit 6.
- **Stands J to N** are at Railway Square. Take Exit 7.

Get the stand letter before you go up the stairs. If you only know the route number, you can
[find the stop by that](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Getting home late

The station is staffed 24 hours. The metro is not.

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: the last metro to Tallawong goes at 12.30am. The last to Sydenham
  goes at 12.42am.
- **Friday and Saturday nights**: 1.30am and 1.42am.
- **Sunday nights**: 11pm and 11.10pm.

Sunday is the catch. The metro goes to bed before anything else does.

Miss the 11.10pm on a Sunday and you have not missed the night. The 12.30am from platform 16 still
runs to Hornsby. The L1 from the Grand Concourse goes until 1am.

Trackwork moves these times on some nights. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** on the Grand Concourse, and on the lower concourse above platforms 26 and 27. More
  near the Chalmers Street exit and at the Eddy Avenue end.
- **Food and drink** on the Grand Concourse.
- **Luggage lockers** for hire on the Grand Concourse.
- **Regional trains** have a waiting room on platform 1, off the Grand Concourse. The luggage
  check-in and collection office is there too.
- **Lifts and step free paths** are marked with blue signs. Follow those with a pram, a bag or a
  bike.
- **Lost property** at Shop 9 Eddy Avenue, under the station. Open Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm.

Central looks like one station and works like three. Find your exit number first. The rest of it
gets simple after that.
