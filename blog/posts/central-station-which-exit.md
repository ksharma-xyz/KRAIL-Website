Welcome to Central, Sydney's big sandstone front door! Chinatown, Surry Hills and a picnic in Prince Alfred Park are all a short wander from here. Pick the right exit and you're a few minutes from the fun. Here's which one.

## Where are you headed?

Just spot your place below, follow the number on the signs, and enjoy the wander.

- **Chinatown, Haymarket and the Capitol Theatre** are out Exit 1 or Exit 2.
- **Surry Hills** is out Exit 3 or Exit 6.
- **Railway Square, UTS and Paddy's Markets** are out Exit 1 or Exit 7.
- **Moore Park** is out Exit 5.
- **Prince Alfred Park** is out Exit 6, and you walk straight into it. Bring a picnic!
- **Coaches, taxis and pick-ups** are out Exit 1, on the Western Forecourt.

Catching a coach? Bays 1 to 9 are on the Western Forecourt, and bays 15 to 18 are just around on Pitt Street. Exit 1 gets you to both.

## Which light rail stop is yours?

Central has two light rail stops, one at each end of the station, so have a quick look at your line and you're set.

Off to Dulwich Hill? The L1 leaves from the Grand Concourse stop, out Exit 1.

Off to Randwick or Kingsford? The L2 and L3 leave from Chalmers Street, out Exit 5. That's the same exit as Moore Park, so it's easy to remember.

## Where does your bus go from?

Heading for a bus? Find your stand letter and here's the exit that lands you right at it.

- **Stands A to D** are on Eddy Avenue, out Exit 2.
- **Stands E and F** are on Elizabeth Street, out Exit 3.
- **Stand G** is on Devonshire Street, out Exit 6.
- **Stands J to N** are at Railway Square, out Exit 7.

Grab your stand letter before you head up the stairs and you'll walk right to it. Only got a route number? You can [find the stop by that](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) too, which is handy.

## Catching a train?

Heading out to the suburbs? Your train is down on platforms 16 to 25. That's home to the T1, T2, T3, T4, T8 and T9.

Off on a bigger adventure to Newcastle, Kiama or out to Bathurst? The intercity and regional trains leave from platforms 1 to 14, off the Grand Concourse.

Your platform is up on the big screens, so check them and off you go!

The metro is the easy one, down at the Chalmers Street end.

- **Platform 26** has the metro to Tallawong.
- **Platform 27** has the metro to Sydenham.

A metro comes every 4 to 10 minutes, so you can just turn up and hop on.

## Heading home late

Staying out late? Lovely! The metro will get you home, and here are the last ones.

- **From Monday to Thursday**, the last one to Tallawong goes at 12.30am and the last to Sydenham at 12.42am.
- **On Friday and Saturday** you get an extra hour, with the last two at 1.30am and 1.42am. Proper night out!
- **On Sunday** they head home early, at 11pm and 11.10pm.

Missed the 11.10pm on a Sunday? The L1 from the Grand Concourse keeps rolling until 1am, and the screens will show you any late trains from platform 16.

The [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) will show you the next one home, so enjoy your evening.

## Before you go exploring

Pop your bags in a locker on the Grand Concourse and you can wander Chinatown hands-free. The food and drink up there makes a good snack stop too.

- **Need a loo stop?** There are toilets on the Grand Concourse and on the lower concourse above platforms 26 and 27, and more at the Chalmers Street and Eddy Avenue ends.
- **Regional travellers** have a waiting room on platform 1, off the Grand Concourse, and the luggage check-in and collection office is right there too.
- **Lifts and step free paths** have blue signs, which makes life easy with a pram, a big bag or a bike.

If you leave something behind, the friendly folks at Shop 9 on Eddy Avenue, under the station, look after it, weekdays 8am to 4pm.

Have a wonderful day out! Find your exit number and the rest of Central falls into place, I promise.
