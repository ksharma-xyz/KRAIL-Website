Town Hall has five exits, and the street you want decides which one.
Exit 1 for the buses on York St.
Exit 2 for the light rail and every night bus.
Exit 5 for the Sydney Town Hall and Town Hall Square.

Downstairs there are six platforms. Two of them send trains to Hornsby, by two
completely different routes.

::: facts Town Hall, in short
Exits: **five**, numbered 1 to 5
Platforms: **six**, numbered 1 to 6
Buses: York St at exit 1, Park St at exit 2, Druitt St at exit 5
Light rail: exit 2 or exit 4
Night buses: every one of them from **Park St**, at exit 2
:::

## Which exit at Town Hall?

Each one is signposted with a number and a name.

- **Exit 1, QVB**: the QVB, The Galeries, and York St buses at stands A to F.
- **Exit 2, George St**: the light rail stop, and Park St buses at stands G to K.
  The lift to the street is here.
- **Exit 3, Pavilion Plaza**: Pavilion Plaza.
- **Exit 4, Cathedral**: the light rail stop, St Andrew's Cathedral and Darling Harbour.
- **Exit 5, Town Hall**: Sydney Town Hall, Town Hall Square, Darling Harbour, and
  Druitt St buses at stands L and N.

Two of them reach the light rail. Take exit 2 if you want a bus as well.

## Which platform for your train?

- **Platform 1**: the T2, and some weekday T3 services to Liverpool.
- **Platform 2**: the T1 and the T9 heading south through Central and on to the west.
- **Platform 3**: the T1 and the T9 heading north towards Gordon. A few intercity
  services to the Central Coast go from here too.
- **Platform 4**: the T4 towards Cronulla and the Illawarra. A few intercity services
  to the South Coast go from here too.
- **Platform 5**: the T4 through the eastern suburbs to Bondi Junction.
- **Platform 6**: the T3 to Liverpool and Lidcombe, and the T8 to the airport.

Late at night that shifts. After midnight some T2 services to Central leave from
platform 6 instead. Read the indicator, not your habit.

## Two platforms, both saying Hornsby

On a Wednesday night the 11.05pm from platform 2 is a T9 to Hornsby via Central.
Eight minutes later the 11.13pm from platform 3 is a T1 to Hornsby via Gordon.

Same destination, opposite directions out of the station. One heads south through
Central and comes at Hornsby the long way. The other heads north through North Sydney.

Check the word after "via" before you go down the stairs.

## Where does your bus go from?

Three streets, three exits.

- **York St**, at exit 1, stands A to F. The BN1 to Mona Vale, the 100 to Taronga
  Zoo, the 120 to Chatswood.
- **Park St**, at exit 2, stands G to K. The 324 and 325 to Watsons Bay, the 389 to
  Bondi Junction, and every night bus.
- **Druitt St**, at exit 5, stands L and N. The 505 to Woolwich, the 506 to
  Macquarie University, the 507 to Meadowbank.

If all you have is the route number, you can
[find your stop by that number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Where do the night buses go from?

Park St. Every one of them.

From about midnight most trains stop and night buses take over until around 4.30am.
At Town Hall they all leave from Park St, which means exit 2 on George St.
Not Druitt St, and not York St.

- **Stand H**, twelve routes: N10 Sutherland, N11 Cronulla, N20 Riverwood, N30
  Macarthur, N40 East Hills, N50 Liverpool, N60 Fairfield, N61 Carlingford, N70
  Penrith, N71 Richmond, N80 Hornsby via Strathfield, N81 Parramatta.
- **Stand K**, three routes: N90 Hornsby via Chatswood, N91 Macquarie Centre, N92
  Tallawong.

On a Wednesday night the first one out of stand H is the N50 to Liverpool at 12.04am.
After that each route runs about once an hour. The last few go between 4.00am and 4.30am.

Stand K starts later. The first N91 is at 1.10am, and the first N90 and N92 at 1.40am.

The N61 and the N81 only run overnight on Thursday, Friday and Saturday.
The N92 finishes at Tallawong, where the
[Park & Ride](/blog/tallawong-parking-lottery/) is.

## Is there a metro at Town Hall?

No. The nearest metro station is Gadigal. It has two entrances, one on Park St and
one on Bathurst St.

Come up at exit 2 and walk along Park St towards Pitt St. Metro services leave
Gadigal every 4 to 10 minutes through the day, so there is no timetable to learn.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** on the concourse, towards the Bathurst St end.
- **Lift to the street** at exit 2, on George St.
- **Prams and big bags**: take the lifts, not the escalators.
- **Light rail** outside, the L2 and the L3, from exit 2 or exit 4.
- **Tickets** from the single trip ticket machines at the station.

Pick the street before you pick the stairs. Park St for anything after midnight.
York St for the zoo and Chatswood. Druitt St for Woolwich and Macquarie University.

And check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you head
down, because the platform is the wrong place to find out.
