It is 6am at Blacktown, still dark, and your phone screen is blinding you. Switch KRAIL to dark mode
and it is kind on your eyes. While you are in there, pick a colour that feels like yours.

::: shot
![The Pick your favourite colour screen with six colours, each with its own tagline, and a Light, Dark and System switch at the bottom](/images/krail/make-krail-your-colour/01-theme-picker.png "Six colours, and each one brought its own line.")

## Pick your colour

Tap the gear on the home screen, then tap **Change Theme**. You land on **Pick your favourite colour!** and six choices,
each with its own tagline. A few of our favourites:

- "On the track, no lookin' back!"
- "Smooth sail, no fail!"
- "Surf the sub, no cap!"
- "Hoppin' the concrete jungle!"
:::

There is a purple one and a pink one too. Tap any colour and the whole app changes to match, straight
away.

::: shot flip
![The same colour picker in dark, with Dark selected on the Light, Dark and System switch](/images/krail/make-krail-your-colour/02-theme-picker-dark.png "Dark is a setting on this screen, so it does not wait on your phone to decide.")

## Light, dark, or whatever your phone does

At the bottom there is a switch with three options:

- **Light** for bright days
- **Dark** for early starts and late trips home
- **System** to follow whatever your phone is set to
:::

## Good to know

- KRAIL adjusts the colours to keep text readable, in light and in dark.
- Change it as often as you like. It is your app.
