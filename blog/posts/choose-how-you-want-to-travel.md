Some days you want the ferry to Watsons Bay rather than the fastest way there. The view is better and
you are not in a hurry. Tell KRAIL, and it only shows trips on the kinds of transport you want.

::: shot
![The mode row open with every chip filled: Train, Bus, Metro and Light Rail](/images/krail/choose-how-you-want-to-travel/01-mode-sheet-all-on.png "Filled means it is on. The row scrolls, there are more than four.")

## Tap Mode

Search your trip and tap **Mode**. A row of chips appears, one for each kind of transport:

- Train, Metro, Bus, Light Rail, Ferry and Coach
- plus School Bus, and On Demand for the bookable shuttles in some areas
:::

They all start switched on. Tap the ones you do not want to switch them off, then tap **Done**.

::: shot flip
![Results after applying the filter, showing only ferry trips with the whole list tinted ferry green](/images/krail/choose-how-you-want-to-travel/03-results-ferry-only.png "Ferries only, and the list changes colour to say so.")

## Your results, your way

KRAIL takes out every option that uses anything you switched off. So if you turn off Bus (and School Bus), you will not get a trip that sneaks in a bus for the last
two stops.
:::

If nothing is left, KRAIL tells you, with a nudge to tap **Mode** and switch something back on.

## Ideas for trying it

- **Ferry only** on a sunny weekend, just for the ride.
- **No buses** when you would rather stay on the train or the metro.
- **Light rail and train** when you are heading somewhere along the line and want to keep it simple.

## Good to know

- The choice is for the trip you are looking at. Come back later and everything is switched back on.
