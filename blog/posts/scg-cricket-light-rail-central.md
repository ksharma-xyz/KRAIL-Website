Australia versus England at the SCG, and up to forty thousand of us in there for it. First ball at
7.15pm on a Sunday night. All done by 10.25pm.

Then the whole ground pours out onto Anzac Parade at the same moment. That part is on us.

Here is the good bit, before you even leave the house. Your match ticket is your ride. It covers
public transport there and back, right through until 4am.

::: facts The match, in short
When: **Sunday 29 November**, gates open **5.15pm**
First ball: **7.15pm**, end of play **10.25pm**
Stop: **Moore Park** on the L2 or the L3, across from the ground
From Central: light rail from **Chalmers Street**
Your ticket: covers transport both ways, until **4am**
Walk instead: Central to Moore Park, about **25 minutes**
:::

::: callout Do this first
Keep your match ticket somewhere easy to reach. It is your ride home as well, and you show it to
transport staff when you board.
:::

## How do you get to the SCG?

Get yourself to Central by train or metro. Then walk out to the light rail stop on Chalmers Street.
Jump on an L2 or an L3 heading south.

Get off at Moore Park. The stop sits on Anzac Parade, just south of the ground.

Before 7pm a light rail comes along every five minutes. After 7pm on a Sunday it is one every eight.
There are extra services before and after the match on top of that.

Coming by ferry? Ride in to Circular Quay. The L2 and the L3 both start there, so you get on once and
stay on all the way to Moore Park.

Want the whole thing drawn out before you go? [Put the trip on a map](/blog/see-sydney-trip-on-map/).

## Coming from the east?

From Bondi Junction, hop on a 333, a 352 or a 440 to Oxford Street. Route 355 drops you on Lang Road.
Either way it is a short walk in from there.

Routes 339, 373, 374 and 396 all stop along Anzac Parade near the ground.

## Fancy the walk instead?

Central to Moore Park is about 25 minutes on foot. Head along Devonshire Street, then cross the
Albert Tibby Cotter Walkway.

That bridge carries you straight over the traffic on Anzac Parade. You miss the queues at both ends,
which on a forty thousand night is the whole point.

## Check this before you leave the house

There is trackwork on Sunday 29 November, and two pieces of it matter.

On the T1, buses replace trains between Hornsby and Milsons Point. Take the metro between Chatswood
and the city instead. Those replacement buses also call at Crows Nest and Victoria Cross.

On the T8, buses replace trains between Sydenham and Central via the airport. Change at Sydenham for
a bus to the airport stations, or for the metro.

Trains out of Central run one direction only that day, and Museum is the first stop. The light rail
still runs both ways between Circular Quay and Central.

## How do you get home after the last ball?

The L2 and the L3 keep going until 1am. Extra services start as soon as the game ends.

Express shuttle buses run from Moore Park to Central after the match as well. That is more room on
top of the light rail.

The metro runs through to midnight on the Sunday. Your match ticket gets you home until 4am on the
Monday.

Everyone heads for the light rail at once, so expect a queue. Let the crowd thin out a bit, or just
walk the 25 minutes back to Central.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave your seat. A
timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## The practical stuff

- **Your ticket** covers every mode both ways, light rail and ferries included.
- **Keep it safe** for the trip home. It runs out at 4am on the Monday.
- **Airport stations** charge an access fee on top, and your match ticket does not cover it.
- **Taxis** rank on Errol Flynn Boulevard, near the Hordern Pavilion.
- **Rideshare** drop-off is on Errol Flynn Boulevard or Driver Avenue.
- **Pick-up** after the game gets restricted, so choose a spot away from the ground.
- **Driver Avenue** is partly closed outside the ground from 3.15pm.
- **Clearways** run from 3pm to midnight on parts of Lang Road, Moore Park Road and Albion Street.
- **Park & Ride** gives you up to 18 hours of free parking when you finish a public transport trip.
- **On the way out** of the car park, press the intercom at the exit gate and show your match ticket.
- **Spaces** at Park & Ride run down through the day, so [check them first](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).

Get there early. There are plenty of local places to eat and drink around Moore Park before the
gates open at 5.15pm, and plenty still going after the game.

Leave the car at home, take the light rail in, and give it everything from the first ball.
