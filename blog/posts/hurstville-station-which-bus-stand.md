Beach at Cronulla. Shops at Miranda. The city the other way. One station sends you to all three.

The catch is finding your bus. Twelve stands are scattered around the station, on two different
sides of it. Pick the wrong side and yours pulls out somewhere behind you.

That part is on us. Here is the stand, the side it is on, and which train is the quick one.

::: facts Hurstville, in short
Trains: the **T4**, plus intercity services running south
Bus stands: **twelve**, split across two sides of the station
One side: Forest Rd, with Barratt St and Macmahon St behind it
The other side: Ormonde Pde
Night buses: the **N10** and the **N11**, from Stand L
:::

## Which side are you on?

There are three ways into the station. Two of them open onto Forest Rd. One opens onto Ormonde Pde.

Those two roads are the whole trick. Forest Rd runs along one side of the station and Ormonde Pde
runs along the other. Your stand is on one or the other.

Buses leave from nine of the twelve stands. Two take arrivals only. One runs on event days.

## Buses from the Forest Rd side

- **Stand A**, on Forest Rd: 410 Macquarie Park. 452 Rockdale. 455 St George Hospital. 947 Kogarah.
- **Stand B**, on Forest Rd: 450 Strathfield. 940 Bankstown. 941 Chullora Marketplace.
  945 Bankstown. 949 Lugarno. 950 Bankstown. 952 Roseland.
- **Stand C**, on Forest Rd: the 949, 950 and 952 to Westfield Hurstville.
- **Stand D**, on Macmahon St: 490 Drummoyne. 491 Five Dock.
- **Stand E**, on Barratt St: 452 Beverly Hills. 455 Kingsgrove.

Only know the route number? You can [find your stop by it](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/)
instead of reading every sign.

## Buses from the Ormonde Pde side

- **Stand H**: 953 Connells Point.
- **Stand J**: 958 Rockdale Plaza.
- **Stand K**: 959 Bald Face Point. 970 Miranda. 971 Cronulla.
- **Stand L**: 954 Hurstville Grove. 955 Mortdale. The N10 and the N11 at night.

Stand F and Stand G take arrivals only, so nothing leaves from them. Stand M, down on Butler Rd,
runs on event days.

## The two numbers that catch people out

The 452 and the 455 each leave from two different stands here.

The 452 goes to Rockdale from Stand A. The same number goes to Beverly Hills from Stand E, on a
different street.

The 455 goes to St George Hospital from Stand A. The same number goes to Kingsgrove from Stand E.

Read the destination on the front of the bus. The number on its own will not tell you which way you
are about to go.

## Which train is the quick one?

Two trains leave here for the same place four minutes apart and do not get there together.

On a weekday afternoon the 4.17pm from platform 3 runs via Wolli Creek. Seven stops, and at Bondi
Junction at 4.50pm.

The 4.21pm goes via Banksia instead. Fifteen stops, and in at 5.04pm.

Four minutes later out of Hurstville, fourteen minutes later in. The word after "via" is the only
thing on the board that tells them apart.

The platform moves as well. Trains towards the city go from platform 1, 2 or 3, depending on the
service. Trains heading south almost always go from platform 4.

So read the board on the way in. Or check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave the house.

## Getting home late

On a Tuesday night the last train towards the city leaves Hurstville at 12.42am. It is an intercity
service and it runs through to Central. The last T4 before it goes at 12.05am.

Heading the other way, the last train is the 1.25am to Cronulla.

Then the trains stop. Nothing runs towards the city again until 4.11am.

Saturday night is kinder. The last train to the city is the 12.58am and the last T4 is the 12.52am.
The last one south is the 1.59am to Cronulla.

The night buses leave from Stand L on Ormonde Pde. The N10 runs to the city and to Sutherland. The
N11 runs to the city and to Cronulla.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are on duty 24 hours.
- **Toilets** at the station, with an accessible toilet and a baby change table.
- **Lifts** from the concourse, and another one on the Ormonde Pde side.
- **A ramp** at the Forest Rd entry nearest Ormonde Pde.
- **Bike racks** at the station, and a kiss and ride stopping area.
- **Taxi ranks** on both sides, plus one over near Westfield.
- **Free phone charging** at the station.
- **Paying**: tap on with a card or your phone. There are top up and single trip ticket machines.
- **Peak** here runs Monday to Thursday, 6.30am to 10am and 3pm to 7pm.

Hurstville is not a hard station. It is a wide one. Work out your side before you walk out of the
concourse and the rest of it is easy.
