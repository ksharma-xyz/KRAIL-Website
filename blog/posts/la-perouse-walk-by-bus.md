A clifftop path out to a lighthouse, with World War II battlements built into the headland on the
way. Botany Bay on one side. The open ocean on the other.

Bring lunch and your snorkel. Congwong Beach is on the way out, and the snorkelling is around the
headland and Bare Island.

**It is the last stop on the bus route**, which is why people assume they need a car out here. Drive
and the gate beats you home. It is closed to vehicles from 7pm, every day of the year. Your bus is
not.

That part is on us. Here is the stand it goes from, the two tracks, and what time the night buses
run.

::: facts The walk, in short
Getting there: the **390X** from Bondi Junction, 48 minutes end to end
The short one: **Henry Head track**, 4km return, 45 minutes to 1hr 15
The long one: **Cape Banks track**, 7km return, about 3 hours
Grade: **Grade 3** either way, short steep hills and the odd step
Water: **carry your own**, there is little or none in the park
:::

## How do you get there without a car?

1. Take a train to Bondi Junction.
2. Walk to Stand L. The 390X to La Perouse goes from there.
3. Get off at Botany Bay National Park on Anzac Parade. That is the stop at the park entry gates.
4. For Cann Park, the museum and the start of the Cape Banks track, stay on two minutes to the
   terminus.

On Saturday 31 October the 9am from Stand L is at the park gates at 9.46am. It reaches the terminus
at 9.48am. They run every ten minutes that morning.

Coming from the city, take the light rail to Juniors Kingsford instead. The L3 runs there from
Circular Quay, Town Hall and Central. The 390X calls at Stand B beside it. That same 9am bus is at
Juniors Kingsford at 9.26am, twenty minutes short of the park.

## Which track, and how far?

Henry Head walking track is the short one. It is 4km return and takes 45 minutes to an hour and a
quarter. Grade 3, on a formed track with short steep hills and the odd step.

It is signposted the whole way and needs no walking experience. The views run across Congwong Beach
and Botany Bay as you go. At the far end is the Endeavour Lighthouse at Henry Head, with the wartime
battlements beside it.

From the lighthouse you can keep going another 1.2km to Cruwee Cove and the headland at Cape Banks.

The full version is the Cape Banks walking track. That is 7km return and about three hours, and it
starts at the southern end of Cann Park. It goes past Congwong Beach before joining the Henry Head
track. Moderate fitness is recommended. Cape Banks is a whale watching spot.

Two things to watch on the way. The cliffs are unfenced, so stay on the marked track. The path also
cuts through a golf course, so keep to it there and watch for golf balls.

The rock around Cruwee Cove gets slippery. That beach can be impassable at high tide or in a big
swell. Check the tide and the forecast before you set out.

Walk back the way you came. The loop along the public road from Cape Banks is narrow.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** and food outlets at Cann Park, by La Perouse Museum.
- **Water**: little or none in the park. Carry your own, with a hat and sunscreen.
- **Swimming** at Congwong Beach, on the way out.
- **No lifeguards** on the beaches here, and the currents can run strong.
- **The museum** at Cann Park opens on Sundays only.
- **Snorkelling** is around the headland and Bare Island. The Bare Island tour is ticketed.
- **No entry fee** at the La Perouse end of the park.
- **Phone reception** is limited once you are in. Load what you need on the bus.
- **Birds**: kookaburras, lorikeets and yellow-tailed black cockatoos. Water dragons on the track.
- **Park gates** open at 7am. They shut at 8.30pm from November to March, 7.30pm from April to
  October.
- Areas can close for poor weather or fire danger.

## What is the last bus home?

The fear that puts people in a car is being out at the end of the road after dark. Here it is not
real.

The 390X goes all night from the stop at the park gates on Anzac Parade.

- **Every twenty minutes** through the evening. The last three are 11.17pm, 11.37pm and 11.57pm.
- **Hourly after midnight**, at seventeen minutes past, from 12.17am to 4.17am.

A Thursday night and a Saturday night run the same. This stop does not thin out at the weekend.

The gate is the only thing with a closing time. Be off the track before it shuts and the rest of the
evening is yours. A picnic at La Perouse Point as the sun goes down, and a bus whenever you are
done.
