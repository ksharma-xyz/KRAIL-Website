Two changes, a short walk, and a stop you have never heard of. On a map it all makes sense in a second.
Open any trip in KRAIL, tap **Maps**, and see the whole thing laid out in front of you.

::: shot
![An expanded trip card showing the leg by leg breakdown, with Maps and Share buttons](/images/krail/see-your-whole-trip-on-a-map/01-trip-card-expanded-with-maps-button.png "Open a trip and the Maps button is right there.")

## Tap Maps on your trip

Search your trip and tap one of the options to open it up. You will see a **Maps** button. Tap it and
the trip fills the screen.
:::

::: shot flip
![A journey map with a green line leaving Circular Quay across the harbour, labelled with the ferry route](/images/krail/see-your-whole-trip-on-a-map/03-ferry-route-line-harbour.jpg "The line is drawn per leg and coloured by how you are travelling.")

## What the lines mean

- Every train, metro, bus, ferry or light rail part of the trip is drawn as a **solid line in that
  line's own colour**, following the real route it takes.
- The bits where you walk are a **dashed grey line**, so you can see exactly how far you are going on
  foot.
- Your start, your finish and every place you change are marked, and the stops in between are small
  white dots.
:::

## Tap a stop for its times

Tap any stop on the map and you get the time you arrive there or leave from there. Handy when you want to know exactly when you will be
at each stop along the way.

Tap the location button and the map shows where you are, which is great when you have just stepped off
somewhere new and want to know which way to walk.

## Good to know

- On a tablet or a foldable, the map sits right next to your times the whole time, no button needed.
- The map needs a data connection to draw the streets.
