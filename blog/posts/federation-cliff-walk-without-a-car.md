Walk the sandstone clifftops from Dover Heights round to Watsons Bay, then sit down with a beer at
the water and catch a ferry home. It is flat almost the whole way, so the views come cheap.

There is one thing to sort out before you go, and it is the reason this page exists. The ferry home
stops running at quarter past four on a weekday.

::: facts The walk, in short
Getting there: Route **380** to Military Rd at Blake St, every day, about every 20 minutes
Do not take: the **323**, peak weekdays only and nothing at all on a weekend
Coming home: the ferry from Watsons Bay, and on a weekday the last one goes **quarter past four**
Terrain: paved and close to flat almost the whole way
Watch out: a few short unfenced stretches right at the cliff edge, near The Gap in particular
:::

## How do you get there without a car?

Catch the 380 bus and get off at Military Rd at Blake St, Dover Heights. The 380 runs between
Watsons Bay and Bondi Junction by way of Bondi Beach, it goes every day of the week, and there is
one about every 20 minutes, so it is not something you have to plan a morning around.

Coming out of the city, take a train to Bondi Junction and pick the 380 up at the station.

If anybody tells you to catch the 323, do not. The 323 does stop up in Dover Heights, but it runs
Monday to Friday in peak only, five trips each way, and there is no Saturday or Sunday service at
all. On a weekend you would be standing at a bus stop that nothing comes to.

## The walk itself

The path runs along the top of the sandstone cliffs from Dover Heights down to Watsons Bay, past
Diamond Bay Reserve and the Macquarie Lighthouse, and finishes at The Gap. It is paved and close to
flat almost the whole way, which is not what the word cliff suggests, and that is exactly the
appeal.

Neither Waverley nor Woollahra council publishes a distance or a time for it, and between them they
manage the whole path, so we are not going to put a number on it. What we can tell you is that
there is a 380 stop every few hundred metres along Old South Head Rd, so you can cut the walk short
anywhere you like and still get home.

**A few short stretches are unfenced right at the cliff edge**, close to The Gap in particular.
Watch where you put your feet and keep hold of children; this is not a walk to do while reading your
phone. It is exposed the whole way as well, so a strong southerly turns it into a completely
different afternoon.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** at Diamond Bay Reserve and Robertson Park in Watsons Bay.
- **Water** fountains at both.
- **Coffee and a pub** at the finish. The Watsons Bay Hotel and Doyle's are both right by the wharf.
- **Kids**: fine on the paved path, but supervise near the unfenced edges close to The Gap.
- **Prams**: yes, for most of it.
- **Dogs**: on lead for almost the whole route, and banned in Gap Park at the Watsons Bay end.
- Almost no shade anywhere on the clifftop.
- Paved sections handle rain fine. The grassy shortcuts get slick.
- May to November is whale season, and this clifftop is as good a place to watch from as the walk
  itself is to do.

Weekday mornings are quiet. Weekends around The Gap get busy.

## Getting home, and the trap in the timetable

Walk down into Watsons Bay and catch the F9 ferry back to Circular Quay from the wharf. It is 23
minutes across the harbour and it is the best part of the day. On the wrong day, it is not there.

**Monday to Friday** the F9 calls at Watsons Bay from 10.15am to 4.15pm, and that 4.15pm is the last
one. Finish a late afternoon walk on a Tuesday and there is no ferry at all.

**On a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday** it runs from 8.15am to 12.15am, so you can stay for
dinner and still get the boat. The 12.15am from Watsons Bay is at Circular Quay at 12.38am.

Now the trap, because it is one row up in the same table. Rose Bay has F9 sailings until five past
midnight on a weekday. Those services turn around at Rose Bay and never come out to Watsons Bay.
Read the wrong line and you will stand on that wharf at nine o'clock on a Wednesday waiting for a
boat that was never coming.

If you have missed it, catch the 380 back instead. The last one leaves Robertson Park at Watsons Bay
at 8.50pm, every day of the week. After that the 380 starts up the hill at South Head Cemetery on
Old South Head Rd, so you would be walking up out of the bay to reach it.
