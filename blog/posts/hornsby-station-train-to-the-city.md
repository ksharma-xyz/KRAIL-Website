Three stops and you are in the middle of the city. Hornsby to Central in thirty six minutes. The
next two trains to the city both get there more than twenty minutes later.

All three end up in the city. They leave within eight minutes of each other. Two of them go
opposite ways round.

Knowing which one you want is the whole trick at Hornsby. That part is on us.

::: facts A weekday at Hornsby, in short
Fastest to Central: the **10.19am**, three stops, in at **10.55am**
For Wynyard or Town Hall: the **10.27am** the other way, Wynyard at **11.10am**
The one that looks the same: the **10.26am** all stations, Central at **11.19am**
Bus stands: **A to E** on Station Street, and **E is arrivals only**
Staffed: **24 hours**, every day of the week
:::

## Which train is the fast one?

The quick way to Central runs through Epping and Strathfield. It stops nowhere else.

Take the 10.19am. Epping at 10.30am. Strathfield at 10.42am. Central at 10.55am.

On a weekday it goes at 19 and 49 past the hour. That holds from 9.19am right through to 9.49pm.
The morning and afternoon peaks add more.

One thing to know before you get on. This train finishes at Central. It does not carry on to Town
Hall or Wynyard.

## Which one for Wynyard or Town Hall?

Take the train that goes the other way, down through Gordon and Chatswood.

The 10.27am from Hornsby is at Chatswood at 10.51am and North Sydney at 11.04am. Wynyard is
11.10am, Town Hall is 11.14am and Central is 11.18am.

Now compare it with the all stations train through Strathfield. The 10.26am reaches Wynyard at
11.25am. That is fifteen minutes later, off a train that left Hornsby a minute earlier.

## Why do two trains go opposite ways?

Hornsby sits at the top of a loop. One side of it runs down through Epping and Strathfield. The
other side runs down through Gordon and Chatswood. Both sides meet in the city.

So a train leaving Hornsby for the city can turn either way. Both reach Central, Town Hall and
Wynyard. They just reach them in opposite order.

Here is what that is worth in minutes. Gordon is fourteen minutes away on the 10.27am. On the
10.26am it is an hour and twenty eight minutes away, because that train gets there the long way
round through the city.

So check where a train stops before you get on it, rather than the platform you usually use.
Intercity services running through to the Central Coast stop here too.

## Which bus stand at Hornsby?

There are five, lettered A to E, along Station Street.

- **Stand A**: the 575 to Macquarie University and the 591 to St Ives.
- **Stand B**: the 596 to Hornsby Heights. The N80 and N90 night buses to Town Hall also go from
  here.
- **Stand C**: the 587 to Westleigh, the 588 to Normanhurst West, the 589 to Sydney Adventist
  Hospital and the 590 to Pennant Hills.
- **Stand D**: the 592 to Cowan, the 595 to Mount Colah, the 597 to Berowra and the 598 to Asquith.
- **Stand E**: arrivals only. Nothing leaves from it, so do not wait there.

Stand D is the one worth remembering. Three of those four places are also stops on the train north.
So if you have just watched that train pull out, there is a bus.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are on duty 24 hours, every day, weekends and public holidays included.
- **Lift** down from the concourse, and stairs as well.
- **Toilets**, an accessible toilet and a baby change table.
- **Bike** racks, bike lockers and a bike shed.
- **Parking**: a Park & Ride and a commuter car park.
- **Paying**: tap on with a card or your phone. There are top up and single trip ticket machines.
- **Phone** charging at the station, which is the kind of thing you remember at 11pm.
- **Taxi** rank, and a kiss and ride stopping area for a lift home.
- **Accessibility**: the station is listed as independent access, with a hearing loop and platform
  edge tactiles.

## Getting home late

The two sides of the loop stop for the night at different times. The gap is bigger than you would
guess.

The last train to Hornsby leaves Central at 1.47am, every night of the week. It calls at Strathfield
and Epping and nothing else. It is at Hornsby at 2.23am from Sunday to Thursday, and 2.25am on
Friday and Saturday nights.

The way home through Gordon and Chatswood finishes a lot earlier.

- **Monday to Thursday**: the last one leaves Central at 12.25am.
- **Friday**: 1.25am.
- **Saturday**: 1.15am.
- **Sunday**: 12.30am.

So from about half past midnight on a Sunday, the only train home is the one through Strathfield.
Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave the pub, not after.
Trackwork moves all of this on some nights.

Three trains to the city leave Hornsby inside eight minutes. Pick by the stop you are getting off
at, and remember that 19 and 49 past are the quick ones.
