Two light rail lines leave Central Chalmers Street. Only one of them stops across the road from the
racecourse. On Golden Eagle Day, Saturday 31 October, that one is the L2.

Getting the other one is not a disaster. The L3 puts you on Anzac Parade at Kensington, which is a
walk from the Ascot Street entry. It is just longer than you planned, in shoes you did not plan it
in.

::: facts Race day, in short
When: **Saturday 31 October**, gates open 10.30am
Races: first at **12.10pm**, last at **6pm**
Light rail: the **L2** to Royal Randwick, on Alison Road
From Central: **9 minutes**, about every 10 minutes
Your fare: **in the race day ticket**, both ways, until 4am
Last one to the city: **1.10am**
:::

## Which light rail goes to the racecourse?

Board the L2 at Central Chalmers Street. The front of the tram reads Randwick.

Both lines start out the same way. Surry Hills first, then Moore Park. They split after that.

The next L2 stop is Royal Randwick. It sits on Alison Road, just across from the racecourse.

The L3 turns down Anzac Parade instead. ES Marks, then Kensington, then Kingsford.

The 11.05am from Central Chalmers Street is at Royal Randwick at 11.14am. Trams run about every ten
minutes, and extra ones run between Central and Royal Randwick for the race day.

## Is the last stop the racecourse?

No, and this is the other way to get it wrong. The end of the L2 is a stop called Randwick, three
stops further on.

Get off at Royal Randwick. If you hear Wansey Road, you have gone one too far.

## Coming in on the train?

Central is the closest station. Walk out to Chalmers Street and the trams are there.

Check your own line before you leave that morning. Trackwork on the Saturday changes the timetable
across the train lines. Inner West services do not run at all.

On the T4, trains run to a changed timetable and stopping pattern from 6.30am to 12.20pm. Some start
or end at Hurstville or Sutherland. Platforms change too, so read the screens rather than your
memory.

On the metro, ride the M1 to Central and change to the light rail there.

On a ferry, get off at Circular Quay and take the L2 from the wharf end. The 11.01am from Circular
Quay is at Royal Randwick at 11.24am.

## Does the race ticket cover the fare?

Yes. A ticket bought ahead of the day covers the trip there and the trip back.

It works on the train and on the metro. It works on buses, light rail and ferries. Show it to staff
when you board. Keep it in your pocket afterwards, because it is still your fare home until 4am on Sunday.

## What about the buses?

Five bus routes run along Anzac Parade and Alison Road. They stop near the racecourse. They are the
339 and the 373. The 374, the 375 and the 396 run there too.

One stop is shut from 9am to 7.30pm. On the 339 towards Clovelly, the Darley Road stop opposite
Randwick TAFE is closed. Use Centennial Park Main Gate on Darley Road instead.

## Getting home after the last race

The last race is at 6pm. Trams to the city leave Royal Randwick about every ten minutes into the
evening.

The 6.31pm is at Central at 6.40pm. It reaches Circular Quay at 6.56pm.

If you stay on into the night, the last one towards the city goes at 1.10am. The last one the other
way, towards Randwick, goes at 1.32am. Nothing then runs until 5.16am on the Sunday.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk out of the course.
A timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually coming.

## If you were going to drive

There is parking on site, in the Infield car park. The entry is on High Street opposite UNSW.

The streets around the course are not an option that day. Clearways run on Darley Road from 4am to
7pm, on Ascot Street from 8am to 7pm and on High Street from 3pm to 7pm. Cars left in them get
towed, and the fee is yours.

Driving to a Park & Ride instead works. It gives you up to 18 hours of parking in a day once you
complete a public transport trip. Press the intercom at the exit gate on the way out and show your
race day ticket. You can
[check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).

## The practical stuff

- **Taxis** come in through the Ascot Street entrance, where there is a rank.
- **Rideshare** pick-ups close to the course are restricted from 3pm to 8pm.
- **Trams** cannot stop quickly. Use the pedestrian crossings on Alison Road.
- **Accessible trips** show up in the trip planner with a wheelchair icon.
- **Paying** without a race day ticket: tap on and tap off with your phone, or a credit or debit card.

One tram, nine minutes, and it stops across the road from the gate. Pick the L2 and the day starts
at the first race rather than at a parking space.
