Friday night under lights in the south west, and Melbourne City are the visitors. Macarthur FC kick
off at 7.40pm on 27 November, and the ground sits 450 metres from the platform.

Off the train and you are nearly in your seat. The worry on a Friday night is the trip back, not the
trip out. Full time is 9.40pm and the last train to the city goes at 11.59pm. That part is on us.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Friday 27 November**, kick-off **7.40pm**, full time 9.40pm
Where: Campbelltown Sports Stadium, **Leumeah**
From the station: **450 metres** to the ground
Getting there: the **6.23pm** from Central is in at **7.19pm**
Last train to the city: **11.59pm**, platform 1
:::

::: callout Do this first
Check the back half of your trip before you leave home. From 11pm that night the City Circle runs
one direction only, first stop Museum out of Central.
:::

## How do you get there from the city?

Take a T8 train from Central towards Macarthur or Campbelltown. Leumeah is the stop before
Campbelltown.

The 6.23pm leaves Central from platform 23 and is at Leumeah at 7.19pm. That is 56 minutes on the
train and 21 minutes before kick-off.

Want longer at the ground? The 6.05pm from platform 22 is in at 6.58pm.

From there the pedestrian link out of the station walks you to the gate.

## Coming from Liverpool or the south west?

- From **Liverpool**, take a T2 or T5 train to Glenfield. Change there for a T8 to Leumeah.
- From **Leppington** or **Edmondson Park**, do the same. T2 or T5 to Glenfield, then the T8.
- From the **T3** at Bankstown, change at Liverpool first, then again at Glenfield.
- From **Holsworthy**, **Revesby** or **Riverwood**, the T8 runs straight through.
- From **Picton** and the Southern Highlands, change at Campbelltown for the T8.

## Which platform at Leumeah?

- **Platform 1**: trains back towards the city.
- **Platform 2**: trains out to Campbelltown and Macarthur.

Get that one right on the way home.

## What time is the last train home?

Full time is 9.40pm, so nobody is sprinting for anything.

Trains to the city leave platform 1 every fifteen minutes through the evening. They go at 14, 29, 44
and 59 minutes past.

The last one is the 11.59pm, and it runs to Central.

Going the other way, platform 2 keeps running to Campbelltown and Macarthur until 2.11am.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk back up. A timetable
is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## What if you miss it?

The N30 night bus takes over. It leaves from O'Sullivan Road at Leumeah Station and runs to Town
Hall.

It runs every half hour through the small hours. The first is at 12.29am and the last at 3.29am.

Trains to the city start again at 3.49am from platform 1.

Heading south instead? The N30 to Macarthur leaves from the stop opposite at 2.53am, and then every
half hour to 4.23am.

## Is there a bus to the ground?

Five regular routes stop within a walk of the ground. The stops are on O'Sullivan Road and Pembroke
Road.

- **870** and **871** come up from Liverpool through Ingleburn.
- **872** runs from Glenfield by way of Macquarie Fields.
- **879** runs to and from Campbelltown station.
- **881** is the north loop out of Campbelltown.

## If you were going to drive

There is limited parking off Old Leumeah Road.

The big one is the commuter car park at Leumeah Station, with 1,040 spaces. Get in from Airds Road
and Plough Inn Road, then take the station pedestrian link across to the ground.

Roads near the stadium are busier than usual on a match night, so leave yourself extra time.

## The practical stuff

- **Platform 1** for the city, **platform 2** for Macarthur.
- **Taxi rank** at Leumeah Station, 450 metres from the ground.
- **Bus stops** on O'Sullivan Road and Pembroke Road.
- **Commuter car park** at the station, 1,040 spaces.
- **Trackwork** from 11pm that night on the City Circle, the T1 and the T9.
- **Night bus** N30 to Town Hall once the trains stop.

Get there early and have a wander. It is a short walk from platform to turnstile going in, and the
same one coming out.
