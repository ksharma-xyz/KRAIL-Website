There is a platform 0 at Lidcombe. The train that leaves from it puts you at Olympic Park five minutes
later.

You will not find it in the row with the others. That is the catch, and it is the first thing on this
page.

::: facts Lidcombe, platform by platform
Platform 0: the shuttle to Olympic Park, five minutes
Platform 1: into the city, then on to North Sydney
Platform 2: west to Penrith and Schofields
Platform 3: into the city, all stops through the Inner West
Platform 4: out to Parramatta, Liverpool and Leppington
Platform 5: the shuttle to Bankstown, nineteen minutes
:::

## Which platform for Olympic Park?

Platform 0. Look for it at the far end of the station, away from the row of platforms 1 to 5.

The train from it runs to Olympic Park and stops nowhere else. It turns around and comes straight
back.

On a weekday it goes every 10 minutes through the middle of the day. The 11.03am is at Olympic Park
at 11.08am.

Weekends start slower. It is every 20 minutes first thing, then every 10 minutes from 8.03am.

## Why do two platforms go to the city?

Platform 1 and platform 3 both say the city. They are not the same trip.

Platform 1 is the T1. It runs to Strathfield, then Redfern and Central. After that come Town Hall and
Wynyard, then North Sydney.

Platform 3 is the T2 and the T3. It calls at the Inner West stations on the way. Burwood, Ashfield
and Newtown are all on it.

Here is the difference in numbers. The 11.05am from platform 1 is at Town Hall at 11.28am. The
11.08am from platform 3 gets there at 11.39am.

Three minutes apart on the platform. Eleven minutes apart at Town Hall.

Take platform 1 if you only want to get into town. Take platform 3 if your stop is one of the Inner
West ones.

## What leaves from the other platforms?

Platform 2 is the one going west. Penrith and Schofields both leave from it, both via Parramatta.

Platform 4 is the other outbound side. Parramatta and Leppington go via Granville. Liverpool goes via
Regents Park.

So two platforms will take you to Parramatta. Read the board rather than the platform number.

Platform 5 is the shuttle to Bankstown. It calls at Berala, Regents Park, Birrong and Yagoona. The
11.07am is at Bankstown at 11.26am, and through the middle of a weekday it goes every 15 minutes.

## Where are the bus stands?

On two different streets. That is what catches people out.

Stand A and Stand B are on Church St. Stand C is on Railway St.

Route 920 uses both Church St stands, so the stand picks your direction. Stand A goes to Bankstown.
Stand B goes to Parramatta.

The 525 to Parramatta also leaves from Stand B. The 925 to East Hills leaves from Stand C. The 401
goes from Stand A.

Night buses split the same way. N50, N60 and N61 run to Town Hall from Stand C on Railway St. Going
the other way they leave from Stand B on Church St.

Not sure which stand is yours? You can
[find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## How late can you leave it?

The last train out of Olympic Park is 12.01am, every night of the week. It is at Lidcombe at 12.08am.

From Lidcombe the city trains run a few minutes later than that. Platform 1 goes first and arrives
first.

- **Monday to Thursday**: 12.04am from platform 1, then 12.07am from platform 3.
- **Friday**: 1.04am from platform 1, then 1.07am from platform 3.
- **Saturday**: 12.49am from platform 1, then 12.56am from platform 3.
- **Sunday**: 12.19am from platform 1, then 12.26am from platform 3.

Monday to Thursday the 12.04am is at Town Hall at 12.28am. The 12.07am gets there at 12.40am. The
later train is the slower one.

Trackwork moves all of this. Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before
a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Lifts** and stairs down to the platforms.
- **Toilets**, including an accessible toilet.
- **Ticket machines** at the station.
- **Bike racks** and a bike locker.
- **Taxi rank** on the Church St side.
- **Two ways in**, one off Church St and one at the Joseph St end.

Six platforms, three bus stands, two streets. Get the number right on the way in, and Olympic Park is
five minutes away.
