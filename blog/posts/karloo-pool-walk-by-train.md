Oh, you're going to love this one! Picture yourself floating in Karloo Pool with nothing but bush all around. Hop off the train at Heathcote and Royal National Park is all yours.

Bring a picnic and something comfy to sit on. Karloo Pool is the spot where you stop for a quick swim and end up staying all afternoon.

The track starts right by the station, so you can be in the bush a few minutes after the train doors open.
How good is that?

::: facts The walk, in short
Getting there: **T4** to Heathcote, about 50 minutes from Central
The track: **Karloo walking track**, starting at the station
Distance: **10km return** to Uloola Falls, 3 to 5 hours
Grade: **Grade 3**, short steep hills and many steps
Park hours: **7am to 8.30pm**
:::

## How do you get there from the city?

1. Jump on a T4 from Central heading for Waterfall and hop off at Heathcote. On a Saturday the 9.12am
   from Central gets you there at 10.03am, and there's another train every half hour after that.
2. Pop out of the station and wander south-east along Wilson Parade.
3. Stroll on past the emergency services centre. Just after it you'll reach an intersection with the
   Karloo track signposted, and you're on your way!

Off the train, a little walk up the road, and the bush is waiting for you.

## What's the walk like?

It's 10km there and back from the station to Uloola Falls, so give yourself 3 to 5 hours and make a
whole day of it. Your legs will earn that swim! The track is formed and signposted the whole way, with a
few short steep hills, plenty of steps and the odd obstacle to step over. If you've done a bit of
bushwalking before, you'll feel right at home.

Karloo Pool comes up first, and it's the reason most people fall in love with this track. Swim,
picnic, repeat!

Keep going past the pool and you'll spot big whaleback rock outcrops, then the Uloola Turrets a little
further on.

Uloola Falls is the far end of the walk. Come in spring and the heath around it is bursting with
wildflowers.

## Can you finish at a different station?

You sure can! From Uloola Falls, the Uloola walking track carries on to Waterfall station and pops you
out at the back of the station carpark. It's grade 3 too, with gentle hills and plenty of steps, so
it's a lovely way to stretch the day out. Pack a big lunch for this version!

Trains leave Waterfall twice an hour on a Saturday afternoon, so you won't wait long. Catch the 4.08pm
and you'll be at Central by 5.03pm.

## What should you pack?

- **Swimmers**, because Karloo Pool is calling!
- **Water**, a hat and sunscreen, and plenty of each.
- **Shoes** you're happy to get a bit muddy, as the track can go boggy after rain.
- **The track map**, downloaded to your phone before you leave, because reception is limited in the park.

Good to know, the signs guide you the whole way, and you'll find toilets at Heathcote station and at
Uloola Falls campground. The park is open 7am to 8.30pm. On a stormy or fire-danger day it can close.
Give the National Parks page a quick look first and you'll know the gates are open. Dogs have to sit
this one out because it's a national park, so give the pup a big cuddle before you go!

Fancy another bush day in Royal National Park? [Wedding Cake Rock](/blog/bundeena-wedding-cake-rock-without-a-car/) is
a train and a little ferry ride away.

## What is the last train home?

Good news, the trains keep running for hours after the park closes at 8.30pm, so take your time over
that last swim.

On a Saturday, trains leave Heathcote for the city twice an hour. The 5.13pm has you at Central by
6.03pm, just in time for dinner. Fancy a late one? The last city trains go at 11.06pm and 11.36pm, and
an intercity heads to Central at 12.36am.

On a weeknight, the 11.29pm is the last train all the way to the city. The 12.29am goes as far as
Hurstville, so the 11.29pm is the one to aim for.

Leave Karloo Pool with enough daylight for the steps, and you'll be home happy and sun-kissed.
