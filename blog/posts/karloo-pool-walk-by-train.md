The Karloo walking track starts at Heathcote station. Come out of the gate, turn south-east down
Wilson Parade, and within a few minutes you are on it. It runs to Karloo Pool, a swimming and picnic
spot in Royal National Park, and on to Uloola Falls.

Bring lunch and something to sit on. Karloo Pool is where most people stop and stay a while.

Most people think you need a car for Royal National Park. This corner of it is a station and a
signpost.

::: facts The walk, in short
Getting there: **T4** to Heathcote, about 50 minutes from Central
The track: **Karloo walking track**, starting at the station
Distance: **10km return** to Uloola Falls, 3 to 5 hours
Grade: **Grade 3**, short steep hills and many steps
Park hours: **7am to 8.30pm**
:::

## How do you get there without a car?

1. Take a T4 train from Central towards Waterfall and get off at Heathcote. On a Saturday the 9.12am
   from Central is at Heathcote at 10.03am. They run every half hour.
2. Walk south-east along Wilson Parade.
3. Keep going past the emergency services centre. Just after it you reach an intersection, and the
   Karloo track is signposted.

Park entry in Royal National Park is $12 per vehicle per day. You are not bringing one.

## The walk itself

It is 10km return from the station to Uloola Falls, so allow 3 to 5 hours. Grade
3, with short steep hills and many steps. It is a formed track with some obstacles, and it is
clearly signposted the whole way. Some bushwalking experience is recommended.

Karloo Pool comes first. It is a swimming and picnic spot, and it is the reason most people are on
this track at all.

Past the pool, look for the whaleback rock outcrops. They are named after their resemblance to
whales surfacing for air. The Uloola Turrets are further on.

Uloola Falls is the far end of the walk. In spring the heathland around it is in flower.

## Can you walk out at a different station?

Yes. From Uloola Falls the Uloola walking track carries on to Waterfall station, and it comes out at
the rear of the station carpark. It is grade 3 as well, with gentle hills and many steps.

Take any total distance you see quoted for the station to station version as somebody's estimate.

Trains run back from Waterfall too. On a Saturday afternoon they go at eight and thirty-eight
minutes past the hour, and the 4.08pm is at Central at 5.03pm.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** at Heathcote station and at Uloola Falls campground. Nothing in between.
- **Water**: carry your own. Hat and sunscreen too.
- **Swimming** at Karloo Pool.
- **Phone reception** is limited in the park. Load what you need before you walk in.
- **Steps**: many of them, plus some short steep hills.
- **Signposting** is clear the whole way.
- **After rain** the track gets boggy.
- **Dogs**: not permitted in the park.
- **Park hours** are 7am to 8.30pm. It can close for poor weather or fire danger.

Royal National Park has a second walk that needs no car. [Wedding Cake Rock](/blog/bundeena-wedding-cake-rock-without-a-car/) is
a train and a little ferry away.

## What is the last train home?

The fear on a bush walk is being out there when the last train goes. On this one that is not the
number that matters.

Royal National Park closes at 8.30pm. The trains keep going for hours afterwards.

City-bound trains leave Heathcote at thirteen and forty-three minutes past the hour on a Saturday.
The 5.13pm is at Central at 6.03pm. The last two are 11.06pm and 11.36pm, and an intercity service
follows at 12.42am.

On a weeknight the last through train to the city is the 11.29pm. A 12.29am runs as far as
Hurstville.

So the deadline is the park gate, not the platform. Turn back from Karloo Pool with enough light for
the steps and you are fine.
