Bondi Junction in eight minutes. Chatswood in eleven. Central in three, if you pick the right staircase.

Martin Place has four platforms and two of them are a metro. Get it wrong and you are heading the other way across town. That part is on us.

Here's the quick version.

- **Bondi Junction** is platform 2.
- **Central, Sydenham and Bankstown** are platform 3.
- **Barangaroo, Chatswood and Tallawong** are platform 4.
- **Hurstville, Cronulla and the South Coast** are platform 1.

::: facts Martin Place, in short
Train: **platforms 1 and 2**, the T4 and the intercity services
Metro: **platforms 3 and 4**, the M1, every 4 to 10 minutes
Buses: **Elizabeth St**, stands C to H
Metro doors: **Hunter St** at one end, **Martin Place** at the other
Train exits: Elizabeth St East, and Macquarie St with Phillip St
:::

## Which platform at Martin Place?

Four platforms, and each one has a clear job.

- **Platform 1** is the train heading south. Hurstville, Cronulla, Waterfall and the intercity run down to the South Coast.
- **Platform 2** is the train east to Bondi Junction, which is the end of the run.
- **Platform 3** is the metro heading south, through Central and Sydenham to Bankstown.
- **Platform 4** is the metro heading north, through Barangaroo and over to Chatswood and Tallawong.

On a Friday morning they leave within three minutes of each other. The 9.00am from platform 2 is at Bondi Junction at 9.08am. The 9.01am from platform 3 is at Central at 9.04am. The 9.03am from platform 4 is at Barangaroo at 9.05am and Chatswood at 9.14am.

Three minutes apart, three different sides of the city.

## Should I take the train or the metro?

If you are going to Central, both work, and the metro is quicker.

On that same Friday morning the 9.01am from platform 3 is at Central at 9.04am. The 9.07am from platform 1 gets there at 9.12am. Three minutes against five.

The metro also runs more often. It goes every 4 to 10 minutes through the day, so there is rarely much point checking the time before you walk down.

For Bondi Junction there is no choice to make. That is platform 2, and only platform 2.

Have a quick look at the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) if you are deciding on the move.

## Which way out of Martin Place?

The metro station and the train station sit side by side, joined underground, and they do not share their doors.

The metro runs a long way under the street. It has an entrance at **Hunter St** at the north end and another at **Martin Place** itself at the south end. Pick the one nearest where you are going before you ride the escalator up.

The train concourse has its own two ends. The stairs at one end take you **to Elizabeth St East**, and that end is also where the link to the metro is. The lifts and escalators at the other end take you **to Macquarie St and Phillip St**, which is the side for Parliament and the Domain.

## Where does my bus go from?

The buses you want are on Elizabeth St, in stands lettered C to H. Stands A and B sit over on Macquarie St.

- **Stand C** is the way back to Circular Quay.
- **Stand E** is the eastern suburbs. North Bondi on the 333. Coogee on the 373. Kingsford on the 343 and Maroubra Beach on the 396.
- **Stand H** is the express run east, so the 339X to Clovelly and the 374X to Coogee.
- **Stand F** is the inner west. Glebe Point on the 431 and Lilyfield on the 470.
- **Stand G** heads out through Canterbury, on the 426 and the 428.

Every route at stand C also leaves from stand E, pointing the other way. So if you came in on the 333, you go home on the 333 from the stand across the way.

If all you have is the route number, you can [find your stop by that number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Handy things to know

- **Two sets of toilets.** The train ones are on the concourse inside the gates. The metro ones are on its own concourse.
- **Metro opening hours** start 10 minutes before the first service of the day and end 10 minutes after the last.
- **Lifts and escalators** reach every platform, and there are seven accessible toilets and seven baby change tables.
- **Taxi ranks** are on Castlereagh, Elizabeth, Hunter and Phillip Sts.
- **Dropping someone off?** The kiss and ride bay is at Chifley Square.
- **Bike racks** are at the station.

## What is the last train and metro?

Late on a Friday the trains stop before the metro does.

- The last T4 to Bondi Junction is the 1.08am from platform 2.
- Both last metros go at 1.35am. Platform 4 for Tallawong, platform 3 for Bankstown.
- The last train south is the 1.43am from platform 1 to Cronulla.

Everything starts again from 4.53am. Until then the buses on Elizabeth St carry on. The 333 to North Bondi and the 373 to Coogee both leave stand E through the small hours.

So work out your platform number before you go down the escalator, and the rest is easy. Have a great day out.
