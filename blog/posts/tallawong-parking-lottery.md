You leave at what feels like a sensible hour. You turn in, the boom gate does not lift, and you start the slow lap looking for a gap that is not there. Your 7:52 is now an 8:14, and you are parking on a side street you will worry about all day.

Here is the part that turns that morning around. **You were probably queuing at the wrong car park.**

## It is not one carpark, it is three

Tallawong has three separate Park & Ride car parks, and together they hold just over a thousand cars.

::: facts The three car parks
P1: 152 spaces, entry on Conferta Avenue
P2: 455 spaces, entry on Aristida Street
P3: 397 spaces, entry on Conferta Avenue
Cost: free for up to 18 hours if you tap on and tap off
Open: all day, every day
:::

Most people only ever meet one of them. You drive the way you always drive, you reach the first entry you come to, and if it is full that is the whole story. "Tallawong is full" gets said a lot. What is usually true is that **one car park at Tallawong is full.**

![The KRAIL Park and Ride screen showing Tallawong P1, P2 and P3 with spots available, percent full, and the time each count was last updated](/images/tallawong-park-ride.webp "phone | P1 and P2 nearly gone while P3 sits at 12 percent. Same station, three car parks, and you cannot see any of that from the road. Counts as they stood at 1:57pm on 23 August 2026, not right now.")

That is one afternoon, not a rule. But it is the shape of the thing worth knowing: the small one fills first, and the big one at the back is a different question entirely.

## P1 and P3 are the same turn

This is the bit that costs people the most time. **P1 and P3 both come off Conferta Avenue.**

So when you are sitting at a full P1 wondering whether to give up and drive home, you are not far from another 397 spaces on the street you are already on. It is not a detour across the suburb. It is carrying on a little further.

P2 is the odd one out, off Aristida Street, and it is the biggest of the three at 455 spaces.

::: callout Worth knowing before you go
Free parking is not automatic. It is up to 18 hours free **when you complete a trip**, tapping on and tapping off with the card you travel with. Park and then drive off without catching anything and you are on the casual rate, which runs to $30 for the day on a weekday.
:::

## Check it before the driveway, not at the boom gate

Knowing there are three car parks only helps if you know which one to aim for on the morning you are actually running late.

The counts are published as live open data, so they can be read before you leave. In the app they sit on the trip you already take, all three at once, so the decision happens in your kitchen while you are picking up your keys, rather than through a windscreen with a queue behind you.

Thirty seconds at home beats thirty minutes of laps.

## More parking is coming

This is a good problem to have and it is being dealt with. A new multi storey car park has been announced for Tallawong with up to 1,100 extra spaces, part of a $160 million commitment to commuter parking across the northwest.

No opening date has been announced yet, so it does not help you tomorrow. But the pressure at Tallawong has been noticed and the answer is already funded, which is more than most parking complaints ever get.

Until it opens, the three car parks you already have are still better odds than most people realise. Most of us have only ever tried one of them.
