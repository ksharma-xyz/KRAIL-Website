If you drive to a station, you already know the feeling. You leave at what feels like a sensible hour, you turn into the carpark, and the boom gate tells you what you did not want to hear. Now you are looking for a side street, and your 7:52 has become an 8:14.

## The cliff is narrower than you think

Tallawong does not fill gradually. It sits comfortably under half capacity, holds there for about an hour, and then drops off a cliff inside a twenty minute window. Miss that window and there is no recovery.

::: callout The short version
Arriving before the cliff is not about being early. It is about being early on the **right side** of a window that shifts by day of the week.
:::

## What we changed in the app

Knowing the pattern is useless if you find out about it at the boom gate. So the number moved to where the decision actually happens, which is your kitchen, holding your keys.

![Live parking availability next to a saved trip](/images/realtime.webp "phone | Live spaces sit next to the trip, not three screens deep in a menu.")

Open the app, and the count is already on the trip you take every day. If it is green you drive. If it is red you have thirty seconds to make a different plan instead of thirty minutes to regret one.

## Three habits that actually work

- **Check before the driveway, not the boom gate.** The count updates often enough that a look from home is still accurate when you arrive.
- **Learn your own cliff.** Every site has a different one. Watch yours for a week and you will know your number better than any average we could publish.
- **Have a plan B saved.** Keep the bus leg saved as a second trip so switching takes one tap instead of a fresh search while you are already running late.
