Seven numbered exits. Eight bus stands. Two of those stands you can never catch a bus from.

Take Exit 1 or Exit 6 and you can reach all eight. Exit 2 only reaches stands A1 to A4.
Stand A1 and Stand B3 are arrivals only.

Then there is 9pm, when half the stands shut and their buses move somewhere else.
That part is on us.

::: facts Parramatta station, in short
Exits: **seven**, numbered 1 to 7
Bus stands: **A1 to A4** on the station side of Argyle St, **B1 to B4** on the far side
Arrivals only: **A1 and B3**, nothing leaves from either
After 9pm: A3 and A4 move to **A2**, B1 and B4 move to **B2**
Light rail: the **L4**, from the Parramatta Square stop
:::

## Which exit do you want?

- **Exit 1**, on Fitzwilliam St, signed Buses. It reaches the trains and all eight stands.
- **Exit 2**, signed Buses. It reaches stands A1 to A4 only.
- **Exit 6**, on Argyle St, signed Buses. It reaches all eight stands.
- **Exit 3** is signed Westfield.
- **Exit 4** comes out on Darcy St.
- **Exit 5** comes out at Parramatta Square.
- **Exit 7** comes out on Station St.

For the light rail, take Exit 5. The L4 goes from the Parramatta Square stop.

For the ferry, take Exit 7 and walk via Hassall St and Charles St.

Taxis, the kiss and ride bay and the coach bays are all on Fitzwilliam St, through Exit 1.

## Which stand is your bus?

Stands A1 to A4 sit on the station side of Argyle St. Stands B1 to B4 sit across the road.

- **Liverpool**: the 80, 802, 804 and 806 from B2.
- **Blacktown**: the 661, 700, 705 and 711 from B4. The 706 goes from A4.
- **Rouse Hill Station**: the 663, 664 and 665 from B4. The 601 and 603 go from A4.
- **Castle Hill**: the 660 and 662 from B4.
- **Bankstown**: the 907, 909 and 910 from B1. The 920 goes from A2.
- **Epping**: the 546 and 549 from A2.
- **Macquarie Park**: the 550 from A2. The 545 goes from A3.
- **Eastwood and West Ryde**: the 521, 523 and 524 from A3.
- **Lidcombe**: the 525 from A3.
- **Pennant Hills**: the 625 from A2. The 600 goes from A4.
- **City Hyde Park**: the 52 from A3.
- **Westmead Hospitals**: the 824 from B2.
- **Merrylands and Pemulwuy**: the 810 and 811 from B2.
- **Fairfield**: the 906 from B1.
- **Dural and Winston Hills**: the 604 and 606 from A4.
- **North Parramatta**: the 609 from A2.
- **Constitution Hill**: the 708 from B4.

Three of these routes run as frequent services. The 52, the 80 and the 665.
If you only know the number and not the stand, you can
[find your stop by route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## Why is my bus at two different stands?

Because five destinations here are served from both sides of Argyle St.

- **Blacktown**: the 706 from A4, the rest from B4.
- **Rouse Hill Station**: the 601 and 603 from A4, the rest from B4.
- **Pennant Hills**: the 625 from A2, the 600 from A4.
- **Bankstown**: the 920 from A2, the rest from B1.
- **Macquarie Park**: the 550 from A2, the 545 from A3.

So read the route number, not the destination on the sign.
Two buses to the same suburb can be a road apart.

## What changes at 9pm?

Four stands close for the night, and their buses move to one stand on each side.

Anything that normally leaves from **A3 or A4** leaves from **A2** instead.
Anything that normally leaves from **B1 or B4** leaves from **B2** instead.

On a Saturday the last bus out of Stand B4 is the 8.50pm to Rouse Hill Station.
At 9pm the 700, the 705 and the 711 to Blacktown all go from Stand B2.
The 665 to Rouse Hill Station follows at 9.10pm, and the 660 to Castle Hill at 9.25pm.

Stand A4 finishes earlier again. Its last Saturday bus is the 8.35pm to Pennant Hills.
After that the 600 goes from Stand A2.

At night there are only two stands that matter. A2 on the station side, B2 across the road.

## When does the last bus go?

The night buses leave from those same two stands.

Four of them run from **A2** to Town Hall. They are the N60, N70, N71 and N81.
Early on a Sunday morning the N60 and the N71 go at 1.51am.

From **B2** the N60 runs to Fairfield, the N70 to Penrith and the N71 to Richmond.

The 52 keeps going from A2 after them. On a Sunday morning it left at 1.30am and again at 2am.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk out of the station.
The stand is the plan. The countdown is what is actually happening.

## The practical stuff

- **Trains**: four platforms, reached from the West Concourse and the East Concourse. The T1, the T2
  and the T5 all call here, along with intercity and regional services.
- **Toilets** are open 24 hours, inside the paid area of the West Concourse.
- **Lifts, stairs and escalators** at both concourses.
- **Bikes**: parking on Macquarie St and beside Station St at Exit 7, with a bike locker at the same
  spot.
- **Coaches** use the bays on Fitzwilliam St, through Exit 1.
- **The light rail**: the L4 runs between Westmead and Carlingford, from 5am to 1am.
- **The ferry**: at low tide a bus replaces it between Parramatta and Rydalmere, so check before you
  walk down to the wharf.

Eight stands, and the one you want depends on the number on the front of the bus.
Get the exit right and you come up on the correct side of Argyle St.
