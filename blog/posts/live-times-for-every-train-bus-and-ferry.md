You are on Platform 2 at Seven Hills, the train has not turned up, and you are weighing up a coffee.
Open KRAIL and you get a live countdown, the platform, and exactly how late it is running, with the
old time struck through.

::: shot
![An expanded ferry trip showing both wharf names and accessibility icons](/images/krail/live-times-for-every-train-bus-and-ferry/02-ferry-trip-expanded.png "The countdown, the wharf and the side, on every mode.")

## Your times, live

Search any trip and every option shows you:

- a countdown, like **in 7 mins** or **Now**
- where to wait: the **platform**, the bus **stand**, or the ferry **wharf**
- when you leave, when you get there, and how long it all takes, including the walking
:::

It works for trains, metro, buses, ferries, light rail and coaches, all in the same place.

::: shot flip
![A trip from Seven Hills to Central showing in 9 mins on Platform 2, with the 8:33 PM struck through and Delayed 4 mins beside it](/images/krail/live-times-for-every-train-bus-and-ferry/03-train-delayed-4-mins.png "Late means the old time gets crossed out, not quietly replaced.")

## Running late? You will see it

When a service is running behind, KRAIL crosses out the old time and shows the new one next to it,
with **Delayed 4 mins** in colour so you cannot miss it. Early services get the same treatment. No more
guessing whether the 7:42 is really the 7:42.
:::

Leave the screen open and the times keep updating by themselves every 30 seconds. The countdowns tick
down as you watch.

## Open a trip for every stop

Tap an option to open it up. You see which way the train is heading and how long each walk takes. Tap the
stops count, like **12 stops**, to see every stop along the way with its time. Stops marked as wheelchair accessible show a little accessibility
icon, which helps when you need to know where you can get on and off easily.

## Check the alerts before you go

If something is going on with your trip, like trackwork or a detour, open the option and you will see a button like **1 Alert**. Tap it and
read what is happening, in full, before you walk out the door.

## Good to know

- Want to see what you just missed? Tap **Show previous departures**. Want later options? Tap **Load
  more departures**.
- The live times come from Transport for NSW. When there is no live update for a service, KRAIL shows
  the timetable time.
