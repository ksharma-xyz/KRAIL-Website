Iron Maiden at Allianz Stadium, on a Sunday night in November. Gates at 5.30pm, and the band is
still going at half past ten.

Thousands of fans, all walking out of Moore Park at the same moment. That part is on us.

**Your show ticket is your fare.** It covers public transport both ways, light rail included.

::: facts The night, in short
When: **Sunday 15 November**, Allianz Stadium at Moore Park
Clock: gates **5.30pm**, show **6.10pm**, finish **10.50pm**
Your fare: **included in the show ticket**, until 4am
Getting there: **L2 or L3** from Central, Chalmers Street
Getting home: light rail until **1am**, metro until **12.30am**
:::

::: callout Do this first
Keep your show ticket once you are through the gate. It is your ride home as well as your ride
there. Show it to staff when you board.
:::

## How do you get to Moore Park?

Take a train or the metro to Central. Walk out to the light rail stop on Chalmers Street. Catch an
L2 or an L3 heading south.

Get off at Moore Park. The stop sits on Anzac Parade, just south of the stadium.

On a Sunday afternoon the trams go every five minutes between Circular Quay and Moore Park. After
7pm it is every eight. Extra services run before and after the show.

Coming by ferry? Get off at Circular Quay. The L2 and the L3 both start there, so you can board
without changing at Central.

## Coming from the east instead?

From Bondi Junction, take route 333, 352 or 440 to Oxford Street. Route 355 goes to Lang Road.
Either way you finish within a short walk of the stadium.

Regular buses stop along Anzac Parade near the stadium too. Routes 339, 373, 374 and 396 all run
past.

## What if the light rail queue is long?

Walk it. Central to Moore Park is about 25 minutes on foot.

Head out along Devonshire Street, then cross the Albert 'Tibby' Cotter Walkway. It takes you above
the traffic on Anzac Parade.

Queues are expected before the show and after it. Walking skips both.

## How do you get home after 10.50pm?

The L2 and the L3 run until 1am. Extra trams are put on once the show ends.

There will still be a queue, so allow extra time. Metro services are extended to 12.30am that night.

Your show ticket stays valid until 4am on the Monday morning. There is no rush to be first out the
gate.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you join the queue. A
published frequency is the plan. The countdown is what is turning up.

## The practical stuff

- **Trackwork** that afternoon and evening between Gosford and Hawkesbury River. Some services north
  of Sydney run to a changed timetable.
- **Airport stations**: the station access fee is not covered by your show ticket.
- **Taxi rank** on Errol Flynn Boulevard, near the Hordern Pavilion.
- **Rideshare** pick-ups may be restricted afterwards. Choose a spot away from the stadium.
- **Driver Avenue** outside the stadium is partly closed from 3.30pm.
- **Clearways** apply in Moore Park from 3pm to midnight. On Cleveland Street they start at 6am.
  Cars left in one get towed.
- **Driving to a station** is the version that works. Park & Ride gives up to 18 hours of free
  parking a day when you complete a public transport trip. You can
  [check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).
- At the exit gate, press the intercom and show your event ticket.

Come early and take your time getting in. The queue is on the way out, not the way in.
