At Redfern the odd platform goes to the city. The even one goes the other way.
Platform 3 into town, platform 4 out of it, and the same on all six T lines.

Nobody gets lost at Redfern. They pick wrong. Twelve platforms, four exits on two
different sides, and a train already pulling in.

::: facts Redfern, in short
Platforms: **twelve**, 1 to 10 upstairs and 11 and 12 below them
To the city: the **odd** platform, every time
Exits: Lawson St, Gibbons St, Little Eveleigh St, Marian St
Buses: Stand A on Gibbons St, Stands B and C on Marian St
Overnight: the station closes **1am to 4.30am**
:::

## Which platform goes to the city?

The odd one. Here it is line by line.

- **T1 and T9**: platform 3 to the city. Platform 4 away from it.
- **T2 and T3**: platform 5 to the city. Platform 6 away from it.
- **T8**: platform 7 to the city. Platform 8 away from it.
- **T4**: platform 11 to the city. Platform 12 away from it.

Six lines, one rule. Intercity services stop here too, heading for the Blue
Mountains, the Central Coast and the South Coast.

## Where is the T4?

Downstairs. Platforms 11 and 12 sit below the other ten, at the Gibbons St end of
the concourse.

You get down there by escalator or by stairs. No lift goes to them.

Platforms 1 to 10 have lift access. Platforms 11 and 12 do not. If you are on
wheels or carrying something heavy, that is the sentence that matters here.

## Which exit do you want?

There are four, and two of them are a long walk from the other two.

- **Lawson St**, the north end. The University of Sydney is west from here. Kiss
  and ride is on this street.
- **Gibbons St**, the north end on the eastern side. The taxi rank and the bike
  lockers are here. So is bus Stand A, and the way down to platforms 11 and 12.
- **Little Eveleigh St**, the south end on the western side. Carriageworks is
  along here.
- **Marian St**, the south end on the eastern side. South Eveleigh is down here,
  and so are bus Stands B and C.

Redfern Town Hall and Redfern Park are both east of the station, past Regent St.

## Where do the buses go from?

Two streets, three stands.

- **Stand A** on Gibbons St. Only two routes use it and both are night buses. The
  N11 and the N20 run from here to the city and Town Hall.
- **Stand B** on Marian St. The 308 to Central Eddy Avenue and the 310 to Central
  Railway Square.
- **Stand C** on Marian St near Regent St. The 305 and the 306 go to Mascot. The
  308 goes to Marrickville Metro and the 309 to Port Botany. The 310 goes to
  Botany and the 392 to Little Bay.

If all you have is the route number, you can
[find your stop by that number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

## What happens after midnight?

Most trains stop running between midnight and 4.30am. Night buses take over.

The station closes as well. It shuts at 1am on weekdays and at 1.30am on weekends
and public holidays. It opens again at 4.30am. The rest of the time it is staffed
around the clock.

To get into the city overnight, use Stand A on Gibbons St. The N11 and the N20
both run through to Town Hall. Going the other way, both of them leave from Stand
C on Marian St.

## Is there a metro at Redfern?

No. The nearest metro station is Waterloo, south of here down Gibbons St.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** on the concourse, inside the paid area. Open during service hours.
- **Accessible toilet** and a baby change table at the station.
- **Lifts** to platforms 1 to 10. None to platforms 11 and 12.
- **Bikes**: racks and a bike shed.
- **Taxi rank** on Gibbons St. **Kiss and ride** on Lawson St.
- **Free mobile phone charging** at the station.
- **Peak here** is 6.30am to 10am and 3pm to 7pm, Monday to Thursday.
- **Staffed** 24 hours, apart from the overnight closure.

Read the number before you read the destination. Odd for the city, even for home.

head down, because the platform is the wrong place to find out.
