Nine stops between Sydenham and Bankstown, and a metro every four to ten minutes the whole way.
Marrickville, then Dulwich Hill. Canterbury and Campsie after that. Two or three minutes apart.

Sydenham is where you change for it. Six platforms, and the metro ones are on the far side from the
train. That part is on us.

::: facts Sydenham, in short
Metro: platforms **1 and 2**, every 4 to 10 minutes
Train: platforms **3 to 6**, the T4 and the T8
To Bankstown: **platform 2**
To the city: **platform 1** on the metro, 3 or 5 on the train
Buses: stands **A to E**
:::

::: callout Sunday night is the one to watch
The last metro that carries on past Sydenham into the city goes at 10.53pm on a Sunday. Three more
arrive after it and go no further.
:::

## Which platform at Sydenham?

Two platforms for the metro on the northern side. Four for the train on the southern side.

- **Platform 1**: metro to the city, and on to Chatswood and Tallawong.
- **Platform 2**: metro to Marrickville, Dulwich Hill and Bankstown.
- **Platform 3**: the T8 towards the city.
- **Platform 4**: the T8 out of the city, towards Wolli Creek and the south.
- **Platform 5**: the T4 towards Redfern, Central and Bondi Junction.
- **Platform 6**: the T4 out towards Tempe, Wolli Creek and the south.

The 10.05am from platform 1 is at Central at 10.11am. The 10.03am from platform 2 is at Bankstown
at 10.25am.

## Metro or train into the city?

Both get you there. They land you in different parts of it.

The metro from platform 1 calls at Waterloo, then Central. After that come Gadigal, Martin Place,
Barangaroo and Victoria Cross.

The train from platform 5 calls at Redfern, then Central and Town Hall. After that come Martin
Place and Bondi Junction. The 10am is at Central at 10.09am and Bondi Junction at 10.22am.

So take the metro for Central, Barangaroo or anywhere north of the harbour. Take the train for Town
Hall or the eastern suburbs. For Martin Place, take whichever turns up first.

## How do you get to Bankstown now?

Platform 2, on the metro.

Marrickville and Dulwich Hill first. Then Hurlstone Park, Canterbury and Campsie. Belmore and
Lakemba after that. Wiley Park, Punchbowl and Bankstown at the end.

The whole run takes about 22 minutes.

If you need a hand getting on or off, use a Help Point before you board. There are ramps at every
station on the way.

## Where is my bus?

The stands are lettered A to E. Stands B, C and D sit along Railway Pde, on the northern side.
Stands A and E are at the western end, near Gleeson Ave.

Check your stand before you come out of the station. Or [find it by the route
number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) on the way.

## Which side of the station do you want?

- **Railway Pde** runs along the northern side, beside the metro platforms.
- **Burrows Ave** is the southern side, beside the train platforms. The taxi rank and the kiss and
  ride bays are here.
- **Gleeson Ave** is the western end.

There are lifts at the station, so a pram or a suitcase is not a problem.

There is also a marked walking and bike riding route from Sydenham to Marrickville station, if you
would rather move under your own steam.

## What time is the last one?

On the metro to Bankstown, from platform 2:

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: 12.45am.
- **Friday nights**: 1.45am.
- **Saturday nights**: 1.43am.
- **Sunday nights**: 11.13pm.

On the metro to the city, from platform 1:

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: 12.25am.
- **Friday nights**: 1.25am.
- **Saturday nights**: 1.23am.
- **Sunday nights**: 10.53pm. The ones after it stop at Sydenham.

The train to the city from platform 5 finishes at much the same time. It is 12.25am Monday to
Thursday and 1.06am on a Saturday.

Trackwork moves all of these. Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/)
before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are at Sydenham 24 hours a day.
- **Help Points** link straight through to staff, and there are Information Points too.
- **Hearing loops** are fitted, so switch your aid to T.
- **Taxis** and the kiss and ride bays are on Burrows Ave.
- **Paying**: tap on and tap off with a card or your phone.
- **Nearby**: Marrickville Metro Shopping Centre, Fraser Park and Sydenham Green.

Northern side for the metro, southern side for the train. Get that right and the rest of Sydenham
sorts itself out.
