You know you catch the 333 at Bondi Beach, and roughly where you get on. What you do not know is the
stop's official name, and "the one by the coffee place" does not get you far. In KRAIL, just type the
bus number.

::: shot
![Typing 333 returns two route cards, one for each direction, each with its own stop count](/images/krail/find-your-stop-by-bus-number/01-route-cards-collapsed.png "One card per direction, because the 333 does not stop in the same places both ways.")

## Type the number

Tap **Starting from** or **Destination** and type the bus number, like **333**. The route shows up at
the top of the results, with a card for each direction it runs and how many stops it has.
:::

::: shot flip
![A route card expanded into its stop list, each stop with its own stop ID](/images/krail/find-your-stop-by-bus-number/02-route-333-stop-list.png "Every stop on the route, in order, with the official name.")

## Open it and find your stop

Tap the card and it opens into every stop along the route, in order. Scroll down until you see the one
you know, tap it, and it drops into your trip. Now search as normal and you get live times from that
stop.
:::

It is the same trick for the stop you get off at. Type the number again, open the other direction if
you need it, and tap the stop near where you are going.

## Works without signal

The bus routes are stored right on your phone. So even if you are underground, in a lift, or in that
one dead spot on the platform, the search still works. You only need a connection once you want the
live times.

## Good to know

- It is for bus numbers. Type the number exactly as it shows on the front of the bus.
- Some routes show more than one card, because a bus can run a different way on weekends or as an
  express. Open each one and you will spot yours.
- This finds your stop. For the actual times, pick your stops and search the trip.
