Up to thirty thousand people in The Domain, and music from 2pm right through to 10pm. Ministry of Sound Classical takes over the park on Saturday 5 December. Eight hours, in the middle of the city.

Bring something to sit on. Then here is the bit that catches people out. The L2 and L3 do not run at all that weekend. That is the ride most of us would reach for, and it will not be there. The trains and the metro will be, and that part is on us.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Saturday 5 December**, 2pm to 10pm
Where: The Domain, off Art Gallery Rd
Closest stations: **St James**, Martin Place and Town Hall
Walk from: **Martin Place exit 1**, 7 minutes
Not running: the **L2 and L3**, all weekend
:::

## How do you get to The Domain?

Take the train to St James, Martin Place or Town Hall. Those three sit closest to the park.

At Martin Place, head up exit 1 on Macquarie St. The Domain is a 7 minute walk from that exit.

At St James, come up on the Macquarie St side as well. The Domain sits about 400 metres from the station.

Riding the metro? Get off at Gadigal or Martin Place and walk from there.

Coming by ferry? Boats run to Circular Quay all day and the wharves are a stroll from the park. Weekend ferries are popular and can fill up, so keep a second plan in your pocket.

Catching a bus? The closest stands are at Town Hall and on Park St. Martin Place, St James and Hyde Park all have them too.

Tap on with a contactless card or your phone.

## Which exit do you want on the way out?

Here is the one worth remembering. The Macquarie St exit at St James is open from 4am to 9.45pm. The show finishes at 10pm. So the exit on the park side will already be shut when you wander back to it.

Walk round to the Elizabeth St entrance instead. It is on the other side of the station, beside the bus stands.

St James is staffed 24 hours, so there is someone to ask if you get turned around.

## What else is closed that weekend?

The L2 and L3 do not run on Saturday 5 or Sunday 6 December. Buses replace them between Randwick, Kingsford and Central.

The L1 is unaffected. It runs from the Grand Concourse at Central out to Dulwich Hill, 5am to 1am.

Heading north after the show? From 8.40pm, buses replace trains between Hornsby and Berowra. Trains between Hornsby and Central run to a changed timetable.

Going on to the Central Coast? From 8.20pm, buses replace trains between Gosford and Hornsby.

Heading west, buses replace trains between Penrith, Richmond and Granville. Trains run to a changed timetable between Granville and the city.

## Why is the car the wrong idea?

The roads start closing before the music stops. Art Gallery Rd shuts from 8pm to midnight. More of them go at 10pm, including stretches of Macquarie St, College St and St James Rd.

Parking around the park is limited, fills early and costs money. So the car is the slow way out of this one.

## How do you get home?

Most trains stop between midnight and 4.30am. Night buses take over after that, and in the city they all run from Town Hall.

The metro runs night services seven days a week. Gadigal and Martin Place are the two nearest the park.

The L1 keeps rolling until 1am.

Have a look at the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave the park. A timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually happening.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** at St James are on the platforms, open while services are running.
- **Staff** at St James, 24 hours a day.
- **Paying**: tap on with a contactless card or your phone.
- **Taxi ranks** near St James are on Pitt St, Elizabeth St, Castlereagh St and Phillip St.
- **Bus stands** at St James are A to D, beside the Elizabeth St entrance.
- **Ferries** to Circular Quay get busy on a weekend, so have a backup.

That is the whole trick. Pick your exit before you go, and the night ends the easy way.
