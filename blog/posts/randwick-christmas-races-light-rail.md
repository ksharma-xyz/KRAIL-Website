Christmas racing at Royal Randwick, on a Friday evening in December. Get there for the first race at
2.45pm and stay through to the last at 6.30pm.

The thing that puts people in a car is the crawl out along Alison Road afterwards. That part is on
us. A tram stops across the road from the gate, and it runs until 1am.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Friday 4 December**, gates open **1.45pm**
Racing: first race **2.45pm**, last race **6.30pm**
Your stop: **Royal Randwick** on the L2, across Alison Road
On an L3 instead: get off at **Kensington** and walk in
Trams home: one every **10 minutes** after 7pm, until **1am**
Parking: free in the Infield car park, entry off High Street
:::

::: callout Do this first
Check the line number on the front of the tram. Only the L2 stops at Royal Randwick.
:::

## How do you get to Royal Randwick?

Get yourself to Circular Quay or to Central. Both are on the light rail.

At Central, walk out to the light rail stop on Chalmers Street. If you are not sure which way that
is, here is [the exit you want at Central](/blog/central-station-which-exit/).

Catch an L2 heading south. Stay on past Moore Park and get off at Royal Randwick.

The stop sits on Alison Road, straight across from the racecourse. You are at the gate in a minute.

On a Friday between 7am and 7pm, trams run every 4 minutes as far as Moore Park. Past there, on
the Randwick leg, it is one every 8 minutes.

Coming in by ferry? Ride to Circular Quay. The L2 starts there, so you get on once and stay on.

On the metro, change at Central and walk out to Chalmers Street.

## L2 or L3? This is the one that catches people

The two lines look identical for most of the ride. Both leave Circular Quay. Both run down George
Street and call at Central Chalmers Street. Both stop at Surry Hills and Moore Park.

They split after Moore Park. The L2 carries on to Royal Randwick. The L3 turns away towards
Kensington and Kingsford, and it never calls at Royal Randwick.

So the stop you want is only on one of them. Read the front of the tram before you step on.

Already on an L3? Get off at Kensington. The stop is on Anzac Parade. Walk in from there to the
racecourse entry on Ascot Street.

## What about getting home?

The last race goes at 6.30pm, and this is the easy part.

From 7pm a tram leaves Royal Randwick every 10 minutes. On a Friday it stays at every 10 minutes
from 10pm through to 1am.

The L2 runs 5am to 1am in both directions, every day of the week. So there is nothing to sprint for
and no reason to leave before the last race.

One thing to hold on to. Light rail runs to a frequency rather than a clock, and crowds and road
traffic can stretch the gap. Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before
you walk out of the gate.

## The practical stuff

- **Bus routes** near the racecourse: 339, 373, 374, 375 and 396. They run between the city and the eastern suburbs along Anzac Parade and Alison Road.
- **Wheelchair access** at the Royal Randwick stop.
- **Parking** is free in the Infield car park. Entry is off High Street, opposite UNSW.
- **Accessible parking** is in the Infield car park too, with the permit displayed in the car.
- **Taxis** go in through the Ascot Street entrance, where there is a rank for drop offs and pick ups.
- **Rideshare** cannot stop on Alison Road, Ascot Street or Doncaster Avenue.
- **Traffic** around the racecourse will be heavier than usual. Allow extra time if you drive.
- **Trackwork**: none planned on the train network that Friday.

Bring your good shirt and somebody to argue about the form with. The ride home is already sorted.
