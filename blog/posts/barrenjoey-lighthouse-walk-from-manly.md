Barrenjoey Lighthouse stands at the very top of Sydney, above Palm Beach. There is no road up to it.
You walk the last kilometre whether you drove or not, so leave the car at home and take the boat.

It has been up there since 1881, 91m above the water, looking across Broken Bay to the Central
Coast. Ocean on one side, Pittwater on the other. There are picnic areas by the carpark at the
bottom, so take lunch.

::: facts The trip, in short
Getting there: the ferry to Manly, then the **199** bus
Walk up: **1km**, about **30 minutes** each way
Inside the tower: **Sundays** only, on a guided tour
Toilets and water: at the lighthouse, at the top
Coming home: the **199** runs to Manly all night
:::

## How do you get there without a car?

1. Take the ferry from Circular Quay, Wharf 3, to Manly. On a Saturday the 8.40am is at Manly Wharf
   at 9.02am.
2. Walk around to the bus stands on West Esplanade, beside the wharf. The 199 goes from Stand B.
3. Take the 199 towards Palm Beach. The 9.10am gets you there at 10.26am.
4. Get off at Palm Beach Golf Club, on Beach Rd. That is the stop for Governor Phillip Park.

The track starts from the Governor Phillip parking area. If you are not sure which stand or which
stop is yours, [find it on the map](/blog/find-sydney-stop-on-map/) before you leave.

## The walk itself

Two tracks go up. Barrenjoey track is the gentle one, 1km and about 30 minutes each way. It asks for
moderate fitness rather than training. Smugglers track is the short steep option.

The start catches people out. You cross the sand, climb about ten steps, and then it gets properly
steep for a while. Wear shoes with some grip.

At the top the lighthouse is still bare sandstone, quarried on the spot in 1881. The oil room and
the keepers' cottages stand beside it in the same stone.

The view goes north over Broken Bay to the Central Coast, then back down the other way over
Pittwater and Palm Beach. Look up for sea eagles and kites.

You can walk around the outside any day of the week. The inside opens on a guided tour on Sundays.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** at the lighthouse, including an accessible one and a baby change table.
- **Water** from a drinking fountain at the top, with a bottle refill station.
- **Picnic** areas by the carpark at the bottom.
- **Hat and sunscreen**: there is not much shade on the climb.
- **Phone reception** is limited up there. Load what you need before you start.
- **Swimming**: the beaches here are not patrolled, and the rips can be strong.
- **Prams and wheelchairs**: no. There is a beach crossing, steps and some very steep sections.
- **Weather**: give it a miss in strong wind, heavy rain or extreme heat.

## Getting back

The thing that puts people in a car is the thought of standing at Palm Beach in the dark with no way
south. One time settles it.

The bus home stops on Beach Rd, across the road from where you got off.

On a weeknight the 11.21pm reaches Manly Wharf at 12.12am, and the last ferry to Circular Quay goes
at 12.20am. On a Saturday night you get one more. The 11.36pm is in Manly at 12.27am, and the last
ferry goes at 12.50am.

After that the 199 still runs to Manly through the night. On a weeknight it goes hourly. The ferry is
the part that stops, and the first one back is at 6am on a weekday, 7.10am on a Sunday.

So the bus is not what strands you. Keep an eye on the boat.
