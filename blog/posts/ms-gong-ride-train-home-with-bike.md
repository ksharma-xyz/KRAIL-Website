You finish this ride at Fairy Meadow, a long way south of where you started. A train takes you and
the bike back. They leave Fairy Meadow every 30 minutes, from 9.30am until 5.30pm.

Ride it for the charity. Let the train do the way home.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Sunday 1 November**, 80km from 6am, 53km from 8am
Start: **Tempe** for the 80km, **Engadine** for the 53km
Finish: Thomas Dalton Park at Fairy Meadow, by **3.30pm**
Trains home: from Fairy Meadow every **30 minutes**, 9.30am to 5.30pm
Your ticket home: the ride's **$8** train ticket, bought before you board
:::

Up to 10,000 riders will be out there. It is the 45th MS Gong Ride.

## How do you get to the start?

The 80km leaves from Tempe Recreation Reserve. Three stations sit within a ten minute ride of it.
Take a train to Tempe, Wolli Creek or Sydenham. On the metro, ride to Sydenham and pedal the last
seven minutes.

Between 5.30am and 8am that morning, every T4 service stops at Wolli Creek and Tempe. Every T8
service stops at Wolli Creek between 5.30am and 8.30am.

Coming up from the south, four extra early trains leave Wollongong. They go at 4.23am, 4.37am,
5.22am and 5.53am. Each one stops at Engadine, Sutherland and Hurstville. Then Wolli Creek, Tempe
and Sydenham.

The 4.56am and 5.31am from Wollongong make an extra stop at Wolli Creek and Tempe. So does the
5.57am from Kiama.

For the 53km, get off at Engadine. Preston Park is a two minute ride from the station. The 6.15am
from Bondi Junction makes an extra stop at Engadine for the morning.

## Your bike cannot board a bus

That Sunday there is trackwork, and it lands on the way in. Buses replace trains between Central
and Strathfield or Olympic Park until 10am.

Bikes are not allowed on buses. That includes rail replacement buses. It is a flat rule, not the
driver's call.

So if you are coming from the west, do not plan a trip that routes you onto one. Take a T8 train to
Wolli Creek or Sydenham instead.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) the night before. A replacement bus
in your plan at 5am is a problem you want to find at 9pm.

## What about getting home?

Fairy Meadow station is the one at the finish. Special event trains leave there for Central and
Bondi Junction every 30 minutes, from 9.30am to 5.30pm.

- **South of Sydney**, they stop at Thirroul, Stanwell Park and Otford.
- **In the Shire**, Waterfall, Engadine and Sutherland.
- **Closer in**, Hurstville, Wolli Creek and Tempe.
- **Last**, Sydenham, Redfern and Central.

Most trains after 2pm go only as far as Central. If you are heading on to Bondi Junction late in the
day, change there.

These trains need the ride's own $8 train ticket. Tapping on does not work on them. Buy the ticket
at the finish line if you have not already got one.

Pay the normal way to get to the start. That ticket only covers the trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Bikes on trains** are fine at any time, on the train and on the metro.
- **On the metro**, stand in the multi-purpose areas in the first or last carriage.
- **Stow it clear** of the doorways and the aisle. Platforms are busier than usual all morning.
- **Converted e-bikes**, meaning a pedal bike fitted with a motor and a battery, cannot go on a
  train carriage or a platform. The fine is $400. Other e-bikes are fine.
- **Ferries** are not the backup. A crew can refuse a bike when there is no safe space for it.
- **Heading further south** after the finish, buses replace trains between Wollongong and Port
  Kembla from 9am. Your bike cannot get on those either.
- **Roads** around the course are closed from 4am to 10am, and some buses are diverted.

## The last train out

The last special event train out of Fairy Meadow goes at 5.30pm. The ride finishes by 3.30pm, which
leaves two hours.

That is plenty, right up until you stop moving and your legs decide they are finished. Pick your
train before you stop pedalling.

Then check the [live times](/blog/sydney-stop-departure-board/) on the platform. A timetable is the
plan. The countdown is the train.
