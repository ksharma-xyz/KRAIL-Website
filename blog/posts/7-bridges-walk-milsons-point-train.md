The 7 Bridges Walk is 28km around the harbour on Sunday 25 October. The start is Bradfield Park at
Milsons Point, a short walk from the station. You do not need a car for it. You do not have to
finish it either.

The fear that puts people in a car is the back half. Your legs are done somewhere past the
Gladesville Bridge, and the car is parked back at the start.

Two things fix that. Free shuttle buses loop the whole course in both directions until 4.30pm. Every
village sits near a station, a wharf or a bus stop.

There is one catch, and it has nothing to do with your legs. **The trains are not running normally
that Sunday.**

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Sunday 25 October**, villages open from **7am**, finish 4.30pm
Start: **Bradfield Park** at Milsons Point, a short walk from the station
Latest start: **11am** if you want the full 28km
Shuttle: free, both directions, 8.30am until **4.30pm**
Ferry back: the **F4** between Pyrmont Bay and Milsons Point
:::

The walk goes clockwise, on footpaths and shared paths, and it raises money for Cancer Council NSW.
Your event guide gets stamped at each bridge and each village.

## How do you get to the start?

Get yourself to Milsons Point station. The village is at Bradfield Park, on the corner of Fitzroy
Street and Alfred Street South.

The trains need a little planning this Sunday. None run on the city loop, and none run between
Central and Wynyard. The city is not the straight line it usually is.

**Coming from the city.** Take the light rail to the Wynyard stop. Then take the T1 one stop north.
The 6.26am from Central Chalmers Street is at Wynyard at 6.37am. The 6.43am from Wynyard platform 3
is at Milsons Point at 6.46am. Northbound trains leave Wynyard every ten minutes.

**Coming from the north.** Go early and it is one train the whole way. The 6.10am from Hornsby
platform 1 is at Milsons Point at 6.52am. From 7.30am to 8pm buses replace trains between Hornsby
and Gordon. The train takes over again at Gordon and runs through Milsons Point to Wynyard.

You can [check the live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you leave the house.

## What if you cannot finish?

Read this bit before Sunday rather than on the day.

Free shuttle buses run a loop of the whole course in both directions from 8.30am. Anticlockwise they
go every 30 minutes all day. Clockwise it is every 30 minutes in the morning, then every 10 minutes
from midday. Show your event ticket or your event guide when you board.

There are nine shuttle stops. The one at Milsons Point is on Alfred Street South, outside the
station.

Read the stop number, then read the letter after it. **Clockwise stops carry a C and anticlockwise
stops carry an A.** Some C and A stops are not across the road from each other. The right number
with the wrong letter sends you around the loop the other way.

## Can you get back by ferry?

From Pyrmont, yes, and it is the best shortcut on the course. The F4 runs between Pyrmont Bay Wharf
and Milsons Point Wharf. Both wharves are a short walk from their event villages.

The 12.11pm from Pyrmont Bay Wharf is at Milsons Point Wharf at 12.29pm. Eighteen minutes across the
water. Through the middle of the day they go every fifteen minutes.

The Pyrmont village is at Pyrmont Bay Park on Pirrama Road, opposite The Star. The L1 light rail
stops there too.

## Which village should you start from?

Any of the six. Milsons Point opens at 7am and the other five open at 7.30am. If you want the full
circuit, 11am is the latest start recommended.

- **Milsons Point**, Bradfield Park. Walk from Milsons Point station.
- **Wollstonecraft**, Brennan Park on Hazelbank Street. Walk from Waverton station.
- **Pyrmont**, Pyrmont Bay Park. Ferry to Pyrmont Bay, or the L1 to The Star.
- **Rozelle**, Callan Park at Lilyfield. Bus.
- **Hunters Hill**, the Scouts Hall on Durham Street. Bus.
- **Lane Cove**, Central Park on Kenneth Street at Longueville. Bus.

Wollstonecraft is the one worth knowing about. Waverton is two stops up from Milsons Point, four
minutes on the T1. The 1.07pm from Waverton platform 1 is at Milsons Point at 1.11pm.

## What is the last service home?

The walk finishes at 4.30pm, so this is reassurance rather than a deadline.

The free shuttle stops first. The last one leaves Milsons Point at 4.30pm.

At Milsons Point, platform 1 is the city side and platform 2 is the north side. The last train from
platform 1 goes at 12.12am and ends at Wynyard. Change there for the light rail or a bus. Heading
north, the last train leaves platform 2 at 1.09am.

The last F4 of the night leaves Pyrmont Bay Wharf at 11.11pm.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets**, charging points and the course decision points are listed on the event website.
- **Dogs** are welcome on the course but not on the shuttle buses, so the dog walks the whole way with you. Assistance animals are the exception.
- **Paying for the trip**: a participant travel pass covers travel on transport and Park & Ride parking.
- **Accessible travel**: accessible trips show a wheelchair icon in the trip planner, and you can filter to show only those.
- **Driving to a station** still works. Park & Ride gives up to 18 hours free when you complete a public transport trip. You can [check the spaces before you leave](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/).
- **Road closures**: the roads inside Callan Park shut from 5am to 4pm, and Durham Street at Hunters Hill shuts from 6am to 5.30pm.
- **Taxi ranks** sit around the event villages, and rideshare pickups are affected by those closures.
- **Bring the event guide** somewhere dry. It is the thing that gets stamped, and it also gets you on the bus.

Seven bridges, six villages, one Sunday. Walk as much of it as you feel like, and let the bus or the
ferry bring you back.
