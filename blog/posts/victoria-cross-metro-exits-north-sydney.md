Victoria Cross has two ends, and they come up on different blocks of Miller St. The north end is
at McLaren St. The south end is at Berry St, and it has two exits of its own.

Nobody gets lost down there. They come up at the wrong end and walk the block back.

::: facts Victoria Cross, in short
Exits: **three**, Miller St North, Miller St South and Denison St
North end: corner of **Miller and McLaren Streets**, lift only
South end: **Miller St South** and Denison St, at Berry St
To the city: **platform 1**. North to Chatswood: platform 2
Buses: Stands **A and B** at the north end, C to F at the south
Trains: **North Sydney station**, south on Blue St
:::

## Which exit at Victoria Cross?

Three exits, two ends.

- **Miller St North**: the corner of Miller and McLaren Streets. Lift only.
- **Miller St South**: the south concourse, at Berry St.
- **Denison St**: the same concourse, out the other side.

One long mezzanine runs above the platforms. Escalators drop to the platforms at three points
along it. Miller St North is at one end. The escalator down to Miller St South and Denison St is
at the other.

So go up to the mezzanine first. Then walk to the end you want.

## Which exit for what?

- **North Sydney Oval and St Leonards Park**: Miller St North, then head north.
- **Australian Catholic University**: Miller St South.
- **Greenwood Plaza**: Miller St South or Denison St, then head south.
- **The train**: those same two exits, then keep going south.

The offices run along Miller St between the two ends. Either end works for those.

## Which platform, and how long to the city?

Platform 1 goes to the city. Platform 2 goes north.

- **Platform 1**: Barangaroo, Martin Place, Gadigal and Central.
- **Platform 2**: Crows Nest, [Chatswood](/blog/chatswood-station-metro-platform/), Macquarie Park
  and Tallawong.

From platform 1, Barangaroo is three minutes away. Martin Place is six. Central is nine.

From platform 2, Crows Nest is two minutes and Chatswood is six.

You do not need a timetable here. Services leave every 4 to 10 minutes through the day.

## Where do the buses go from?

Stands A and B are at the north end, on Miller St at McLaren St. Stands C to F are at the south
end. Which way you are going picks the stand.

- **Into the city, or to Milsons Point**: Stand A at the north end. Stand E or F at the south.
- **Out to Manly or Dee Why**: Stand B at the north end, Stand C at the south.
- **Out to Northbridge or Lindfield**: Stand B at the north end, Stand D at the south.
- **To Mosman Bay Wharf**: Stand B or Stand C.

## Where is the train?

The train does not stop at Victoria Cross. North Sydney station is a different station, south of
here on Blue St.

Come out at Miller St South or at Denison St. Then walk south down Miller St. Greenwood Plaza is
on the way.

## Getting home late

The metro runs past midnight most nights. Sunday is the one to watch.

- **Monday to Thursday**: 12.33am to the city, 12.39am north to Tallawong.
- **Friday**: 1.33am and 1.39am.
- **Saturday**: 1.31am and 1.39am.
- **Sunday**: 11.01pm and 11.09pm.

On a Sunday the metro stops first. Miss the 11.01pm and you still have the train.
Walk south to North Sydney station. Trains towards the city go past midnight there on a Sunday
night.

First one back in the morning is 4.51am from platform 1, every day of the week. Trackwork moves
these times, so check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Staff** are on from 10 minutes before the first service to 10 minutes after the last.
- **Toilets** are on the concourse, towards Miller St North and at Miller St South. They are open
  while the station is staffed.
- **Lifts**: Miller St North is lift only. For an escalator, follow the signs to Miller St South.
- **Taxi rank and kiss and ride** are both on McLaren St, at the north end.
- **Bikes** go in the bike sheds at the station, which you can book online.
- **Paying**: tap on and tap off with your phone, or a credit or debit card.

Two ends, one station. Pick your end before you go up, and you save yourself the block.
