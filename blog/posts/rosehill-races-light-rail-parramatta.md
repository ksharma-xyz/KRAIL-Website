Spring racing at Rosehill, and Ladies Day is a full afternoon of it. Gates at 11.15am,
first race at 12.20pm, and the last one at 6.10pm.

Then comes the part that puts people in a car, which is the thought of getting home.
Not here. There is a light rail stop named after the racecourse. That part is on us.

::: facts The day, in short
When: **Saturday 7 November 2026**, gates **11.15am**, last race **6.10pm**
Getting there: the **L4** tram to **Rosehill Gardens**
From Parramatta Square: **6 minutes**, about every 10 minutes
Coming home: last tram towards Westmead at **1.12am**
Travel: included with your race day ticket, until **4am**
:::

## How do you get to Rosehill Gardens without a car?

1. Take a train to Parramatta. The 10.11am from Central is at Parramatta at 10.43am.
2. Walk out to the light rail stop at Parramatta Square, on Macquarie Street.
3. Take an L4 tram towards Carlingford. The 11.03am is at Rosehill Gardens at 11.09am.
4. Get off at Rosehill Gardens. It is the third stop.
5. Walk to the gates along Grand Avenue and through the underpass.

Six minutes on the tram. Gates open at 11.15am, so that one puts you there right on open.

Trams come about every 10 minutes, and extra ones run before and after the racing.
Coming from Westmead instead? The light rail stop is right at the station there.

## Which tram, and which way?

Rosehill Gardens is on the stretch between Parramatta and Carlingford, so direction matters.

Going to the track from Parramatta Square, take the tram signed **Carlingford**.
It calls at Robin Thomas, then Tramway Avenue, then Rosehill Gardens.

Coming back, take the tram signed **Westmead**. That is the one that runs through Parramatta.
The one signed Carlingford goes the other way, out to Dundas and Telopea.

## When does the last tram go?

This is the question that decides whether you drive, so here it is plainly.

After the last race, the 6.23pm from Rosehill Gardens is at Parramatta Square at 6.29pm.

If you stay on much later, you are still fine.

- **Last tram towards Westmead**: 1.12am, at Parramatta Square at 1.18am.
- **Last tram towards Carlingford**: 1.20am.
- **Then nothing** until 4.53am on the Sunday morning.

Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before you walk up to the
stop. A timetable is the plan. The countdown is what is actually happening.

## Is there a bus instead?

Yes. Route 920 goes from Parramatta Station, Stand A2.

It runs to James Ruse Drive, opposite Prospect Street. Buses go about every 20 minutes on
weekends. If you are not sure which stand is yours, you can
[find it by the route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/).

Coming from the north west, take the M1 and change at Epping. From there you can pick up a bus
to Rosehill, or a train to Parramatta and the tram.

## Can you get there by ferry?

Parramatta Wharf is the closest one. Walk from the wharf along the river and up Argus Lane to
the Robin Thomas light rail stop.

Two things to plan around. The F3 fills up on a busy day, so leave yourself longer than the
timetable says. At low tide, ferries between Parramatta and Rydalmere can be replaced by buses.

## Should you drive?

Grand Avenue is the one to know about. A special event clearway runs there from 7am to 8pm, from
100m before Gate 1 to opposite Grand Avenue North. Cars left in it are towed.

Expect heavier traffic on James Ruse Drive and Hassall Street.

If you do take the car, leave it at a [Park & Ride](/blog/sydney-park-and-ride-live-spaces/)
instead. Parking there is free for up to 18 hours a day when you travel on public transport.
Use the intercom at the exit and show your event ticket.

## The practical stuff

- **Gates** open at 11.15am. First race 12.20pm. Last race 6.10pm.
- **Your ticket** covers your travel on every mode. Show it to staff when you board.
- **Keep it** for the trip home. It works until 4am the next day.
- **Airport** station access fees are not included.
- **Trackwork** on 7 November changes several train routes. Check your trip before you leave.
- **Crossing** the tracks: trams cannot stop quickly, so use the marked crossings.
- **Taxis** pick up on Oak Street, over the Equine Bridge.
- **Accessible trips** show when you turn the accessible filter on.

Sort out the tram home before the first race. Then the rest of the day is just the racing.
