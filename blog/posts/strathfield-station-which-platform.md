Strathfield has eight platforms. Three of them are the ones that take you to the city, and three take you the other way. Which you want depends on your line, not on which way you are facing.

::: facts Strathfield, in short
To the city: platform **4**, platform **5** or platform **7**
The other way: platform **3**, platform **6** or platform **8**
Islands: **four** of them, each with its own lift
Buses: **Everton Rd** on one side, **Albert Rd** on the other
Night buses: **stand G**, on Albert Rd
:::

## Which platform goes to the city?

Three do, and your line picks which one.

- **Platform 4**: the T1, in from Emu Plains and Richmond.
- **Platform 5**: the T9, down from Hornsby.
- **Platform 7**: the T2 from Parramatta and Leppington. The T3 from Lidcombe and Liverpool.

So a T1 and a T2 both take you to the city from here. They do it from platforms that are not next to each other.

::: callout Do this first
Check the line code on the indicator before you pick a staircase. At Strathfield the direction is not the thing that decides your platform.
:::

## Which platform goes the other way?

- **Platform 6**: the T1 out to Emu Plains and Richmond. The T9 back up to Hornsby.
- **Platform 8**: the T2 to Parramatta and Leppington. The T3 to Lidcombe and Liverpool.
- **Platform 3**: the intercity trains out of the city. Up to Gosford and Newcastle, or over the mountains to Katoomba and Bathurst.

Some T9 and T1 services use platform 3 too. Platforms 1 and 2 pick up a handful of extras.

## How are the platforms arranged?

Eight platforms sit on four islands. The pairs are 1 with 2 and 3 with 4. Then comes 5 with 6 and 7 with 8.

Each island has its own lift.

On three of those islands one side goes to the city and the other goes away. Platform 5 is city bound and platform 6 is not. The same holds for 3 and 4, and for 7 and 8.

So if you are standing on the wrong side, you often only have to turn around.

## Where is your bus stand?

The stands are split across two sides of the station. The letters run straight through both.

- **Everton Rd** on the north east side. Stands A to D.
- **Albert Rd** on the other side. Stands E to H, plus L and M.

The 458 leaves from two stands on Everton Rd. Stand A for Ryde, stand D for Burwood.

On the Albert Rd side, stand F is the 450 to Hurstville. Stand E is the 913 to Bankstown and the 914 to Greenacre. Stand H is the 407 and the 408 to Burwood.

Stand M is arrivals only. Nothing leaves from it, so do not wait there.

If you know the number but not the stand, you can [find it by route number](/blog/find-sydney-bus-stop-by-number/) before you come down.

## Getting home late

The trains stop a little after midnight. Then the night buses take over, and all of them use stand G on Albert Rd.

On Tuesday 10 November the last train to the city is the 12.17am from platform 7. Platform 4 finishes at 12.11am and platform 5 at 12.04am.

Platform 3 runs longer. Intercity trains leave at 1.03am, at 2.00am and at 4.03am.

Towards the city the night buses are the N50 at 12.28am, the N60 at 12.47am and the N80 at 1.16am. Each one repeats hourly on the same minutes.

Going the other way it is the N50 to Liverpool at 12.38am, the N60 to Fairfield at 1.10am and the N80 to Hornsby at 1.20am.

Both directions leave from the same stand. Read the front of the bus before you get on.

Two more stop here on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights only. They are the N61 to Carlingford and the N81 to Parramatta.

The first train of the morning is the 4.10am from platform 4.

Trackwork changes these times on some nights. Check the [live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late trip home.

## The practical stuff

- **Entries** on Everton Rd, on Albert Rd and beside Strathfield Square.
- **Lifts** to all four platform islands.
- **Tickets** from machines near the Everton Rd entry and near the Albert Rd entry.
- **Taxis** rank on Everton Rd and on Albert Rd.
- **Stand C** carries school services only.
- **Intercity** trains stop here, so you can reach Gosford or Katoomba without going into the city first.

Eight platforms sounds like a lot until you know the three that matter to you. Find your line on the indicator, and the stairs sort themselves out.
