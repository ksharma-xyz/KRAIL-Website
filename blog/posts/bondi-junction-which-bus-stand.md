At Bondi Junction the bus stands run from A to T, and the letter is the part that
matters. Bondi Beach goes from Stand A. Bronte from Stand K. Tamarama from Stand B.
Coogee from Stand F.

What catches people is the route number. The 379 leaves from two stands here, one minute
apart, and they go opposite ways.

::: facts Which stand, in short
Bondi Beach: the **333** from **Stand A**
Bronte Beach: the **379** from **Stand K**
Tamarama Beach: the **381** from **Stand B**
Coogee Beach: the **313** from **Stand F**
Back to the city: the **333** from **Stand R**, up on Grafton Street
Trains: platforms **1 and 2**, both towards the city
:::

## Which stand for which beach?

**Bondi Beach.** Take the 333 from Stand A. The 1pm on a weekday is at Campbell Parade at
1.14pm, and at that hour one goes every six minutes. The 380 from Stand B gets there too.
Its 1.10pm is at the beach by 1.22pm, then it carries on to Watsons Bay.

**Bronte Beach.** Take the 379 from Stand K. The 1.01pm is at the Bronte Beach terminus at
1.18pm.

**Tamarama Beach.** Take the 381 from Stand B. The 1pm is at Tamarama Beach at 1.11pm. It
is a loop back to Bondi Junction, so the way home is the same bus.

**Coogee Beach.** Take the 313 from Stand F. The 1.03pm is at Arden Street by Coogee Bay
Road at 1.21pm.

Bring your swimmers. If you would rather do it the long way, the
[walk from Bondi to Coogee](/blog/bondi-to-coogee-without-a-car/) links all four of those
beaches.

::: callout Read the letter, not the number
The 379 goes to Bronte from Stand K and to North Bondi from Stand D. They leave one
minute apart.
:::

## Why is one route at two stands?

The number tells you the road. The letter tells you the direction.

The 333 does the same thing. Stand A sends it out to North Bondi. Stand R sends it into
the city, and Stand R is not even in the interchange.

So check both before you sit down. If you only check the number, you have a one in two
chance of going the wrong way.

## Six stands that will never pick you up

Six of the eighteen are arrivals only. Buses pull in, everyone gets off, and nothing
departs. The six are E, G, N, P, Q and S.

Stand M is the other one to leave alone. It runs school services and nothing else.

That leaves eleven stands you can actually board from.

Here is where the rest of them go.

- **Stand C**: the 386 to Vaucluse and the 387 to South Head Cemetery.
- **Stand D**: the 379 to North Bondi.
- **Stand F**: the 313 to Coogee and the 350 to the airport.
- **Stand H**: the 326 and the 327 to Edgecliff, and the 328 round by Darling Point.
- **Stand J**: the 356 to Eastgardens and the 360 to Clovelly.
- **Stand L**: the 390X to La Perouse.
- **Stand R**: the 333 to Circular Quay, the 352 and 355 to Marrickville, the 389 to Pyrmont.
- **Stand T**: the 440 to Rozelle.

## Which exit do you want?

Stands A to P are through the Oxford Street exits. There is one at each end of the
concourse, each with stairs and an escalator. A lift runs from the concourse down to the
buses and Oxford Street.

Stands Q to T are not in the interchange at all. They sit up on Grafton Street, out the
Grafton Street exits. That is where the 333 into the city goes from, and the 440 to
Rozelle.

Coming the other way, the interchange has its own lift back up to the trains. There are
stairs and an escalator up to Oxford Street as well.

## Can you get a train going the wrong way?

Not here. Platforms 1 and 2 both send you towards the city, so take whichever one is
boarding.

The 11.05am from platform 2 on a weekday is at Martin Place at 11.13am. Town Hall is
11.15am. Central is 11.18am.

## Getting home late

- **Monday to Thursday nights**: the last train leaves at 12.55am.
- **Friday nights**: 1.35am.
- **Saturday nights**: 1.38am.
- **Sunday nights**: 12.48am, the earliest of the week.

The last 379 out of Bronte goes at 1.07am and is back at Bondi Junction at 1.14am. On a
Friday or Saturday night there is still a train. On every other night there is not.

Trackwork moves all of this. Check the
[live times](/blog/live-sydney-train-bus-ferry-times/) before a late one.

## The practical stuff

- **Toilets** on the train concourse, with an accessible toilet, and more in the bus interchange.
- **Lifts** from the concourse down to the buses, and from the interchange back up to the trains.
- **Stop numbers** on every stand. Stand K is 202277.
- **The shortcut**: put a stop number after transportnsw.info/ and you land on that stand's departures.
- **Flying out?** The 1.06pm from Stand F is at the Domestic Terminal at 2.12pm. Change there for the 420 to the International Terminal.
- **Two kerbs** in the interchange. Stands A to G run along one side and H to P along the other.

Eighteen stands, and the only thing you need off the map is one letter.
